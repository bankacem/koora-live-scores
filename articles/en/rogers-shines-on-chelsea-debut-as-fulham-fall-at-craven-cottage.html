Rogers Shines on Chelsea Debut as Fulham Fall at Craven Cottage

8/24/2026 · By Koora Live

Chelsea claimed a noteworthy victory over Fulham at Craven Cottage, with debutant Morgan Rogers making an immediate impact. Xabi Alonso's men secured all three points, though Fulham had their moments. BBC Sport's player ratings reveal standout performances and areas where improvement is needed, including a tough outing for Chelsea’s goalkeeper, Robert Sanchez.

Morgan Rogers Impresses on Chelsea Debut

Morgan Rogers managed to mark his first appearance in Chelsea colours with a goal, instantly endearing himself to the Blues' faithful. Debuting under new manager Xabi Alonso, who took charge earlier in the campaign, Rogers showcased his potential while adapting to the fast-paced action of the Premier League. Such a strong debut could be highly encouraging for Chelsea, who have been on a steady rebuilding path under Alonso's guidance.

Contrasting Fortunes for Chelsea Stars

While Rogers earned the plaudits, Chelsea goalkeeper Robert Sanchez had a less fortunate day, struggling to match the high standards expected at the elite level. Defenses often begin their stability with their shot-stopper, making Sanchez's form a point to watch for Alonso and his coaching staff. Meanwhile, Fulham fought hard at home, continuing to establish Craven Cottage as a tricky venue for visiting teams. Marco Silva's men have shown real promise since their promotion in 2022, though this defeat will serve as an important reminder of the challenges posed by the Premier League’s top sides.

Analysis

Morgan Rogers' debut goal is a testament to Chelsea’s strategic recruitment and youth development. Adding a young talent like Rogers to the mix could signal a commitment to crafting a squad that blends vibrant up-and-coming players with established stars. However, Robert Sanchez's struggles highlight a potential weak link in Chelsea's setup that could dampen long-term ambitions if not addressed. Elsewhere, Fulham's resilience at home was evident despite the defeat, and they remain a capable side under Silva—though matching the league's elite on a consistent basis remains a challenge.

Chelsea's victory over Fulham at Craven Cottage showcased a promising debut for Morgan Rogers and signaled areas for improvement within their squad. As Xabi Alonso continues to shape his team, addressing defensive frailties and inconsistent performances will be key. Meanwhile, Fulham can look to regroup and maintain their strong spirit in the encounters ahead.

Rangers reject bid for Youssef Chermiti as striker is reported injured

8/9/2026 · By Koora Live

BBC Sport reports that Rangers have turned down an offer for striker Youssef Chermiti, with manager Derek McInnes saying the bid did not match the club's valuation. The same coverage also notes that Chermiti has been reported injured following the rejected offer.

What BBC Sport has reported

According to BBC Sport, Rangers received an offer for striker Youssef Chermiti which the Scottish Premiership club declined. Manager Derek McInnes is reported to have confirmed that the proposal fell short of the valuation the club had placed on the player. The outlet also linked the rejected approach with a subsequent injury to Chermiti, noting that the striker was reported as injured after the bid was turned down.

These are the core, verifiable points from the BBC Sport report: an offer was made for Chermiti, Rangers rejected it on valuation grounds, Derek McInnes confirmed the valuation issue, and Chermiti was reported injured after the rejection. The report does not specify financial details of the bid, the nature or severity of the injury, or other transfer-related particulars.

How this fits into wider transfer and squad management patterns

It is common in modern football for clubs to value players according to their strategic importance, contract status and market demand. When a bid is described as not meeting a club's valuation, it typically means the selling club believes the offer undervalued the player's worth in one or more of those areas. Managers and sporting directors often publicly state such positions to manage expectations and signal that the club is not under pressure to sell.

Injuries are an important variable in transfer activity. A reported injury after interest in a player emerges can complicate negotiations for both buying and selling clubs: buyers may seek medical reassessments or reconsider the timing of an approach, while sellers must weigh the player's recovery timeline against squad needs. All of these are general dynamics; the BBC Sport piece provides the specific news items but does not detail any medical assessments or next steps taken by Rangers or potential suitors.

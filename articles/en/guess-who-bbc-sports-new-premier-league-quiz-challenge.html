Guess Who? BBC Sport’s New Premier League Quiz Challenge

8/2/2026

BBC Sport has launched a fresh interactive feature that keeps fans on their toes, presenting a new question in the “Who am I?” series: “Guess Premier League star No 13.” The premise is simple yet engaging – zeroing in on the mystery player in as few attempts as possible.

While the article itself refrains from disclosing identifiers or statistics about the star, it encourages readers to tap into their knowledge of club, position and past moments to piece together clues. The format tests a fan’s breadth of awareness across the league, turning a routine news feed into a quick brain‑teaser that can be tackled during a lunch break or a pre‑match chat.

Readers can reason through the challenge by drawing on all the surrounding context they can gather from the club history, current form, and the unique feature of wearing the number 13 shirt across the Premier League. The piece invites repeat engagement: each miss invites a new questioning, making the quiz both educational and entertaining.

For anyone eager to flex football trivia muscles, BBC Sport’s “Guess Premier League star No 13” is a fresh twist on traditional quizzes. It offers a fun, quick‑hit fence‑post exercise that blends test‑like pressure with the thrill of recognition all in one click.

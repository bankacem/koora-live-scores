Brentford Break Club Record to Sign Mamadou Sangare

8/1/2026

Brentford have announced the signing of Mali international Mamadou Sangare from French side Lens, with the deal marking a new club record fee for the Premier League outfit.

The acquisition of the midfielder underscores Brentford's ambitions as they continue to strengthen their squad. Sangare's performances in France and on the international stage with Mali have reportedly impressed the club, leading them to invest significantly in the 24-year-old.

While the exact fee remains undisclosed, this transfer represents a major statement of intent from Brentford, who are eager to build on their recent successes in England’s top tier. Sangare is expected to provide both physicality and technical skill in the heart of their midfield.

The Bees' move for Sangare reflects their commitment to identifying and securing top talent from across Europe. Brentford fans will be eager to see how the club's latest record signing integrates into Thomas Frank’s system in the coming season.

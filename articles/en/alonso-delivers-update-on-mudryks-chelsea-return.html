Alonso Delivers Update on Mudryk's Chelsea Return

8/1/2026

Xabi Alonso expressed his enthusiasm at the prospect of seeing Mykhailo Mudryk reintegrated into Chelsea's squad but urged patience as the Ukrainian winger works his way back. Speaking on the player's return, Alonso said he is "very happy" to welcome Mudryk back but emphasized that it remains "too early" to assess the full extent of his contributions moving forward.

Mudryk's return represents a potential boost for Chelsea, whose season has seen its share of challenges as they aim to rebuild under new leadership. Alonso's cautious optimism suggests the club is taking a measured approach to reintroducing the dynamic winger into competitive action.

The 22-year-old, who joined Chelsea in a high-profile move, has shown flashes of brilliance but has faced setbacks in maintaining consistency. Both Alonso and the club will likely be keen to manage expectations as Mudryk looks to regain his rhythm and solidify his role in the team's plans for the future.

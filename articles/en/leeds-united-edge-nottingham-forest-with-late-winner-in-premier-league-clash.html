Leeds United Edge Nottingham Forest with Late Winner in Premier League Clash

8/22/2026 · By Koora Live

Leeds United secured a dramatic 1-0 victory over Nottingham Forest in the Premier League, thanks to an 88th-minute goal from Anton Stach. The match marked the debut of Oliver Glasner as Forest’s new manager but ended in disappointment for the home fans at the City Ground.

Anton Stach Seals the Points for Leeds

The game looked destined to end goalless until Leeds United midfielder Anton Stach stepped up to deliver a decisive moment in the dying minutes. His 88th-minute strike broke the deadlock, silencing the Nottingham Forest fans and ensuring a narrow but valuable win for the visitors. Stach’s late heroics showcased the quality Leeds can summon in critical moments, providing the perfect ending to a tightly contested Premier League fixture.

Oliver Glasner's Nottingham Forest Tenure Begins with a Loss

Nottingham Forest supporters had high hopes as new manager Oliver Glasner took charge for the first time. Glasner, who brought a reputation for success with previous teams, faced a tough test against an organized Leeds United side. Despite glimpses of promise, Forest’s efforts fell short, and a lack of clinical finishing ultimately cost them a positive result. The match will undoubtedly leave Glasner with much to ponder as he sets about implementing his philosophy and turning around Forest’s fortunes in the Premier League.

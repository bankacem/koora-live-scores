Enes Unal Transfers from Bournemouth to Getafe

8/13/2026 · By Koora Live

Turkish striker Enes Unal has completed a move from Bournemouth to Spanish side Getafe, marking his return to La Liga. This transfer represents a significant shift in the player's career as he rejoins a club where he has previously played and found success.

Enes Unal's Journey Back to Getafe

Enes Unal has made the decision to leave English club Bournemouth and sign with Getafe, the La Liga outfit where he has played in the past. While the details of the transfer fee or contract duration have not been disclosed, this move signals the 26-year-old forward's return to Spanish football after his stint in England. Getafe, based in Madrid, has a reputation for being a competitive mid-table side in La Liga, often prioritizing tactical discipline and defensive solidity.

A Brief History of Enes Unal's Career

Enes Unal is regarded as one of Turkey's most talented footballers, having risen through the ranks at Bursaspor before securing a high-profile transfer to Manchester City in 2015. However, as is common with many young players signed by City, he was loaned out to gain experience, including stints in Belgium, the Netherlands, and Spain. His previous spell at Getafe saw him developing into a reliable goalscorer in La Liga, a quality that will no doubt excite fans of the Azulones. At Bournemouth, Unal faced tougher competition for starting opportunities, which likely influenced his decision to return to a league where he has thrived.

Analysis

The move from Bournemouth to Getafe illustrates an interesting aspect of modern football: adaptability and career management. For Unal, returning to Getafe could signify a desire for stability and familiarity, especially in a league where he has demonstrated his capabilities effectively in the past. Analysts might view this as a strategic move to rekindle his form and return to being a prominent player. On the other hand, Bournemouth, who recently gained promotion back to the Premier League for the current season, might not have been able to guarantee consistent playing time for the Turkish forward, prompting his exit.

Enes Unal's transfer back to Getafe represents a return to familiar surroundings for the talented striker. The move is likely to benefit both player and club, as Unal seeks to reestablish himself as a key performer in La Liga while Getafe bolsters their attacking options for the upcoming season.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which clubs has Enes Unal played for?

Enes Unal began his career at Bursaspor before moving to Manchester City in 2015. Over the years, he has played for clubs such as Genk, FC Twente, Villarreal, Real Valladolid, Getafe, and Bournemouth.

What league does Getafe compete in?

Getafe competes in La Liga, the top flight of Spanish football.

Why might Enes Unal have left Bournemouth?

While the specifics are unknown, it is likely that competition for playing time at Bournemouth and his prior success in La Liga with Getafe influenced his decision to return to Spain.

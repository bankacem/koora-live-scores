Liverpool Eye Real Madrid's Endrick Amid Chelsea and Arsenal Transfer Ambitions

8/21/2026 · By Koora Live

Late last week’s transfer gossip, highlighted by BBC Sport, set the tone for a speculative summer. In a swirl of rumours, Liverpool’s hierarchy is reported to have reached out to Real Madrid regarding the Brazilian teen Endrick. Meanwhile, Chelsea are reportedly preparing a bid for Brazilian forward Nicolas Jackson, and Arsenal have signalled a desire for Nigerian striker Victor Osimhen. While the specifics of these moves remain unconfirmed, the chatter underscores the high‑profile nature of the clubs involved and the leagues they target.

Liverpool’s In‑Depth Inquiry Into Endrick

Liverpool’s short‑listing of Endrick follows the Premier League club’s tradition of identifying breakthrough talent from simply the same experience as they value. The 19‑year‑old, who began his senior career with Real Madrid’s youth academy, has already shown his potential through his brief first‑team appearances during the 2023‑24 pre‑season. A move to Anfield would, if confirmed, create a high-profile cross‑lingual partnership that London clubs often look for.

The reported enquiry signals Liverpool’s willingness to travel beyond their domestic transfer market. The club’s recent history of loaning players to gain experience before an outright transfer suggests a strategic long‑term plan. One can interpret the interest as a sign that Liverpool is still keen to broaden their attacking options amid the stature of their existing forwards.

The conversation with Real Madrid would undoubtedly cover a variety of topics: financial terms, sporting expectations, and the player’s fit with the club’s tactical philosophy. Though no concrete offers have surfaced, this dialogue is a classic benchmark of a club building a future pipeline of talent.

Chelsea’s Possible Offer for Nicolas Jackson and Arsenal’s Pursuit of Victor Osimhen

Meanwhile, Chelsea appear to be prepping a bid for the young Brazilian talent Nicolas Jackson, a promising forward whose brief performances for São Paulo’s senior squad have drawn attention. The academy system at Chelsea is renowned for nurturing fast‑scoring, flexible forwards, which might explain their interest in a player with Jackson’s profile.

Arsenal, on the other side of the Premier League, have reportedly approached Napoli about Victor Osimhen, a prolific striker who powers the Italian side with excellent finishing and aerial prowess. Their request signals an ambition to add a dedicated goal‑scoring threat within the first team roster.

The trio of clubs illustrates a broader trend of English sides looking abroad for high‑potential players to complement their squads. Each club’s tactical setup would benefit from these potential acquisitions, should negotiations be successful.

Crystal Palace complete transfer for US forward Zavier Gozo from Real Salt Lake – reported $15m move

8/18/2026 · By Koora Live

Crystal Palace have bolstered their attacking options by signing United States prospect Zavier Gozo from Major League Soccer side Real Salt Lake, according to BBC Sport. The move was reported for a fee of $15m (£11.08m), with Palace securing the forward from the MLS club.

What the signing adds to Crystal Palace

Bringing in Zavier Gozo gives Crystal Palace another forward option as they look to strengthen their squad. Palace are an established Premier League club with a recent history of blending experienced professionals with younger talent, and the acquisition of a United States prospect fits that pattern. Adding a forward from MLS underlines the club’s willingness to scout talent from a growing North American market.

While BBC Sport reported the transfer fee as $15m (£11.08m), how Palace integrate Gozo into their attacking rotation will depend on the manager’s plans and the existing recruitment strategy. Premier League clubs typically aim to develop new signings while balancing immediate performance needs, and a forward arriving from MLS can bring different attributes and a fresh profile to the squad.

Background on Zavier Gozo and Real Salt Lake

Zavier Gozo is described in reports as a United States prospect and plays as a forward. He was with Real Salt Lake, one of the teams competing in Major League Soccer — the top tier of men’s professional football in the United States and Canada. MLS has become an increasingly prominent platform for young and emerging players to attract attention from European clubs.

Real Salt Lake have been part of MLS since the early years of the league and operate with a structure common to American clubs, including youth development and a pathway for players to move abroad. Transfers from MLS to European leagues have grown in frequency as clubs across Europe monitor the league for talent that can be adapted to different tactical environments.

Analysis

This acquisition illustrates a continuing trend of Premier League clubs recruiting from Major League Soccer, seeking promising players who can either contribute immediately or be developed further. Analysts might see Palace’s approach as a bid to diversify their attacking options and tap into the talent pipeline emerging from North America. It also signals confidence in the scouting networks that increasingly connect MLS with top European competitions.

At the same time, integrating a player from MLS into the rhythm and intensity of the Premier League presents typical challenges: adaptation to a new tactical system, physical demands, and the cultural transition of moving continents. Framing this move as part of a longer-term plan rather than an instant fix could be the most realistic interpretation of what Palace have secured with this reported deal.

The move for Zavier Gozo, reported by BBC Sport as a $15m (£11.08m) deal from Real Salt Lake to Crystal Palace, continues the flow of talent from MLS to Europe. How he settles in at Palace and what impact he will have will unfold as he adapts to his new environment and the demands of the Premier League.

Everton Target Free Agent Jamie Vardy in Transfer Pursuit

9/4/2026 · By Koora Live

According to a report from BBC Sport, Everton are evaluating a move for free agent Jamie Vardy as part of their transfer plans. The club is reportedly weighing up their options, which could also include Manchester United's Anthony Martial, while Wilfried Zaha remains linked with AC Milan.

Everton and Jamie Vardy: A Possible New Chapter

Jamie Vardy's potential move to Everton has become a hot topic in the football transfer rumour mill. The former Leicester City striker, who has built a reputation as one of the Premier League’s most prolific forwards, is now a free agent and reportedly on Everton’s radar. At 36 years old, Vardy might bring invaluable experience and a winner's mentality to the Toffees, who have faced recent struggles in both attacking depth and league standings. With over 100 Premier League goals to his name and a storied role in helping Leicester achieve an unlikely Premier League title in the 2015-2016 season, Vardy remains a sought-after forward despite his advancing age.

Anthony Martial: Another Option for Everton

Another potential target for the Merseyside club is Manchester United’s Anthony Martial. The French forward, despite his obvious talent, has had a fluctuating career at Old Trafford, often grappling with injuries and form inconsistencies. Martial’s ability to play in various attacking roles could add versatility to Everton's front line. However, considering his history of fitness issues and sporadic performances, signing Martial may represent both a risk and an opportunity for the Toffees as they aim to bolster their squad.

Wilfried Zaha and His Potential Move to AC Milan

Away from Everton’s transfer dilemma, Crystal Palace winger Wilfried Zaha has reportedly attracted attention from AC Milan. Known for his explosive pace and dazzling footwork, Zaha has spent the majority of his career at Selhurst Park and has long been seen as one of the brightest talents outside of the Premier League's traditional top six. A move to Milan could mark an intriguing step in his career, giving the Ivorian winger a chance to showcase his abilities on the European stage.

Estevao Willian Considers a Move from Chelsea

Elsewhere in the transfer market, young Chelsea prospect Estevao Willian is reportedly weighing up his options regarding a potential move. At a time when Chelsea have been investing heavily in youth, the player's decision could reflect both the challenges of breaking into an established squad and the lure of opportunities elsewhere. While details remain sparse, any move involving this prospect could shape his early career trajectory and development.

Analysis

These transfer links underscore various interesting narratives in the summer window. For Everton, pursuing either Vardy or Martial highlights the urgent need to strengthen their attacking options as they seek to recover from a difficult patch in recent seasons. Vardy's proven Premier League pedigree could make him a short-term solution, whereas Martial's signing would signal investment in a younger, albeit more inconsistent, talent. Meanwhile, Wilfried Zaha’s rumoured departure from Crystal Palace could signal the Ivory Coast international's desire to test himself in a new environment after years of being the talisman for the Eagles. Finally, the mention of Estevao Willian possibly leaving Chelsea hints at the challenges facing young players in breaking into an increasingly star-studded Blues squad. It will be intriguing to see how these stories develop further.

This transfer update reflects the bustling activity across Europe as clubs aim to strengthen their squads ahead of the new season. The decisions made by players like Vardy, Martial, Zaha, and Estevao Willian could have significant implications for their respective careers and clubs. Fans everywhere will be watching closely as these stories unfold in the coming days.

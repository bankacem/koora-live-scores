Mason Mount to Miss Manchester United's Dublin Pre-Season Friendly Against Leeds

8/11/2026 · By Koora Live

Manchester United's preparations for the upcoming campaign have hit a minor snag as midfielder Mason Mount is likely to sit out their penultimate pre-season fixture against Leeds United at Croke Park, Dublin, according to BBC Sport. This news raises questions about United's lineup plans as the team gears up for the 2023-24 season.

Key Absence Ahead of Manchester United's Dublin Fixture

The news of Mason Mount's expected absence from Manchester United's pre-season friendly against Leeds in Dublin signals a significant development for the Red Devils. Croke Park is set to host the encounter, making this one of the marquee events of their summer schedule. Mount, who joined United earlier in the transfer window, has been a focal point of attention in the lead-up to the new season. His absence means manager Erik ten Hag will need to rejig his midfield plans ahead of this high-profile clash.

Mount’s Role in Manchester United’s Summer Rebuild

Mason Mount joined Manchester United earlier this summer after leaving Chelsea, a club where he rose through the ranks to become one of their most pivotal players. Known for his creativity, work rate, and versatility, Mount is expected to make a significant impact at Old Trafford. While pre-season fixtures are traditionally an opportunity for players to familiarize themselves with their teammates and tactics, his absence may delay this adjustment period. Leeds, on the other hand, are gearing up for their first season back in the Championship after last year’s relegation from the Premier League.

Analysis

Manchester United's midfield dynamics could face a temporary setback with Mason Mount’s absence from the match in Dublin. Analysts might view this as a chance for other midfielders, such as Bruno Fernandes and Christian Eriksen, to reinforce their synergy ahead of the competitive season. Mount's early signings were intended to instill creativity and energy to Manchester United’s play, but missing pre-season time could impact his immediate influence. On the Leeds side, their Championship preparations bring a different focus, aiming to test themselves against top-tier opposition despite being relegated last term.

The absence of Mason Mount from Manchester United’s pre-season game against Leeds adds a layer of intrigue to the team’s preparations for the upcoming campaign. As Erik ten Hag works to finalize his tactics, fans will eagerly watch how United adjust without one of their marquee summer signings.

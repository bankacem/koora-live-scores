In a fiercely contested opening fixture, reigning Premier League champions Manchester City dug deep to snatch a late win over Bournemouth. This match served as a baptism of fire for two new Premier League managers. On one side, Enzo Maresca’s first competitive outing as Manchester City manager ended on a high note, signaling a smooth transition from the Pep Guardiola era. On the other side, Marco Rose’s debut as Bournemouth boss saw him come agonizingly close to an initial triumph, only for his side to fall short at the death. For Bournemouth, the performance nonetheless offered glimpses of encouragement as they went toe-to-toe with one of Europe’s elite clubs, showcasing a potential to be competitive under their new tactician.

8/7/2026 · By Koora Live

Southampton head coach Tonda Eckert has spoken out about the recent Spygate scandal, expressing gratitude to club owner Dragon Solak for his support during this challenging period. Eckert firmly declared that the image portrayed by the controversy is far from the truth.

Eckert’s Stand Against Spygate Allegations

Tonda Eckert, who has been at the helm of Southampton’s first team, addressed the ongoing Spygate controversy, which has cast a shadow over the club. Speaking to the press, he acknowledged the turbulence the situation has caused but expressed confidence in the backing of Dragon Solak, Southampton’s owner. According to Eckert, the portrayal of the scandal does not accurately reflect reality, a statement that seeks to distance the club and himself from any perceived wrongdoing.

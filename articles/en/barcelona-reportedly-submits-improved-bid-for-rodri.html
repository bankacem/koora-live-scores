Barcelona Reportedly Submits Improved Bid for Rodri

8/8/2026 · By Koora Live

Barcelona has reportedly made a second attempt to secure the services of Rodri, according to BBC Sport. This new bid signals the club’s determination to bolster their squad as the transfer window remains active. Alongside this, Cristian Romero is said to want a move to Arsenal, while Nottingham Forest are poised to complete the signing of Ousmane Diomande.

Barcelona's Pursuit of Rodri

Barcelona, one of Europe’s most decorated football clubs, has reportedly upped their efforts to sign Rodri with an increased bid. The La Liga giants appear intent on strengthening their midfield, an area crucial to their tactical philosophy of quick transitions and dominant ball possession. Rodri, currently a key player for Manchester City and a linchpin in their midfield, is widely regarded as one of the finest defensive midfielders in football today. A move for Rodri demonstrates Barcelona's ambition to rebuild a squad capable of competing at the highest levels domestically and internationally.

Cristian Romero Linked with a Move to Arsenal

Meanwhile, Tottenham Hotspur’s Cristian Romero reportedly has eyes on a potential switch to Arsenal. Romero, highly regarded for his defensive prowess and aggressiveness in duels, has been a standout performer for Spurs in the Premier League. Arsenal, under the management of Mikel Arteta, have focused on building a squad capable of challenging for the title after years of rebuilding. A move for Romero could be part of Arsenal’s long-term strategy to shore up their backline and establish themselves as consistent competitors domestically and in European competitions.

Nottingham Forest Set to Sign Ousmane Diomande

In another development, BBC Sport reports that Nottingham Forest are on the verge of completing the signing of Ousmane Diomande. The club, aiming to solidify their place in the Premier League following promotion in the 2022-23 season, has been active in the transfer market. Diomande, a promising young talent, could provide much-needed depth and quality to their roster as they seek to avoid the pitfalls faced by newly promoted teams struggling with top-flight demands.

Analysis

Barcelona’s reported pursuit of Rodri comes as little surprise given their historical focus on technically proficient midfielders, a tradition exemplified by legends such as Xavi Hernandez and Andres Iniesta. Rodri’s ability to dictate tempo and protect the defensive line aligns well with Barca's footballing philosophy. However, prying him away from a Manchester City side that has grown dependent on his presence will be a significant challenge.

Cristian Romero’s rumored interest in a move to Arsenal also raises intriguing possibilities. Arsenal already boast a strong defensive core, but adding Romero could elevate their depth and quality in critical match-ups. Transfers between direct rivals in the Premier League are rare and complicated by inter-club dynamics, which makes this potential deal one to watch. Nottingham Forest’s imminent acquisition of Diomande also underscores their intent to consolidate their top-flight status after a long-awaited return to English football's elite tier.

The latest transfer rumors highlight intriguing possibilities, with Barcelona stepping up efforts for Rodri, Romero eyeing Arsenal, and Nottingham Forest continuing to bolster their squad with Diomande. As the transfer window progresses, football fans will be watching closely to see if these deals come to fruition.

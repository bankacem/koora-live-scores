Maresca suffers first defeat as Inter prevail on penalties in Hong Kong

8/2/2026

Enzo Maresca tasted defeat for the first time since taking charge of Manchester City as Inter Milan emerged victorious in a penalty shootout following a 1-1 friendly draw in Hong Kong. The match finished level after regular time, with the outcome decided from the spot in favour of the Italian side, BBC Sport reports.

The friendly in Hong Kong provided a competitive outing between two high-profile teams, ultimately requiring spot kicks to separate them after the sides shared a 1-1 scoreline during the match. Inter progressed from the shootout, sealing the result following the draw in normal play.

For Maresca, the result marks his first loss at the helm of Manchester City. While friendlies often serve as an opportunity to test players and tactics, this match will nevertheless stand on the record as the new head coach’s opening defeat.

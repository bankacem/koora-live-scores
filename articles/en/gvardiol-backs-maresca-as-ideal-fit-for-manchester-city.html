Gvardiol Backs Maresca as Ideal Fit for Manchester City

8/2/2026

Manchester City defender Josko Gvardiol has expressed his confidence in new manager Enzo Maresca, describing him as the "perfect one" to lead the team. Gvardiol's remarks underline his belief in the Italian coach's ability to guide City successfully as the club embarks on a new chapter.

Speaking about Maresca, Gvardiol highlighted his suitability for the reigning Premier League champions, signaling strong support for the managerial appointment. While giving no further details, the Croatian international's endorsement suggests optimism in the squad regarding the direction Maresca is expected to take.

Maresca's reputation for tactical acumen and his previous experience as part of top-flight coaching setups seem to have earned the trust of his players early on. City fans will be eager to see how the new boss implements his vision as he steps into the role at one of Europe's most demanding clubs.

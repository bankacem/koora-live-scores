Manchester City Secure Winger Allan Elias in Deal with Palmeiras

8/24/2026 · By Koora Live

Manchester City have reached an agreement with Palmeiras to sign promising winger Allan Elias. According to reports from BBC Sport, the deal could be worth up to £34.2 million, signaling City's ongoing commitment to securing top emerging talents for their squad.

Allan Elias Set for Move to Manchester City

Manchester City have bolstered their already formidable squad by agreeing to sign Brazilian winger Allan Elias from Palmeiras. The deal, valued at up to £34.2 million, reflects the Premier League champion’s strategy of investing in young, talented prospects from across the globe. Allan is highly regarded in Brazil for his pace, skill on the ball, and creative abilities in wide attacking positions.

Spotlight on Manchester City's Transfer Ambitions

Under Pep Guardiola’s management, Manchester City have become renowned for identifying and nurturing young talents who can shine in the first team or flourish during loan spells. This transfer follows a pattern that includes signings such as Gabriel Jesus, who also transitioned from Brazilian football to Europe at a young age. With City looking to sustain their domestic supremacy while chasing European glory, the inclusion of Allan Elias reinforces their commitment to securing future stars who fit their high-energy, possession-based style of play.

Lucas Digne completes move to PSG from Aston Villa — transfer news and what it means

8/9/2026 · By Koora Live

France international Lucas Digne has completed his move from Aston Villa to European champions Paris Saint-Germain, the BBC Sport has reported. The transfer reunites a high-profile left-sided defender with one of Europe’s leading clubs.

The basics of the transfer

According to BBC Sport, Lucas Digne has finalised a move away from Aston Villa to Paris Saint-Germain (PSG). Digne is a France international and the transfer takes him from a Premier League side to a club that are described as European champions. Both Aston Villa and PSG are well-known clubs in their respective domestic leagues, with Villa competing in England’s top tier and PSG a dominant force in France’s Ligue 1.

This switch represents a return to one of Europe’s biggest stages for the player. PSG, based in the French capital and traditionally playing home matches at the Parc des Princes, have regularly featured in the UEFA Champions League in recent seasons. Aston Villa, who play at Villa Park in Birmingham, are an established Premier League club with their own recent resurgence in English football.

Player profile and likely role

Lucas Digne is recognised as an experienced left-sided defender at club and international level. As a France international, he has been part of a national pool that has included some of the world’s top players. His attributes typically include defensive positioning, delivery from the left flank and the kind of experience managers look for when balancing a squad.

At PSG, Digne will join a side that blends domestic dominance with continental ambition. While the exact details of squad plans are set by the club, transfers of this nature are commonly intended to provide depth, competition and tactical options — particularly at full-back positions, which require both defensive solidity and attacking support in modern systems.

Crystal Palace Secure Ben Chilwell and Quinten Timber in Deadline Day Coup

9/2/2026 · By Koora Live

Crystal Palace have strengthened their squad significantly by completing permanent transfers for England international Ben Chilwell and Dutch midfielder Quinten Timber. The late double acquisition highlights Palace's ambitions as they look to climb the ranks in the Premier League this season.

Ben Chilwell Brings Premier League Pedigree

Ben Chilwell, an established England international, arrives at Crystal Palace with a wealth of top-flight experience. Having previously shone at Leicester City, where he was instrumental in their rise to prominence, as well as at Chelsea, Chilwell is regarded as one of the Premier League’s most accomplished left-backs. Known for his defensive solidity and attacking contributions down the flank, his ability to deliver accurate crosses and overlap in the wide areas could add a new dynamic to Palace’s style of play. Chilwell also brings invaluable know-how, having been a key member of Chelsea's 2020/21 UEFA Champions League-winning side. His arrival signals Crystal Palace’s intent to compete against more established Premier League sides.

Quinten Timber: A Versatile Asset in Midfield

Dutch midfielder Quinten Timber is another exciting addition to the Crystal Palace squad. Known for his versatility and technical ability, Timber can operate across various midfield roles, offering creativity and defensive resilience in equal measure. As a player who has represented the Netherlands at the international level, Timber’s inclusion could help provide the Eagles with more options in the middle of the park. At just 22, he embodies Palace's current focus on balancing youth with experience under their manager’s growing vision for the club. While his adaptability and energy are seen as key strengths, it will be intriguing to see how quickly Timber adjusts to the pace and physicality of the Premier League.

Analysis

Crystal Palace’s double swoop for Ben Chilwell and Quinten Timber is a statement of intent from a club looking to solidify its place in the highly competitive Premier League. Adding Chilwell brings a proven performer with title-winning experience, which could help shore up Palace's defensive setup while enhancing their attacking threat from wide areas. Meanwhile, Timber provides versatility and youthful energy, which could offer the Eagles a much-needed spark in midfield. With both players boasting international experience, they bring a blend of quality and composure that could prove pivotal as the season progresses. These moves could reflect a strategic push by the club to not only cement their mid-table Premier League status but to push for a higher finish, perhaps even contending for a European spot in time. However, their immediate impact will depend on how quickly they gel with their new teammates and adapt to manager tactics.

Crystal Palace's acquisition of Ben Chilwell and Quinten Timber after the deadline marks a significant boost for the club as they prepare for the challenges ahead. With these additions, the Eagles are clearly aiming to make their mark in the Premier League this season and beyond.

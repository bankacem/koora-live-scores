Everton Agree Loan Deal to Re-Sign Jack Grealish from Manchester City

9/1/2026 · By Koora Live

In a significant move during the current transfer window, Everton have struck an agreement with Manchester City to secure the services of Jack Grealish on a season-long loan. The return of the England international promises to add creativity and firepower to Everton's ranks as they look to climb the Premier League table.

Jack Grealish Set for Goodison Park Return

Everton have reached a loan agreement with Manchester City to bring Jack Grealish back to Merseyside for the duration of the current season. This move marks a return for the English midfielder, although the circumstances surrounding his prior affiliation with the club are yet to be clarified. Known for his exceptional vision, dribbling skills, and ability to unlock defenses, Grealish is widely regarded as one of English football's most dynamic playmakers.

Everton's Ambitions in the Premier League

Everton have had a tumultuous time in recent Premier League seasons, often finding it challenging to secure a spot in the upper echelons of the table. The addition of a player of Jack Grealish's caliber could signify their intent to return to competing with the league's heavyweights. Traditionally a club with a rich history, having been founded in 1878 and winning nine English top-flight titles, Everton’s loyal fanbase will be eagerly anticipating what the former Aston Villa captain can bring to their side.

Analysis

This loan deal appears to be a strategic move for both clubs involved. For Manchester City, it might be an opportunity to grant Grealish more consistent game time after what has been a highly competitive environment at the Etihad Stadium. For Everton, this signing could serve to revitalize their midfield and wide attacking options. Grealish's ability to draw defenders and produce moments of magic could be exactly what Everton need to spark their attack and maintain possession in the final third. However, the key question will be how quickly he can adapt to the playing style of manager Sean Dyche, as well as how well he connects with the current squad.

The acquisition of Jack Grealish on loan from Manchester City represents a bold move by Everton as they aim to inject creativity and flair into their squad. While much will depend on Grealish’s adaptation and performance, his return to Goodison Park already has fans buzzing with anticipation for the season ahead.

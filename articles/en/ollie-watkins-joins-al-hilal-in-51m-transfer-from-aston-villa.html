Ollie Watkins Joins Al-Hilal in £51m Transfer from Aston Villa

8/30/2026 · By Koora Live

In a landmark move, Ollie Watkins has completed a £51m transfer to Saudi Pro League side Al-Hilal. The striker, previously a key figure at Aston Villa, admitted he never anticipated his Villa career would conclude in such a manner, as noted by BBC Sport.

Watkins Bids Farewell to Aston Villa

Ollie Watkins departs Aston Villa after establishing himself as a pivotal player since joining the club in 2020 from Brentford. During his time at Villa Park, the forward became a fan favorite due to his work ethic, pace, and knack for scoring crucial goals. Watkins was instrumental in helping Aston Villa stabilize as a competitive Premier League side, contributing significantly to their attacking prowess. Despite his success, the forward acknowledged a sense of surprise at how his time at the club reached its conclusion, as per his comments to BBC Sport.

Al-Hilal's Growing Ambitions in the Saudi Pro League

By securing the signature of Ollie Watkins for £51m, Al-Hilal has continued its trend of high-profile acquisitions. The club, based in Riyadh, is one of the most successful in Asian football history, with multiple AFC Champions League titles to its name. Al-Hilal's recent recruitment drive is part of a broader Saudi Pro League campaign to attract top talent and elevate the league's global standing. The addition of Premier League-caliber players like Watkins highlights the ambition of Saudi clubs to compete on the world stage and bolster their squads with elite talent.

Analysis

Watkins' move to Al-Hilal is a significant statement of intent from the Saudi Pro League as it continues to enhance its profile in global football. For Aston Villa, losing a player of Watkins' caliber could leave a void in their attack, requiring the club to explore their options in the transfer market. From Watkins' perspective, this represents not only a lucrative opportunity but also a chance to make an impact in a league undergoing rapid transformation. While some might view his decision to leave the Premier League as unexpected, it underlines how appealing the Saudi Pro League has become for top-tier footballers in recent years.

Ollie Watkins' £51m transfer to Al-Hilal marks a striking development in his career and highlights the evolving ambitions of the Saudi Pro League. As the league draws increasing attention from global football stars, it remains to be seen how the move will shape Watkins' career trajectory and the balance of footballing power worldwide.

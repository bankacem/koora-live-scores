Mariona Caldentey: Arsenal's 'Cool' Pressure and Seven New Signings Fuel Treble Talk

8/7/2026 · By Koora Live

Mariona Caldentey has adopted a calm approach to the mounting expectations around Arsenal as the club openly talks up the possibility of winning a Treble this season. BBC Sport reports that the midfielder says she embraces the 'cool' pressure, and the squad has been bolstered by seven new players — a development the team cites when framing their ambitions.

Why Caldentey and Arsenal are confident

According to BBC Sport, Mariona Caldentey — identified in the report as a midfielder — has spoken about the atmosphere around the squad as they raise the prospect of a Treble. The headline framing and the excerpt make clear that Caldentey views the pressure surrounding big ambitions in a positive light, describing it as 'cool' pressure. The story links that mindset to the club's recent recruitment drive.

The report highlights that Arsenal have added seven new players to their ranks, a fact the team points to when explaining why they believe they can contend on multiple fronts. While BBC Sport provides the core detail that the squad has been strengthened by seven arrivals, the implication is straightforward: increased options and competition for places can change a team's outlook and deepen its capacity over a long season.

What a Treble would mean and why squad depth matters

In football, the term 'Treble' typically refers to a club winning three major trophies in a single campaign. Common examples include a domestic league title, a principal domestic cup and a continental competition; the exact combination can vary between countries and competitions. Because a Treble requires sustained performance across different formats and a congested fixture list, clubs often emphasise squad depth and rotation when they harbour such ambitions.

Bringing in seven new players — the detail noted by BBC Sport — is the kind of investment that clubs say helps sustain challenges on multiple fronts. Additional signings can provide tactical flexibility, allow for rest and cover for injuries, and create greater internal competition for starting spots. All of those elements are frequently cited by managers and players as prerequisites for mounting a sustained bid for multiple trophies.

Analysis

Framing the pressure as 'cool' is a deliberate psychological stance that can help a group focus on performance rather than external noise. When a senior player like Mariona Caldentey publicly accepts and reframes expectation, it can reduce anxiety in the squad and turn external scrutiny into internal motivation. From a tactical perspective, adding seven new players in one window is a significant shift: integration becomes the immediate task. If the newcomers settle quickly and the manager can balance minutes effectively, the squad will be better equipped to cope with the demands of competing on multiple fronts.

That said, talk of a Treble is always ambitious and invites close scrutiny. The claim reported by BBC Sport is a statement of intent more than a guarantee. Success will depend on consistency, injury management, and how well the new arrivals adapt to the team’s style — variables that play out over the course of a season.

BBC Sport's coverage captures a club publicly raising the bar and a midfielder who is comfortable with that spotlight. With seven new players added to the squad and a mindset of embracing pressure, Arsenal have articulated strong ambitions — but converting that talk into trophies will depend on the difficult, season-long work of cohesion, fitness and consistent performance.

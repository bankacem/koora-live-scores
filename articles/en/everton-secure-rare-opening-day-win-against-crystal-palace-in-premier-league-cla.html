Everton Secure Rare Opening Day Win Against Crystal Palace in Premier League Clash

8/22/2026 · By Koora Live

Everton launched their 2023/24 Premier League campaign with a convincing 2-0 victory over Crystal Palace, marking their first opening-day win in five years. Goals from Kiernan Dewsbury-Hall and Thierno ensured a dream start for Everton, while Palace were left to rue missed opportunities under the watchful eyes of new management.

Everton Off to a Strong Start

Everton fans had plenty to cheer about as their team began the new Premier League season with a confidence-boosting 2-0 triumph over Crystal Palace. This win marked the Toffees' first opening-day success since the 2018/19 season, an encouraging sign for a club hoping for a more stable campaign. New signings Kiernan Dewsbury-Hall and Thierno were the stars of the show, each finding the net to secure all three points. Dewsbury-Hall, a proven talent in midfield, looked sharp in his debut, while Thierno's performance brought an added layer of excitement to an Everton side often criticized for its lack of cutting edge last season.

Missed Chances Cost Crystal Palace

Crystal Palace struggled to capitalize on their opportunities and paid the price for their wastefulness. Despite showing promise in spells, the Eagles were unable to find a way past the Everton defense, leaving new manager Sage with much to ponder in their Premier League opener. The costly missed chances highlight a recurring issue for Palace in recent campaigns — difficulties in turning promising build-up play into tangible results. Though their attacking potential was evident at times, they lacked the clinical finish required to trouble Everton, who proved far more efficient in front of goal.

Analysis

The result will provide a significant morale boost for Everton as they seek to climb higher up the Premier League table this season. Securing an opening-day victory for the first time in five years suggests a potential shift in fortunes for the Merseyside club. Kiernan Dewsbury-Hall and Thierno's debuts demonstrate the effectiveness of Everton's summer recruitment, and their performances suggest they could become key players as the campaign unfolds. Meanwhile, Crystal Palace's inability to find the back of the net raises questions about their attacking potency, a concern that could become a recurring theme unless addressed swiftly. For a team with ambition to secure a mid-table or higher finish, failing to convert chances could jeopardize their goals for the season.

Everton's commanding victory over Crystal Palace on opening day offers a promising start to their Premier League campaign. With impressive performances from newcomers like Kiernan Dewsbury-Hall and Thierno, the Toffees have plenty to build on. On the other hand, Crystal Palace will need to address their missed opportunities quickly if they are to bounce back in the coming fixtures. The early signals from both teams suggest an intriguing season ahead, filled with challenges and opportunities for improvement.

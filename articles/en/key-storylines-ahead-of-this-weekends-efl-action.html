Frequently Asked Questions

What is the EFL?

The English Football League (EFL) is a collection of three professional football divisions below the Premier League: the Championship, League One, and League Two. It is one of the most competitive football pyramids in the world.

Why are surprise leaders notable in the EFL?

Surprise leaders are notable as they disrupt expectations and highlight the unpredictable nature of the league, where smaller or unfancied clubs can challenge traditional giants over the course of the season.

What do resurgent runs indicate in football?

Resurgent runs often indicate improved tactics, player form, and morale within a team. They can turn a disappointing season into a successful one, particularly in leagues as tightly contested as the EFL.

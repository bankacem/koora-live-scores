Tottenham Open Transfer Talks for Man City's Savinho and Marmoush

8/20/2026 · By Koora Live

Tottenham Hotspur have reportedly entered discussions with Manchester City over the potential signing of attacking talents Savinho and Omar Marmoush. Known for their emphasis on dynamic, forward-thinking players, Spurs look to bolster their squad options as the season progresses.

Tottenham Target Promising Duo from Manchester City

According to BBC Sport, Tottenham Hotspur are currently in talks with Manchester City to acquire exciting attacking duo Savinho and Omar Marmoush. This move comes as Spurs aim to reinforce their offensive lineup during a crucial phase of the season. Both players are known for their versatility and attacking prowess — attributes that align well with Tottenham's needs under their current tactical approach. While no further details about the transfer negotiations, such as potential fees or timelines, were disclosed, the mere initiation of talks highlights Spurs' intent to strengthen their squad options in forward areas.

What Savinho and Marmoush Could Bring to Spurs

Savinho, a skillful Brazilian talent known for his technical ability and creativity in the final third, has been tipped as one of Manchester City's promising young players. His flair and potential could make an ideal fit for Tottenham's attacking approach, especially in matches where breaking down a stubborn defense demands ingenuity. Meanwhile, Omar Marmoush, another attacking option under City's umbrella, is admired for his physicality and intelligent movement on the pitch. These qualities could complement the existing forward options in Tottenham's lineup, adding depth and variety to their attack.

Analysis

This potential double transfer deal could signify Tottenham's strategic move to invest in younger, high-potential players who can provide immediate impact while also having significant long-term value. The pursuit of Savinho and Marmoush suggests Spurs are prioritizing versatility, given both players' flexibility in multiple forward roles. Analysts might see this as part of the club's broader intent to remain competitive in domestic and European competition, especially with a need to manage player fitness and tactical demands. Moreover, Manchester City's willingness to engage in discussions may reflect their surplus of attacking talent and a potential readiness to offload players to maintain squad balance.

Tottenham's discussions with Manchester City over Savinho and Marmoush underline their ambition to strengthen their attacking options. If successful, these signings could add both creativity and depth to Spurs' forward line, further solidifying their pursuit of domestic and European success.

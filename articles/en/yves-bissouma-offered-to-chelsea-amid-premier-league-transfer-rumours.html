Yves Bissouma Offered to Chelsea Amid Premier League Transfer Rumours

9/5/2026 · By Koora Live

In the world of ongoing Premier League transfer gossip, Yves Bissouma's name has surfaced as a potential target for Chelsea, according to BBC Sport. The report also highlights interest from Chelsea and Manchester City in Crystal Palace's Adam Wharton, while Arsenal are reportedly exploring a move for Barcelona winger Raphinha. These developments add further intrigue as clubs fine-tune their squads for the challenges ahead.

Chelsea Linked with Yves Bissouma

BBC Sport reveals that ex-Tottenham midfielder Yves Bissouma was offered to Chelsea. The Malian international, known for his energy and ball-winning ability, previously made his mark at Brighton & Hove Albion before joining Tottenham Hotspur. Despite being seen as a promising addition to Spurs when he signed in the summer of 2022, his time at the North London club has been underwhelming thus far, possibly explaining his availability in the current transfer market. Chelsea, who are undergoing a significant rebuilding phase under Mauricio Pochettino, may view Bissouma as a solution to their midfield issues, especially considering their struggles with injuries and squad consistency in recent seasons.

Adam Wharton Attracting Big-Name Suitors

BBC Sport also reports that both Chelsea and Manchester City have shown interest in Crystal Palace's Adam Wharton. While Wharton is not yet a household name, his potential has clearly caught the attention of two of the Premier League's top teams. Chelsea, in particular, have been investing heavily in young talents as part of their long-term strategy, while Manchester City continually look to strengthen their squad depth under Pep Guardiola. If a move for Wharton materializes, it could mark another major step in the young player's career as he makes the jump to a top-tier club.

Arsenal Eye Barcelona's Raphinha

Another intriguing development is the possibility of Arsenal targeting Barcelona winger Raphinha. Since joining the Catalan club from Leeds United in 2022, Raphinha has showcased his flair and creativity. Mikel Arteta's Arsenal are building momentum following their impressive form in recent Premier League campaigns, and adding a player of Raphinha's caliber could bolster their attack further. While no concrete moves have been reported, this is a storyline to monitor given Arsenal's ambitions in both domestic and European competitions.

Analysis

These reports underline the constant movement and speculation that characterize the modern transfer market. The potential availability of Yves Bissouma suggests that Tottenham might not see him as a long-term solution in their midfield setup, opening the door for Chelsea to consider his skills. Meanwhile, Adam Wharton's emergence as a talent on the radar of Manchester City and Chelsea highlights the importance of scouting and development at a time when homegrown players are increasingly valued. Lastly, Arsenal's interest in Raphinha could signify their intent to establish more attacking options to close the gap on Manchester City in their quest for Premier League glory.

The latest transfer speculation sheds light on the intricate dynamics of squad building among Premier League clubs. Whether it's Chelsea considering Bissouma, Arsenal looking at Raphinha, or young talents like Adam Wharton being linked with top teams, the transfer market continues to provide plenty of intrigue for football fans worldwide.

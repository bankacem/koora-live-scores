Nottingham Forest Secure Daniel Munoz in £22m Move from Crystal Palace

8/31/2026 · By Koora Live

In a significant transfer move, Nottingham Forest have finalized the signing of Daniel Munoz for a reported fee of £22 million from Crystal Palace. This acquisition marks a key addition to Forest's squad as they look to strengthen their ranks in the ongoing season.

Nottingham Forest Invest in Daniel Munoz

Nottingham Forest has made a bold statement in the transfer market by securing the services of Daniel Munoz for a reported £22 million. Munoz joins from Premier League rivals Crystal Palace, where he honed his skills in recent seasons. This deal underlines Forest's intent to bolster their squad and ensure competitiveness in domestic and potential international campaigns.

Founded in 1865, Nottingham Forest is one of the oldest football clubs in England with a rich history that includes winning two European Cups under the legendary Brian Clough. Today, the club is working to re-establish itself as a consistent Premier League force after their promotion in 2022. The acquisition of Munoz exemplifies their commitment to building a squad capable of challenging at the highest level.

What Munoz Brings to Nottingham Forest

Daniel Munoz arrives at the City Ground with the reputation of being a versatile and reliable player. The 27-year-old Colombian international has made a name for himself through his robust defensive abilities coupled with an impressive ability to contribute in offensive transitions.

While his time at Crystal Palace showcased these attributes, his move to Nottingham Forest represents an opportunity to step into a more prominent role within the squad. Forest fans will be eager to see how his addition enhances their defensive lineup as well as their ability to build from the back. Given the challenges of the English Premier League, his experience and skill set could prove instrumental in Forest's bid for a strong campaign.

Man City Reject Fresh Rodri Bid — Transfer Gossip Featuring Reijnders and Djed Spence

8/13/2026 · By Koora Live

BBC Sport's round-up of transfer chatter in its 'Friday's gossip' column highlights several prominent developments around Europe's top clubs. The key items reported include Barcelona having a renewed approach for Manchester City's midfielder Rodri turned down, Tijjani Reijnders being linked with an exit from Manchester City, and Tottenham's Djed Spence reportedly close to a move to Inter Milan.

Rodri and Barcelona: City said to have rebuffed fresh approach

According to BBC Sport's gossip column, Barcelona lodged another approach for Rodri that Manchester City declined. Rodri is widely recognised as one of City's midfield anchors and has been the subject of speculation in past transfer windows given his profile as a defensive midfielder who shields the backline and initiates possession phases.

Clubs of Barcelona's stature often seek experienced midfield reinforcements to balance creativity and stability, and a reported bid for an established Premier League midfielder would fit that pattern. If true, the rejection documented by BBC Sport suggests Manchester City see continued value in retaining a player with Rodri's attributes for their squad structure.

Reijnders departure and Spence's reported Inter move

BBC Sport's item also states that Tijjani Reijnders is set to leave Manchester City. Reijnders has been discussed in wider football coverage as a player operating in midfield roles, and reports of a potential departure would form part of routine squad reshaping that clubs undertake across transfer windows.

Separately, Tottenham's Djed Spence is described by BBC Sport as being on the verge of a move to Inter Milan. Spence is known for his attacking full-back/wing-back qualities, and a transfer to a club like Inter — a team with a European profile and ambitions — would represent a typical next step for a young defender aiming for regular top-level competition. BBC Sport lists these developments alongside other rumours as part of its gossip bulletin.

Analysis

Transfer gossip columns like BBC Sport's distil many individual threads into a concise snapshot, and they should be read as early indicators rather than confirmed transactions. The report that Manchester City rejected a fresh approach for Rodri can be interpreted as a sign the club values midfield continuity, while links involving Reijnders and Djed Spence point to routine squad turnover that clubs pursue to refresh options and balance books. Analysts might see the combination of these items as part of the broader summer manoeuvrings that tend to intensify as windows progress.

BBC Sport's latest gossip bulletin highlights a mix of rebuffed interest, potential exits and near-deals involving notable players. Readers should watch for official confirmations from the clubs involved, but the items reported give a useful snapshot of how squads could be reshaped in the near term.

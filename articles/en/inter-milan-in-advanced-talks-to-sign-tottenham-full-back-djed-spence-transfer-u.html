Inter Milan in advanced talks to sign Tottenham full-back Djed Spence — transfer update

8/12/2026 · By Koora Live

According to BBC Sport, Inter Milan are in advanced talks to sign Tottenham and England full-back Djed Spence in a deal worth a minimum of £25.6m. The report frames the move as at an advanced stage, with the fee described as a minimum figure, signalling significant progress in transfer discussions between the clubs.

What the reported deal includes

The core facts published by BBC Sport are straightforward: Inter Milan and Tottenham Hotspur are reportedly negotiating over Djed Spence, and the transaction is being discussed with a reported minimum value of £25.6m. The phrasing used — "in advanced talks" — implies that the two clubs and the player's representatives may be closing in on an agreement, although BBC Sport's wording stops short of confirming a completed transfer.

In the contemporary transfer market, "advanced talks" can cover a range of scenarios such as agreement on basic financial terms, personal terms being discussed with the player, or clubs finalising the structure of the deal. However, without a club statement or further reporting beyond the BBC Sport excerpt, the situation remains one of reported negotiation rather than a finalised move.

Inter Milan are one of Italy's leading clubs, regularly competing in Serie A and European competitions. Tottenham Hotspur, commonly called Spurs, are a household name in the English Premier League. A potential transfer involving a full-back like Spence would interest supporters because full-backs often play a pivotal role in modern systems, contributing defensively and in attack.

For Inter, adding an English full-back would reflect continued international recruitment to strengthen depth and options in wide defensive positions. All of this should be read as contextual analysis rather than confirmed fact about the transaction itself.

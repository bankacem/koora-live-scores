8/20/2026 · By Koora Live

West Bromwich Albion forward Aune Heggebo has been suspended for two matches by The Football Association after scoring a goal in which the ball was handled in a fixture involving Norwich City, BBC Sport reports. The disciplinary decision follows an incident that has drawn attention to the enforcement of handball rules in English football.

What the FA ruling says

The ban reflects the governing body's disciplinary response to an event in which a goal was scored with the hand.

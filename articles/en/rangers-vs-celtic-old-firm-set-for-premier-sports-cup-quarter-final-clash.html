What is the Premier Sports Cup?

The Premier Sports Cup is the sponsored name commonly used for the Scottish League Cup in recent seasons. It is a national knockout competition in Scotland that features clubs from the professional leagues and progresses through rounds such as the quarter-finals to determine a champion.

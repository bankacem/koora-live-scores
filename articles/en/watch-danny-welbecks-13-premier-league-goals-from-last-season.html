Watch Danny Welbeck's 13 Premier League goals from last season

8/2/2026

Chelsea's recent signing Danny Welbeck features in a new highlights compilation that collects every Premier League goal he scored last season. The package, presented by BBC Sport, brings together all 13 of his top-flight strikes from the campaign before his move to Stamford Bridge.

The footage is notable for including the three goals he managed against his new club. Those efforts are part of the full set and sit alongside his other finishes from the season, allowing viewers to review the complete run of scoring moments that preceded his transfer.

For Chelsea supporters curious about what their incoming forward delivered in the previous campaign, the compilation offers a concise way to see his goals in sequence. It also gives neutral fans an easy reference to assess the raw output he produced in the Premier League last season.

BBC Sport’s assembled package is available for fans who want to watch all 13 goals in one place, including the trio scored versus Chelsea. The feature serves as a simple visual record of Welbeck’s scoring record in the league ahead of the new chapter at his new club.

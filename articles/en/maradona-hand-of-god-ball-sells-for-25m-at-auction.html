Maradona 'Hand of God' ball sells for £2.5m at auction

8/24/2026 · By Koora Live

According to BBC Sport, the football involved in Diego Maradona's infamous 'Hand of God' goal has been sold at auction for £2.5m. The ball is the same one with which Maradona scored that celebrated and controversial strike in the 1986 World Cup quarter-final victory over England.

The object and its provenance

The item at the centre of this report is the actual football used in the moment widely referred to as the 'Hand of God' goal. This is a piece of sporting memorabilia that ties directly to one of the most discussed incidents in international football, and its sale was reported by BBC Sport. Beyond the fact that the ball was sold at auction for £2.5m, the available report does not provide additional transactional details such as the identity of the buyer or the auction venue.

Why such items attract strong interest

Memorabilia connected to iconic moments in sport routinely commands attention from collectors, museums and enthusiasts. Items that have a direct physical link to notable incidents — whether celebrated or controversial — often transcend their material value because they carry cultural and historical resonance. In football, moments from the World Cup in particular tend to have global significance given the tournament's status as the sport's premier international competition.

Diego Maradona is one of the sport's most recognised figures worldwide, and objects associated with his career naturally attract interest. As the BBC Sport coverage makes clear, even a single match incident can turn an ordinary football into an artefact that collectors are prepared to bid large sums to acquire.

Analysis

This sale underlines how sport intertwines with cultural memory: objects tied to decisive or contentious moments can appreciate far beyond their intrinsic worth. From a market perspective, the provenance — the documented link between an object and the event it was part of — is the key determinant of value. In this case, BBC Sport's report that the football was the one used for Maradona's 'Hand of God' goal is the critical piece of provenance driving interest. Analysts might view such a purchase as both a collectible investment and a statement about the continuing global fascination with landmark World Cup moments and marquee players like Maradona.

The reported £2.5m sale of the football tied to Maradona's 'Hand of God' goal, as covered by BBC Sport, is another example of how single moments in football can create lasting cultural artefacts — items that command significant sums because of the stories and memories they embody.

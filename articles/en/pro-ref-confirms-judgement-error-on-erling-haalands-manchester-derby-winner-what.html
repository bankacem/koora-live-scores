Frequently Asked Questions

What is Pro Ref?

Pro Ref is the refereeing arm of the Premier League, responsible for appointing officials, overseeing match conduct and ensuring compliance with the league’s regulations. The organisation also operates the Video Assistant Referee (VAR) system to support decision‑making on the field.

How does Pro Ref handle judgement errors?

When a potential off‑side or goal recognition issue is disputed, Pro Ref reviews the relevant footage. If an error is confirmed, they issue a formal statement acknowledging the mistake and updating the match record accordingly.

Will the admission change the outcome of the match?

This particular admission from Pro Ref recognizes that the goal was incorrectly awarded. It does not alter the scoreline or the final result, but the correction maintains the accuracy of official records and acknowledges that the decision was a mistake.

Villa open talks for Zion Suzuki as Juventus' Emiliano Martinez hopes rise – transfer gossip

8/15/2026 · By Koora Live

BBC Sport reports that Aston Villa have opened negotiations over Japanese goalkeeper Zion Suzuki, a move that is said to increase Juventus' chances of signing Emiliano Martínez. The same round of transfer rumour coverage also notes that Arsenal have been informed of a price for striker Victor Osimhen.

These developments form part of the weekend’s transfer chatter compiled by BBC Sport. Below we unpack what the claims mean, put the players and clubs into context, and outline likely implications while making clear which details remain unconfirmed.

Aston Villa open talks over Zion Suzuki

According to BBC Sport, Aston Villa are engaged in negotiations concerning Zion Suzuki. Suzuki is a young goalkeeper from Japan who has come to prominence in recent seasons and has attracted attention beyond his domestic market.

For a club like Villa — who have invested in their squad in recent windows and are aiming to build depth across positions — pursuing a goalkeeper makes sense in sporting terms. Any talk of a potential incoming keeper will naturally invite scrutiny over the current goalkeeping situation and what that could mean for existing players at the club.

How Suzuki links feed Juventus' interest in Emiliano Martínez, and Arsenal's Osimhen update

BBC Sport’s report links Villa’s approach for Suzuki with an increase in Juventus’ hopes of signing Emiliano Martínez. Martínez is widely known as Aston Villa’s first‑choice goalkeeper and an Argentina international; reports of Villa pursuing reinforcements are often interpreted as potentially creating an opening for other clubs to move for their established players.

Separately, the same report says Arsenal have been told a price for Victor Osimhen. Osimhen is broadly recognised as a top‑level striker who has been frequently mentioned in transfer speculation; being 'told a price' is a common part of preliminary interest between clubs, but does not constitute a completed deal.

Analysis

If Villa are indeed pursuing a young goalkeeper such as Zion Suzuki, the move can be read as part of routine squad management — adding depth, investing in potential, or planning for the medium term. Reports that Juventus’ interest in Emiliano Martínez has been boosted are logical corollaries: bringing in another keeper often creates uncertainty around the incumbent, which can open negotiation channels with other clubs. However, transfer markets are fluid, and linkages between separate moves do not guarantee downstream departures.

Regarding Victor Osimhen, the phrasing used by BBC Sport — that Arsenal have 'been told a price' — is typical of early, exploratory stages in transfer dialogue. It signals interest and the start of potential negotiations rather than a near‑term resolution. Fans and analysts should therefore treat these developments as evolving narratives rather than confirmed transactions.

The BBC Sport update adds fresh angles to several high‑profile goalkeeper and striker stories: Villa’s reported talks for Zion Suzuki, the knock‑on effect on Emiliano Martínez and renewed price chatter around Victor Osimhen. All are worth following as the transfer window progresses, but for now they remain reported possibilities rather than completed moves.

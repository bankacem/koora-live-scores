Celtic Unlikely to Sign New Players Before LASK Tie, Says O’Neill

8/18/2026 · By Koora Live

Celtic manager Martin O'Neill has revealed that no new signings are expected ahead of their upcoming Champions League play-off first leg against Austrian side LASK. The manager also expressed his satisfaction with retaining key midfielder Reo Hatate amid transfer speculation.

O’Neill Rules Out Immediate Celtic Transfers

In a key update for fans, Martin O’Neill, manager of Celtic, has stated that the club is unlikely to complete any signings before their Wednesday clash against LASK in the first leg of a Champions League play-off. Celtic have been active in transfer markets historically, often looking to bolster their squad depth ahead of European competitions. However, this year the focus may be on consolidating existing strengths rather than rushing new additions.

Reo Hatate Likely to Stay at Celtic

The Celtic boss also emphasized his contentment with the retention of Reo Hatate, an influential midfielder for the team. Hatate has been key to Celtic's midfield dynamism since joining the club. By retaining Hatate amidst reported speculation, O’Neill seems keen on maintaining team cohesion during such a critical phase of the season. With Champions League qualification on the line, experienced and reliable players like Hatate could be essential.

Analysis

This announcement from Martin O’Neill provides insight into Celtic’s current focus and strategy. By deciding against making new signings before such a critical Champions League match, O’Neill might be signaling confidence in his current squad’s ability to deliver under pressure. On the other hand, some supporters may view the absence of new additions as a missed opportunity to inject fresh talent into the team for a tough European campaign. The retention of Reo Hatate is another significant statement, as he is someone whose contributions have been pivotal in Celtic's recent success. Analysts could view his continued presence as a crucial factor in navigating both domestic and European challenges.

As Celtic gear up for their Champions League play-off against LASK, the decisions to forego immediate signings and maintain stability with key players like Reo Hatate suggest a vote of confidence in the current team setup. How this approach fares will soon be tested on the European stage.

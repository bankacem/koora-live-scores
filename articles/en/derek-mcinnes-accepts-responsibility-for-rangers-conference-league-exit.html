Derek McInnes Accepts Responsibility for Rangers' Conference League Exit

8/28/2026 · By Koora Live

Rangers manager Derek McInnes has publicly taken full responsibility following his team's defeat in the UEFA Conference League, which he described as 'embarrassing.' The Scottish club was eliminated by Czech outfit Jablonec, marking a major disappointment for the historic Glasgow side.

McInnes Reflects on an Embarrassing European Exit

In a candid admission, Rangers' manager Derek McInnes has expressed deep regret for his team's unceremonious exit from the UEFA Conference League at the hands of Jablonec. Speaking after the match, McInnes described the result as outright "embarrassing" and did not shy away from accepting full responsibility for the outcome. The Scottish side, internationally renowned for its rich European history, has endured a humiliating setback in what many fans and analysts see as a competition the club had hoped to use for European revival following years of rebuilding.

A Bitter Blow for Rangers in European Competition

The defeat to Jablonec is a stark reminder of the challenges Rangers face in reclaiming their status on the continental stage. Known as one of Scotland's most decorated clubs, with a storied past that includes a famous European Cup Winners' Cup triumph in 1972, Rangers have long held aspirations of competing at the highest levels in Europe. The UEFA Conference League was introduced in the 2021-22 season to offer more clubs the experience of European football, but Rangers' failure to navigate past Jablonec underscores how demanding such tournaments can be, even for legacy clubs.

Analysis

Rangers' early exit from the UEFA Conference League highlights the competitive nature of European football at all levels. While Jablonec is not traditionally considered among the elite of the continent, the result illustrates how clubs cannot afford to underestimate any opponents in European knockout stages. The responsibility taken by Derek McInnes reflects a commendable level of accountability, but it may also open the door for scrutiny from fans and pundits who have high expectations for a club of Rangers' pedigree. This loss raises important questions about the team's preparation and depth, as well as their ability to deliver under pressure in pivotal fixtures.

Rangers' exit from the UEFA Conference League, as lamented by Derek McInnes, underscores the difficulties the club faces in their ongoing quest to return to European prominence. This loss may serve as a harsh but necessary wake-up call for the Scottish giants as they aim to rebuild and deliver on their storied legacy.

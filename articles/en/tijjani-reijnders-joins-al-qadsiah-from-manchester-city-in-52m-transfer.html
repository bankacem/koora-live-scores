Tijjani Reijnders Joins Al-Qadsiah from Manchester City in £52m Transfer

8/19/2026 · By Koora Live

Netherlands international Tijjani Reijnders has bid farewell to Manchester City, completing a high-profile £52m move to Saudi Arabian side Al-Qadsiah. The blockbuster transfer continues the trend of football stars heading to the Saudi Pro League, as the competition strengthens its global footprint.

Reijnders' Move: A Significant Transfer in European Football

Tijjani Reijnders, a key figure in Dutch midfield ranks, has officially left Manchester City to join Al-Qadsiah in a deal worth £52 million. The 25-year-old midfielder has been an important part of his national setup, with his performances earning him recognition on the international stage.

Manchester City, a global football powerhouse, signed Reijnders with ambitions for him to help bolster their already formidable squad. The Citizens, managed by Pep Guardiola, are no strangers to navigating high-stakes footballing markets, and Reijnders' time at the Etihad Stadium likely added depth to one of the most competitive teams in Europe. However, with this latest move, the Dutch playmaker shifts his focus to a new chapter in the Saudi Pro League.

Al-Qadsiah’s Rising Ambition

The signing of Reijnders is a bold statement of intent from Al-Qadsiah, a club keen to cement a reputation as one of the more ambitious entities in the Saudi Pro League. With Saudi football making headlines over recent seasons for its lucrative signings and increased investment in global stars, this transfer underscores the league's intent to attract top-tier talent from Europe.

Reijnders’ arrival will not only boost Al-Qadsiah’s midfield but also elevate the club’s profile among fans worldwide. While historically overshadowed by more dominant footballing nations, Saudi Arabia has seen a dramatic transformation in its league thanks to significant financial backing and the acquisition of marquee players. Reijnders’ move will undoubtedly amplify the growing appeal of the league.

Analysis

Reijnders’ transfer to Al-Qadsiah exemplifies the Saudi Pro League’s determination to attract premium talent from Europe’s leading clubs. While such a departure from Manchester City might raise eyebrows, especially given his potential and capabilities, it reflects the growing pull of Saudi Arabia as a viable destination for top-tier players. The financial clout on display not only enhances the league’s competitive stature but also poses broader implications for the dynamics of global football, as it may encourage other major players to follow similar paths. For City, the reported £52m fee further strengthens their financial position for reinvesting into their squad, sustaining their capability to compete both domestically and in Europe.

Tijjani Reijnders’ transfer from Manchester City to Al-Qadsiah marks another milestone in football’s changing landscape, where Saudi Arabia continues to emerge as a growing force. With the Dutch midfielder joining the Saudi Pro League, both his career trajectory and the league's global profile stand to benefit from this headline-making move.

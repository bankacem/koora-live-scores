Why Tottenham might be tracking Gakpo

Premier League clubs routinely monitor players across their rivals and around Europe, looking at a mixture of on-ball and off-ball data as well as scouting reports. According to BBC Sport, Tottenham have an interest in Liverpool’s forward Cody Gakpo; while the outlet points to underlying data as part of that interest, it does not publish specific figures in its summary. In general, interest of this type can be sparked by attributes such as versatility across the front line, a consistent goal threat, the ability to create chances for teammates, or qualities that suit a high-possession, progressive style.

How Gakpo could be deployed in De Zerbi’s system

Roberto De Zerbi’s teams are known for positional fluidity, emphasis on ball progression and coordinated pressing. When managers prioritise these principles they often seek forwards who can rotate positions, contribute to build-up play, and engage in collective pressing patterns. If Tottenham are looking at Gakpo through this lens, the considerations would include how he performs in tight spaces, his willingness to participate in structured pressing triggers, and his capacity to combine with creative midfielders.

Clubs also weigh adaptability: a forward who can operate on either flank or through the centre gives a coach tactical options in-game and across a season. Any assessment by Tottenham would likely consider how a player’s skillset complements existing attacking personnel and the manager’s preferred way of playing rather than treating a signing as a standalone solution.

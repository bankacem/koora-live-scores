Manchester United Agree £70m Fee for Brighton Midfielder Carlos Baleba

8/21/2026 · By Koora Live

In a major transfer development, Manchester United have reportedly reached an agreement with Brighton & Hove Albion for the signing of midfielder Carlos Baleba. Carlos Baleba Set for High-Profile Move to Old Trafford

Carlos Baleba appears poised to join Manchester United following an agreement with Brighton & Hove Albion over a transfer fee that could rise to £70 million. Baleba, a highly-rated midfielder, has been turning heads with his performances for Brighton, a club known for its knack for developing young talent.

Mohamed Salah Linked with Trabzonspor Move Amid Transfer Rumors

8/2/2026 · By Koora Live

Football fans are abuzz with the latest transfer speculation, as Mohamed Salah is reportedly nearing a move to Turkish side Trabzonspor, according to BBC Sport. Prominent names like Bayern Munich and Manchester City also surface in the rumor mill regarding potential player deals.

Mohamed Salah and Potential Switch to Trabzonspor

Egyptian forward Mohamed Salah, widely regarded as one of the best attackers in the modern game, appears to be edging closer to joining Trabzonspor. Salah, who has had a glittering career at Liverpool, dominating the Premier League since his arrival in 2017, has been linked with various high-profile moves in recent years. Trabzonspor, one of Turkey's biggest clubs and a six-time Süper Lig champion, may offer a fresh challenge for the 31-year-old superstar. While details of the potential deal remain sparse, the prospect of Salah bringing his undeniable talent to the Turkish league could elevate its global profile considerably.

Bayern Munich Eyes Benjamin Sesko

Another headline-making rumor involves Bayern Munich’s interest in Manchester United striker Benjamin Sesko. Known for their attacking prowess, the German giants are continuously on the lookout for young and talented forwards who can complement their established squad. Sesko, a promising talent known for his physicality and finishing, could be an ideal fit for the Bavarian side, especially as they seek to maintain dominance both domestically and in European competitions. If Bayern moves forward with this potential transfer, it would continue their trend of bolstering their squad with emerging talents.

Enzo Maresca Keen on Reuniting with Pedro Neto

The transfer gossip also links Manchester City boss Enzo Maresca to Pedro Neto, the Chelsea winger who Maresca reportedly hopes to work with again. Such a move would reunite the two figures, potentially adding depth to a City squad already brimming with attacking options. Neto’s pace, creativity, and experience in Premier League football could make him a valuable asset to Maresca’s plans, ensuring City sustains their dominant run in both domestic and European competitions. It remains to be seen whether this reunion will materialize, but it highlights the manager’s desire to bring in players he has worked well with in the past.

Analysis

Transfer rumors like these underline the fast-changing dynamics of football, where clubs are constantly seeking to strengthen their squads. Mohamed Salah’s possible move to Trabzonspor could signal a significant shift in Turkish football, with a globally recognized talent potentially joining their ranks. Bayern Munich's interest in Benjamin Sesko reflects their commitment to building for the future, while Enzo Maresca’s reported desire for Pedro Neto demonstrates the importance of trusted connections in managerial strategies. If even one of these moves materializes, they could have lasting impacts on the clubs involved and their competitive landscapes.

Transfer gossip like that involving Salah, Sesko, and Neto showcases the ever-evolving nature of the football world. Whether these rumored moves come to fruition or not, they spark conversations about the ambitions of top players and clubs.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Newcastle 2.0 project?

The Newcastle 2.0 project is an initiative announced by the club’s new ownership to bring a modern, science‑based approach to football. It focuses on building a competitive team that will regularly compete in the Champions League while developing a strong youth academy and sustainable finances.

Who is Matthias Jaissle and why was he chosen?

Matthias Jaissle is an Austrian coach who has managed SC Freiburg in the Bundesliga. He was selected for Newcastle because of his experience in guiding a club through promotion and his proven ability to blend defensive organisation with attacking flair—a blend that aligns with the club’s intended style.

Why face Valencia in the first match?

Valencia, competently organized in La Liga, offers a meaningful challenge that is statistically manageable for a new coach’s debut. The match provides a balanced test of tactics and squad cohesion while delivering substantial spectator interest.

Brentford sign Sangare from Lens in club-record transfer – transfer news

8/2/2026 · By Koora Live

Brentford have confirmed the signing of Malian midfielder Mamadou Sangare from French club Lens for a club record fee, BBC Sport reports. The move represents a significant investment by the London club and will draw attention given the player's nationality and his previous association with Lens.

The transfer was announced via BBC Sport, which is the source of the confirmation. Beyond the headline detail that the fee sets a new record for Brentford, the club and the player will now focus on integration and what the arrival means for the squad balance and midfield options.

What this signing tells us about Brentford's strategy

Brentford's acquisition of Mamadou Sangare for a club record fee signals a willingness to invest in midfield talent from continental Europe. Over recent years the club has built a reputation in English football for intelligent recruitment — identifying players outside the most obvious markets and bringing them into a system that can maximise their strengths. That reputation is consistent with making substantial commitments to secure targeted players.

From a sporting perspective, spending a club record sum on a midfielder suggests Brentford view central areas of the pitch as a priority. Whether the signing is intended to add creativity, ball progression or defensive balance is not specified in the announcement; these are the kinds of strategic choices clubs make when committing significant funds to a single player.

Player background: Mamadou Sangare and RC Lens

The player at the heart of the move, Mamadou Sangare, is identified in the report as a Malian midfielder who was with Lens. RC Lens are a well-established French club that compete in the top tiers of French football and are frequently involved in recruiting and developing talented players from within France and abroad.

Bringing a player from a Ligue 1 side like Lens to the Premier League is a familiar pathway. For the individual, it represents a step into one of the most watched domestic leagues in world football. For Brentford, recruiting from continental leagues can provide access to different player profiles and playing education that complement their existing squad composition.

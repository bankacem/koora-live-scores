Fernandes and Shaw Honored with PFA Player of the Year Awards

8/25/2026 · By Koora Live

Bruno Fernandes and Khadija Shaw have been crowned the Professional Footballers’ Association (PFA) Men’s and Women’s Player of the Year, respectively. The Manchester United captain and Manchester City forward were recognized for their exceptional contributions to the game, marking major milestones in their illustrious careers.

Bruno Fernandes: A Leader Among Red Devils

Bruno Fernandes, the captain of Manchester United, has been a transformative figure for the Red Devils since his arrival in January 2020 from Sporting CP. Operating primarily as an attacking midfielder, Fernandes possesses a rare blend of creativity, vision, and goal-scoring ability, consistently delivering decisive performances for his team. His ability to control matches and contribute in critical moments has cemented his place among the Premier League’s elite players. Winning the PFA Men's Player of the Year award only adds to his growing legacy at Old Trafford. The accolade is a testament to his influence on the team, as both a leader and a playmaker on the pitch.

Khadija Shaw: A Star of Women’s Football

Khadija Shaw, affectionately known as ‘Bunny,’ has emerged as a standout performer in women’s football. Representing Manchester City in the Women’s Super League, Shaw has dazzled with her technical skills, goal-scoring prowess, and physical presence. Since joining City, she has been a focal point of their attacking setup, often leading the charge in key matches. Shaw's impact extends beyond just statistics; her ability to inspire her team and dominate opponents has earned her widespread recognition. Being named the PFA Women’s Player of the Year underscores her tremendous contributions to her club and reinforces her status as one of the game's brightest stars.

Analysis

The recognition of two such influential figures in Fernandes and Shaw as the PFA Player of the Year award winners highlights the pivotal roles they play at Manchester United and Manchester City, respectively. For Fernandes, this award reflects his impact not only as a skillful midfielder but also as a leader who has taken a young and evolving team under his wing. Shaw's achievement signals the growing recognition of Caribbean talent in elite women's football, a significant development given her roots in Jamaica, a nation not traditionally associated with global footballing superstars. Their victories exemplify the importance of consistent excellence and individual brilliance in the team-oriented nature of football.

Bruno Fernandes and Khadija Shaw's triumphs in the PFA Player of the Year awards are richly deserved and highlight their immense contributions to their clubs and the game of football. These accolades not only celebrate their on-pitch brilliance but also underline their influence as role models and leaders in their respective teams.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the PFA Player of the Year award?

The Professional Footballers' Association Player of the Year award is an annual accolade given to the best male and female footballers in English football, as voted by their fellow professional players.

Who has won the 2023 PFA Player of the Year awards?

Bruno Fernandes of Manchester United won the men's award, while Khadija Shaw of Manchester City secured the women's award.

What makes these awards significant?

These awards serve as a recognition of individual excellence and are highly valued within the football community because winners are chosen by votes from fellow athletes, reflecting the respect and admiration of their peers.

Gold family to sell West Ham stake to Amanda Staveley-led consortium

8/1/2026

The Gold family has agreed to sell its stake in West Ham United to a consortium led by Amanda Staveley, the family has confirmed. Vanessa Gold, daughter of former co-owner David Gold, has told the club and other interested parties that she intends to sell the shares to the Staveley-headed group, BBC Sport reports.

David Gold is identified as a former co-owner of West Ham United, while Amanda Staveley is described as an ex-Newcastle co-owner. The confirmation from Vanessa Gold marks a significant development in the ownership picture at the club and signals a transfer of the family's holding to the new consortium, according to the BBC Sport account of events.

Details beyond the announced intention to sell were not provided in the report. The story as carried by BBC Sport focuses on the confirmation from Vanessa Gold and the identity of the consortium leader, Amanda Staveley, without offering further specifics about timing, the size of the stake, or other parties involved.

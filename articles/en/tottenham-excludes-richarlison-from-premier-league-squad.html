Tottenham Excludes Richarlison from Premier League Squad

9/4/2026 · By Koora Live

In a significant decision, Tottenham Hotspur has opted to omit Brazilian forward Richarlison from their Premier League squad for the current season. The move, confirmed by BBC Sport, suggests the player is not part of the club's plans for this campaign.

Richarlison’s Exclusion from Premier League Squad

Tottenham Hotspur has taken a decisive step by leaving Richarlison out of their submitted squad for the ongoing Premier League season. This exclusion underlines the Brazilian international's uncertain future at the club, with BBC Sport confirming that he does not feature in the team's plans this year. Such decisions often hint at broader strategic reshuffles or potential transfer scenarios as squads are finalized post-summer or winter transfer windows.

Richarlison’s Background and Tottenham Career

Richarlison, who has previously starred for clubs like Watford and Everton, joined Tottenham with high hopes and significant expectations. Known for his dynamic playing style, versatility across attacking positions, and knack for scoring crucial goals, the Brazilian brought an exciting prospect to Spurs’ attack. However, his time in North London has not reached the heights envisioned, with injuries and inconsistent form overshadowing his potential impact. In light of his exclusion, questions will inevitably arise about his future with the club, especially given Tottenham's ongoing transition under new management.

Analysis

This decision to leave Richarlison out of the Premier League squad holds weighty implications for both the player and the club. For Tottenham, it underscores the ongoing tactical and squad leadership changes in the post-Harry Kane era. Such moves often signal a desire for a new direction. From Richarlison’s perspective, the omission could pave the way for a potential departure from the club, with his talents likely to attract interest from other teams. Analysts might interpret this decision as a clear message that the Brazilian star needs either a fresh environment or a substantial improvement to re-integrate into competitive plans.

The omission of Richarlison from Tottenham’s Premier League squad marks a pivotal moment for both the player and the club. While it highlights the strategic direction Spurs are taking, it leaves Richarlison facing an uncertain future that could lead him to seek a fresh start elsewhere.

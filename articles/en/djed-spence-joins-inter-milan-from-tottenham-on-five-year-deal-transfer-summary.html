Djed Spence joins Inter Milan from Tottenham on five-year deal — transfer summary

8/15/2026 · By Koora Live

Inter Milan have bolstered their defensive options by signing England full-back Djed Spence from Tottenham, according to BBC Sport. The move sees Spence make the switch from the Premier League to Serie A on a five-year contract as he begins a new chapter in Italy.

What the transfer involves

BBC Sport reports that England full-back Djed Spence has completed a transfer from Tottenham to Inter Milan, agreeing a five-year deal. The headline capture is straightforward: a player who has been registered at Tottenham will now be on Inter Milan’s books and will relocate to Italy to join the Serie A side.

The contract length is a notable element in the announcement. A five-year agreement signals that Inter see Spence as a medium- to long-term addition to their squad structure, providing the club with time to integrate him into their plans and for the player to adapt to a different league and style of football.

Context: clubs and competition

Inter Milan are one of Italy’s leading clubs, based in Milan and competing in Serie A, the top tier of Italian football. They play home matches at the San Siro — a stadium they share with city rivals — and frequently operate with ambitions in both domestic and European competitions.

Tottenham Hotspur are a prominent London club in the Premier League, competing at the top level of English football and playing home fixtures at the Tottenham Hotspur Stadium. Transfers between the Premier League and Serie A are not uncommon, and moves of young full-backs to Italy have precedent as clubs seek to blend tactical development with competitive minutes.

Analysis

This signing suggests Inter are investing in a full-back with the intention of securing stability in that position over several seasons. Moving from the English game to Italy will present both tactical and cultural adjustments; Serie A is often characterised by different defensive responsibilities and positional nuances compared with the Premier League. Analysts might view a multi-year deal as an indication that Inter want to provide the player with time to develop within their system rather than expecting an immediate, guaranteed impact.

The transfer of Djed Spence from Tottenham to Inter Milan on a five-year contract, reported by BBC Sport, marks a clear commitment by the Italian club to add an England full-back to their squad. The coming months will reveal how he adapts to Serie A and how the signing fits into Inter’s tactical plans.

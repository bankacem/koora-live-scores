Celtic Manager Martin O'Neill Hospitalized After Surgery

8/5/2026 · By Koora Live

Celtic Football Club has revealed that their manager, Martin O'Neill, is currently recovering in hospital after undergoing a minor medical procedure. The announcement was made by the club, sparking concern and well-wishes from the football community for the prominent manager known for his leadership at the prestigious Scottish club.

Martin O'Neill's Career and Impact at Celtic

Martin O'Neill has been a pivotal figure in Celtic's recent history. Appointed as manager of the Glasgow-based club in 2000, O'Neill quickly earned respect for his tactical expertise and ability to motivate players. Under his leadership, Celtic won multiple Scottish Premier League titles and regularly competed in Europe, enhancing its reputation on the continental stage. Known for his calm demeanor and managerial acumen, O'Neill is regarded as one of the finest football minds of his era.

Details of O'Neill's Current Condition

News of Martin O'Neill's hospitalization following a minor surgery was confirmed by Celtic. Although no specific details about the procedure or the timeline for his recovery have been disclosed, the club referred to it as a 'small procedure.' Fans, analysts, and former players of Celtic have expressed their support and are hoping for his speedy return to the sidelines. O'Neill’s contributions to Celtic both during his tenure as manager and in broader roles within football make this update particularly significant for the football community.

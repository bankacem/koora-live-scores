Chelsea Sign Danny Welbeck from Brighton

8/1/2026

Chelsea have secured the services of Brighton's prolific forward Danny Welbeck, completing a move that sees the 27‑year‑old arrive at the club without a transfer fee disclosed. The English striker joins Chelsea under a long‑term contract, bolstering the side’s attack for a campaign that promises fresh competition for places.

Welbeck, who has spent the last two seasons with the Seagulls, left Brighton to take his career to a new challenge in London. The veteran forward is expected to bring experience, a strong aerial presence and a proven goal‑scoring instinct to the Premier League giants. Although the precise financial details were not released, the move was announced in a statement issued by Chelsea.

The goalkeeper of the stadium’s cheer and the passionate fans will soon be listening for the familiar name of Welbeck’s potential. For Brighton, the departure marks the end of an era for a striker who delivered crucial goals across multiple seasons.

This transfer now brings the two clubs' histories even closer: Welbeck had previously featured briefly for Chelsea in the early 2010s. According to BBC Sport, his return is seen as a positive step for both teams as they look toward the upcoming season. With confidence and experience coming from the striker, supporters anticipate an exciting shift in tactical dynamics on the pitch.

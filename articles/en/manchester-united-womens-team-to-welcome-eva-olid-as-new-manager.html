Manchester United Women's Team to Welcome Eva Olid as New Manager

8/4/2026 · By Koora Live

Manchester United are on the verge of confirming Eva Olid, the former Hearts boss, as the next manager of their women's team. This move comes as a replacement for Marc Skinner, signaling a new chapter for the club's growing push in women's football.

Eva Olid: A Proven Leader in Women's Football

Eva Olid's impending appointment by Manchester United Women's team reflects her growing reputation in football management. Olid is best known for her tenure at Hearts, a Scottish club where she showcased her ability to lead and develop teams in a competitive environment. Her experience in the Scottish Women’s Premier League (SWPL) has likely shaped her into a strong candidate for this position. Given Manchester United's ambitions to further solidify their presence in the Women's Super League (WSL), Olid's experience is well-suited for the challenges ahead.

Manchester United Women: A Rising Force in the WSL

The Manchester United Women's team has grown steadily since its formation in 2018. Competing in the FA Women's Super League, they have consistently aimed to challenge traditional powerhouses such as Chelsea and Arsenal. The departure of Marc Skinner opens the door for new leadership, with Olid expected to build upon the solid foundation forged by her predecessor. Skinner's tenure brought important progress, but Olid's arrival could signal a fresh approach that aligns with United's ambitions to secure trophies and establish themselves as a top contender.

Analysis

The decision to bring Eva Olid into the fold is likely viewed as a strategic move by Manchester United. Her track record with Hearts emphasizes her ability to manage teams in high-pressure scenarios while fostering talent in women's football. Analysts may interpret this appointment as United's intent to continue building a team capable of competing at the highest level, domestically and beyond. This move further illustrates the club’s commitment to establishing itself as a top competitor in the evolving landscape of women's football.

The appointment of Eva Olid marks an exciting new era for Manchester United Women as the club strives to enhance its status in the Women's Super League. Fans and analysts alike will eagerly await her impact in the upcoming campaigns.

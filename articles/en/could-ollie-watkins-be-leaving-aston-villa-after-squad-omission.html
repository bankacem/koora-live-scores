Could Ollie Watkins Be Leaving Aston Villa After Squad Omission?

8/23/2026 · By Koora Live

Aston Villa forward Ollie Watkins was notably absent from the match-day squad in the team's recent clash against Brighton. BBC Sport’s Match of the Day panel, featuring Gabby Logan, Danny Murphy, and Joe Hart, speculated whether this development signals a potential move away from Villa Park, with Al-Hilal reportedly being a possible destination.

Watkins' Aston Villa career in the spotlight

Ollie Watkins has been a central figure for Aston Villa since his arrival at the club in September 2020 from Brentford. Known for his pace, work rate, and an eye for goal, Watkins quickly became a fan favorite and an integral part of Villa's attacking setup. Under current manager Unai Emery, Villa have been rejuvenated, staking a claim for European competition qualification in recent times. Watkins has played a pivotal role in this revival, often leading the line with tenacity and providing a consistent source of goals for the team.

Speculation over a transfer to Al-Hilal

The possibility of Watkins departing for Al-Hilal, one of the most prominent clubs in the Saudi Pro League, has added intrigue to his sudden absence from Villa’s squad against Brighton. Al-Hilal, a club with a storied history in Saudi football, has recently become synonymous with eye-catching moves for European stars as part of the Saudi league's ambitious project to attract global footballing talent. Such speculation gains further weight given the financial muscle and allure that clubs in the region now possess. However, it is important to note that no formal statement or confirmation of any transfer talks has been provided at this stage.

Analysis

While Ollie Watkins’ omission from the match-day squad has raised eyebrows, it is worth noting that absences can be attributed to various reasons, including tactical decisions, fitness issues, or even precautionary measures. Despite the Match of the Day panel’s conjectures, there has been no concrete evidence to suggest that a departure is imminent. That said, the growing interest from clubs like Al-Hilal signals the increasing pull of the Saudi Pro League on top-level players. Aston Villa, given their ambitions under Unai Emery, would likely be reluctant to let their main forward leave without a strong contingency plan in place.

The speculation surrounding Ollie Watkins’ future with Aston Villa is sure to be a talking point in the coming days. Until official confirmation emerges, discussions will continue, but what remains clear is the player’s significant contribution to Villa’s recent success and his value to the team. Fans will be eagerly watching for updates on this developing story.

What does 'second leg' mean in a two-match tie?

A 'second leg' refers to the return match in a two-game, home-and-away tie. The combined result across both matches — often called the aggregate score — determines which team progresses. If the aggregate score is level after the second leg's regular time, competitions commonly move to extra time and, if still tied, a penalty shootout to produce a winner.

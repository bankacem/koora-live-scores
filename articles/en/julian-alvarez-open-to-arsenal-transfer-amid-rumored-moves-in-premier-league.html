Julian Alvarez Open to Arsenal Transfer Amid Rumored Moves in Premier League

8/23/2026 · By Koora Live

In the latest transfer gossip, Atletico Madrid forward Julian Alvarez is reportedly open to a move to Premier League giants Arsenal, according to BBC Sport. Meanwhile, Tottenham Hotspur are said to be optimistic about sealing a deal for Omar Marmoush, while Aston Villa's Ollie Watkins appears to have his sights set on a potential transfer to Al-Hilal. These developments highlight the dynamic nature of the winter transfer window as clubs look to bolster their squads.

Julian Alvarez Linked with Arsenal Move

Julian Alvarez, currently playing for Atletico Madrid, has reportedly expressed interest in joining Arsenal, one of England's top clubs. Although the 23-year-old Argentine forward has already established himself as a rising star in European football, this potential link to Arsenal puts him in line for a move to one of the Premier League's most historic clubs. Arsenal, based in North London, are known for their attacking style of play and are consistently on the hunt for high-caliber players to strengthen their squad. With the team performing strongly under Mikel Arteta in recent seasons, the addition of a versatile forward like Alvarez could offer the Gunners added depth and attacking prowess as they aim to solidify their position as title contenders.

Tottenham Eyeing Omar Marmoush Deal

While Arsenal may be focusing on Alvarez, their North London rivals Tottenham Hotspur appear to be closing in on a deal to sign Egyptian forward Omar Marmoush. The 24-year-old currently applies his trade with Eintracht Frankfurt in the German Bundesliga. Spurs, under the guidance of manager Ange Postecoglou, are actively looking to reinforce their attacking options this season after experiencing a transformative summer following the departure of Harry Kane. Marmoush, known for his speed and ability to operate both centrally and on the wing, could provide another dimension to Tottenham's forward line as they compete in the Premier League and domestic cup competitions.

Ollie Watkins Attracts Interest from Al-Hilal

Aston Villa striker Ollie Watkins has reportedly shown interest in a move to Al-Hilal, one of Saudi Arabia's most prominent football clubs. Watkins has been a crucial figure for Villa since joining from Brentford in 2020, regularly contributing goals and assists in the Premier League. Al-Hilal, a Saudi Pro League heavyweight, has made significant investments in attracting high-profile talent to their squad in recent transfer windows, and landing a player like Watkins could further signal their intent to grow as a global force in football. For Villa, losing Watkins would mean losing one of their most reliable forwards, making his potential exit a key storyline to follow in the transfer market.

Analysis

The latest transfer rumors underscore the ongoing competition among top clubs to enhance their squads during this critical point of the season. Arsenal’s reported interest in Julian Alvarez aligns with their strategy of targeting young, versatile attackers who can adapt to Mikel Arteta’s tactical philosophy. For Spurs, securing a talent like Omar Marmoush could offer valuable flexibility in their forward line, especially after the departure of a talisman like Harry Kane. Meanwhile, Ollie Watkins potentially seeking a move to Al-Hilal highlights the growing influence of the Saudi Pro League in luring top talent from Europe. It emphasizes the shifting dynamics of the global football market, with the Middle East becoming an increasingly attractive destination both financially and competitively for players in their prime.

With Julian Alvarez, Omar Marmoush, and Ollie Watkins making headlines in the transfer market, it remains to be seen how these rumors will unfold in the coming days. As the winter transfer window continues, fans can expect more twists and turns as clubs race to secure their key signings and reshape their squads for the future.

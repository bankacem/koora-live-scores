Curtis Jones transfer: Inter Milan close to £30m deal for Liverpool midfielder

8/19/2026 · By Koora Live

According to a BBC Sport report, Italian champions Inter Milan are closing in on a transfer agreement worth 35 million euros (£30m) for Liverpool midfielder Curtis Jones. The story places Jones at the centre of reported negotiations between two of Europe’s most recognised clubs, with Inter described as nearing a deal to bring the England-born midfielder to Serie A.

The essentials of the reported move

The core details emerging from BBC Sport are straightforward: Inter Milan, the current Italian champions, are reportedly on the verge of agreeing a 35m-euro deal — around £30m — to sign Curtis Jones from Liverpool. The player is identified as a midfielder and the move, as framed by the report, is nearing completion rather than being finalized. That phrasing leaves room for typical transfer-stage steps such as medicals, paperwork and final approvals.

Transfers between prominent clubs often include complex logistics beyond the headline fee. While the reported figure establishes the financial scale, clubs still need to complete contractual terms and player agreement processes before declaring a transfer confirmed. BBC Sport’s coverage underlines that this is a developing story and should be seen as an advanced negotiation rather than a concluded transaction.

Context on the clubs and the player involved

Inter Milan are introduced in the report as Italian champions, a status that reflects recent domestic success in Serie A and their profile as a major Italian club based in Milan. Liverpool, meanwhile, are one of England’s most decorated clubs and compete in the Premier League; the club has a long history of developing young talent through its academy pathway. Curtis Jones has been associated with Liverpool and is described in the report as a midfielder — a versatile role that can encompass a range of central and attacking responsibilities on the pitch.

From a broader perspective, moves between top-tier English and Italian clubs are common in the modern transfer market. Players transfer for a range of sporting and personal reasons — opportunities for more game time, a new tactical fit or a fresh environment. The BBC Sport report places Jones squarely in that landscape, with Inter positioned as the interested club and Liverpool as the selling side.

Analysis

If the reported negotiations complete, the move would represent a notable transfer between two high-profile European clubs. Analysts might interpret Inter's interest as a signal that they are reinforcing their midfield, possibly with an eye toward sustaining domestic performance and competing in continental competitions. For Liverpool, allowing a homegrown midfielder to depart could reflect squad management choices — balancing player development, opportunities for game time and broader squad composition. Any firm conclusions, however, should await official confirmations from the clubs involved, as BBC Sport presents the information as an ongoing deal rather than an announced transfer.

The BBC Sport report places Curtis Jones at the heart of serious transfer talks, with Inter Milan reportedly close to agreeing a 35m-euro deal with Liverpool. Supporters and observers should watch for formal announcements from the clubs to confirm any final terms and completion of the move.

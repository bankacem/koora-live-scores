Everton and Crystal Palace Discuss Dwight McNeil-Brennan Johnson Swap Deal

8/9/2026 · By Koora Live

According to BBC Sport, Everton and Crystal Palace are reportedly negotiating a swap deal that could see winger Dwight McNeil moving to Selhurst Park, while Welsh international Brennan Johnson heads to Goodison Park. This potential exchange would involve two players who have demonstrated considerable talent and promise at their respective clubs, raising interest among fans and pundits alike.

An In-Depth Look at Dwight McNeil’s Role at Everton

Dwight McNeil joined Everton in 2022 and has since become an integral player for the Merseyside club. Known for his dribbling ability and precise crosses, McNeil has provided width and creativity to the Toffees' attacking play. A product of Burnley's academy, McNeil enjoyed a successful stint at Turf Moor before transferring to Everton, where he has continued to hone his craft in the Premier League. While Everton have endured a challenging period in the league, including narrowly avoiding relegation last season, McNeil has been one of their standout performers. His potential move to Crystal Palace could bolster their wing options and lend greater depth to Roy Hodgson's side.

Brennan Johnson: A Rising Star at Crystal Palace

Brennan Johnson has made waves in English football as one of Wales' brightest prospects. The 22-year-old joined Crystal Palace and quickly established himself as a dynamic forward with keen instincts and an eye for goal. Johnson's versatility allows him to operate as a winger or a central attacker, making him an asset in various tactical setups. With Wales, he has earned international recognition for his performances, showing potential to become a long-term mainstay for his national team. If a move to Everton materializes, Johnson could add youthful energy and attacking flair to Sean Dyche’s side, aligning with the manager's preference for hardworking players who can adapt effectively to different phases of the game.

Analysis

This proposed swap deal brings together two players whose skill sets may serve their new teams well. From Everton’s perspective, securing Brennan Johnson would be a move toward injecting youthful energy into their attack. Sean Dyche’s tactical system, which demands work ethic and discipline, could see Johnson thrive in varying roles. For Crystal Palace, Dwight McNeil offers proven Premier League experience and the ability to deliver consistently from wide areas. While swap deals are less common in football compared to outright transfers, they can be an efficient means for clubs to address gaps in their squads without substantial financial outlays. Analysts might view this as a practical solution for two clubs seeking improvement without stretching their budgets excessively. However, negotiations like this often hinge on finer details, including player preferences and contract terms, which can make or break the final agreement.

The possible swap deal between Everton and Crystal Palace involving Dwight McNeil and Brennan Johnson is an intriguing prospect for both clubs. While the finer details remain to be resolved, the move could provide fresh opportunities for the players and address important tactical needs for the teams. Fans will undoubtedly watch the developments closely as negotiations unfold.

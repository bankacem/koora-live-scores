Decisive moments: Crawford’s back-heel and the own goal

The match turned on two key incidents that put Partick Thistle on the front foot. Robbie Crawford supplied an early intervention with a back-heel that contributed to the hosts taking the lead, and later in the contest a misdirected effort by Cammy MacPherson ended up in his own net, doubling Partick’s advantage. Those two moments were the clear match-winners in a fixture between sides widely regarded as promotion hopefuls.

Both goals underline how fine margins can determine league games: a creative finish or an unfortunate deflection can be enough to separate evenly matched teams. With the game played at Firhill, Partick Thistle enjoyed home surroundings as they converted those moments into a victory.

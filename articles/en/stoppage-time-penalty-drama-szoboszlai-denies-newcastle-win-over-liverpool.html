Stoppage-Time Penalty Drama: Szoboszlai Denies Newcastle Win Over Liverpool

8/24/2026 · By Koora Live

A dramatic late penalty converted by Dominik Szoboszlai saw Liverpool snatch a draw from Newcastle at St James' Park in their Premier League opener. The Hungarian midfielder's composure from the spot ensured Liverpool left Tyneside with a point after a tightly contested fixture.

Late Penalty Drama at St James' Park

In a pulsating encounter to kick off the Premier League season, Liverpool and Newcastle went head-to-head at St James' Park, delivering an edge-of-the-seat contest. With Newcastle seemingly on the verge of victory, it was Liverpool’s summer signing Dominik Szoboszlai who emerged as the hero. The Hungarian delivered for the visitors by converting a stoppage-time penalty to prevent Newcastle from securing all three points. The dramatic equalizer not only denied Newcastle a win but also set the tone for what could be a thrilling season ahead.

Liverpool’s New Signing Makes an Immediate Impact

Dominik Szoboszlai, a highly-touted addition to Jürgen Klopp’s squad, showcased why Liverpool acquired his services. The 22-year-old midfielder demonstrated immense composure under pressure, stepping up to take the decisive penalty deep into stoppage time. This crucial equalizer underscores Szoboszlai's potential to play a key role for Liverpool this season, as they aim to fight for domestic and European honors. For Newcastle, who were counting on securing a home victory to launch their campaign strongly, the result will feel more like two points dropped than one gained. Nonetheless, both teams showed moments of brilliance, highlighting their potential to challenge the league's elite in the upcoming season.

Cameroon win first Women's Africa Cup of Nations as Ngah Manga nets twice in 3-0 final

8/16/2026 · By Koora Live

Cameroon secured the Women's Africa Cup of Nations crown with a convincing 3-0 victory over Malawi, a result reported by BBC Sport. Forward Marie Ngah Manga played a decisive role, scoring twice as Cameroon lifted the tournament trophy for the first time in their history.

Match outcome and key contributions

The final ended with Cameroon defeating Malawi by three goals to nil, a scoreline that underlined a clear margin between the two sides on the day. According to BBC Sport, Marie Ngah Manga was central to Cameroon's success, finding the net twice in the match. The two goals from Ngah Manga accounted for the bulk of the scoring, and Cameroon added another goal to complete a 3-0 triumph.

Context and significance of the victory

The win represents a milestone for Cameroon, who have now claimed their first ever Women's Africa Cup of Nations title. The WAFCON is the premier continental competition for women's national teams in Africa and is organized under the Confederation of African Football (CAF). Winning this tournament is a major achievement for any nation on the continent and typically carries both sporting prestige and broader implications for the development of the women's game at home.

For Malawi, finishing as runners-up in this edition of the competition — as implied by the result — is a notable achievement in its own right, even though they came up short in the final. For Cameroon, the trophy will be celebrated by players, staff and supporters as a breakthrough moment that can boost visibility, investment and interest in women's football domestically.

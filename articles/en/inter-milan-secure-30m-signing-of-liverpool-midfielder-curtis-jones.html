Inter Milan secure £30m signing of Liverpool midfielder Curtis Jones

8/21/2026 · By Koora Live

In a major summer transfer move, Italian giants Inter Milan have completed a deal worth £30 million to bring Liverpool midfielder Curtis Jones to the San Siro for the 2026-27 campaign. The appointment signals Inter’s intent to strengthen their central midfield with a young player who has already proven himself on the biggest stages of English football.

BBC Sport reported that the transaction, which gives Inter a dynamic box-to-box presence, will add depth to a squad that has struggled to find a consistent midfield cog in a season of manager changes and injuries. For Liverpool, the move removes a prospect that holds a pricey resale value while freeing squad space for the likes of Ansu Fati and new signings in the ring of midfield rewiring.

The Transfer Deal Behind the Curtain

The £30 million transaction underscores the financial tightrope clubs must play in balancing talent acquisition and wage budgets. While Inter Milan’s finances have been historically constrained by the Gluckman austerity strategy, the club’s acquisition of Marcus Berg last season for €9.5 million was heralded as a prudent investment that paid dividends by securing a top spot in Serie A.

Inter’s move aligns with a trend of stable clubs in Serie A seeking young, dynamic midfielders to vie for domestic titles and Champions League qualification.

Curtis Jones: A Midfield Anchor

Curtis Jones has carved a reputation as a reliable engine in Liverpool’s midfield with over 100 competitive appearances for the club across all competitions. A former Aston Villa youth star who joined Liverpool's academy as a teenager, Jones has been praised for his compact shape, tactical discipline, and relentless work rate.

Standing at 1.84 meters, the Englishman is known for his vision and passing accuracy, which have helped break up play and initiate attacks. The 24‑year‑old's disciplinary record shows that he rarely finds himself in trouble, making him a dependable pick for managers in high‑pressure matches.

During the 2025‑26 season, Jones’s contributions saw Liverpool finish in the top four of the Premier League, securing UEFA Champions League qualification. His role, though sometimes criticised for lack of flair, was pivotal in midfield transitions.

Inter Milan’s Inter Milan team, under the stewardship of head coach Simone Inzaghi, has historically prioritized a disciplined midfield. The signings of players like Sam O'Connor, and the return of striker Giacomo Raspadori, demonstrate the club’s appetite for solid performers rather than flashy stars. Jones' addition fits within the club’s current tactical philosophy of high pressing, a system that demands stamina and tactical awareness from midfielders.

This transfer raises questions about squad balance in a league that struggles to unite young talent with seasoned veterans. Aligning a seasoned Premier League midfielder with Serie A’s physicality and tactical nuances presents both an opportunity and challenge for Inter’s integration strategy.

Analysis

The deal signals Inter Milan’s ambition to reassert themselves as a Serie A powerhouse while Liverpool continues to recalibrate their midfield after recent managerial changes. A £30 million outlay places the move among the most expensive signings in the club’s history, rivaling the purchase of Alex Telles for €32 m in 2022. These figures illustrate how midfielders, often considered unspectacular in transfer news, remain the bedrock of tactical strategy in modern football.

From a broader perspective, the transfer reflects a growing trend where top European leagues engage in cross‑border talent exchange. English midfielders moving to Serie A are not a novelty; it highlights the club’s appetite for professional resilience and technical ability that can adapt to the slower, more strategic nature of the Italian game. Analysts might view this transfer as a double‑edged sword: it gives Inter a proven performer while possibly accelerating a trend of young English players migrating to a less glamorous league for consistent first‑team action.

Inter Milan’s acquisition of Curtis Jones at £30 m underscores the club’s ambition to rejuvenate a critical midfield role while Liverpool chooses to reconfigure its squad for the future. As the 2026‑27 season approaches, all eyes will be on how the English midfielder acclimates to Italy’s tactical demands and how his presence reshapes Inter’s journey in Serie A and European competitions.

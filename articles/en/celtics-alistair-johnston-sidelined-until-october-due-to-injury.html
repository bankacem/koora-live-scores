Celtic's Alistair Johnston Sidelined Until October Due to Injury

8/24/2026 · By Koora Live

Celtic have been dealt a blow as manager Martin O'Neill confirmed that full-back Alistair Johnston is set to miss action until October. This update leaves the Scottish champions with a gap to fill in their defensive lineup as they prepare for crucial early-season fixtures.

Alistair Johnston Ruled Out

Celtic full-back Alistair Johnston has been ruled out until October, according to manager Martin O'Neill. This news comes as a significant setback for the Glasgow giants, who rely on Johnston's versatility and defensive acumen. Johnston, a Canadian international, joined the club as part of their efforts to bolster the squad's defensive stability. The player has quickly cemented a key role in the team with his energetic displays on the field.

Celtic's Plans for the Upcoming Season

With domestic and European campaigns approaching, Celtic will need to find ways to adapt to Johnston's absence. The Scottish Premiership is known for its physicality and fast-paced games, making the loss of a reliable player like Johnston a significant challenge. Additionally, Celtic have aspirations to make an impact in continental competitions, where squad depth and experience often play key roles. While manager Martin O'Neill has not disclosed further details about Johnston's condition, the club will likely look to other squad members or potentially the transfer market to ensure their defense remains solid.

Aston Villa Bolster Squad with Signings of Mbaye and Harwood-Bellis

9/1/2026 · By Koora Live

Aston Villa have made waves in the transfer market by securing two key additions to their squad. Senegalese winger Ibrahim Mbaye joins from Paris Saint-Germain, while English centre-back Taylor Harwood-Bellis arrives from Southampton. These acquisitions underline Villa's intent to strengthen their depth and quality as they aim for greater success this season.

Ibrahim Mbaye Brings Pace and Flair from PSG

Ibrahim Mbaye, the Senegalese winger, is set to add speed, creativity, and skill to Aston Villa's attack. Moving from Paris Saint-Germain, Mbaye arrives with a reputation as an exciting wide player who can unlock defenses and contribute both in terms of goals and assists. Wingers of Mbaye's profile, especially those trained in PSG's highly competitive environment, often bring a high technical level and the experience of playing alongside world-class teammates. For Villa, his signing could address their need for more dynamism on the flanks, providing additional options in attack. It also reaffirms the club's ambition to attract talented international players with proven pedigree.

Taylor Harwood-Bellis: A Solid Defensive Option

Joining from Southampton, Taylor Harwood-Bellis strengthens Aston Villa's defensive line. The English centre-back has garnered attention for his composure on the ball and defensive solidity. As a versatile defender, Harwood-Bellis can excel in various tactical systems, making him a valuable asset for manager Unai Emery. With Villa competing in a demanding Premier League campaign and potentially other competitions, squad depth in defense is crucial. By acquiring an English centre-back, the club also fits the quota for homegrown players, which is an added benefit under domestic league regulations.

Analysis

These signings demonstrate Aston Villa's ambition to solidify themselves as contenders in English football. By securing a winger of Mbaye’s caliber, the club is looking to inject energy and cutting-edge skills into their attacking setup. Meanwhile, the addition of Harwood-Bellis ensures defensive reliability, signaling a thoughtful approach in addressing gaps across the lineup. This blend of international flair and English defensive steel should provide a balanced contribution to their squad. Observers might view these signings as a clear indication of Villa's long-term plans to compete at a higher level domestically and, potentially, in European competitions.

Aston Villa's acquisition of Ibrahim Mbaye and Taylor Harwood-Bellis signals a clear intent to build a competitive and well-rounded squad. As the Premier League season unfolds, these players could play pivotal roles in helping Villa achieve their aspirations both domestically and on bigger stages.

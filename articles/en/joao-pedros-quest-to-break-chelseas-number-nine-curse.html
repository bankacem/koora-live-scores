Joao Pedro's Quest to Break Chelsea's Number Nine Curse

8/26/2026 · By Koora Live

Joao Pedro wasted no time making a statement this season, netting a goal within just 31 seconds of the opening whistle. Wearing Chelsea's iconic – and often dreaded – number nine shirt, the striker faces the challenge of living up to his stellar form from last season, when he notched 20 goals while wearing the number 20. But can he rise above the so-called 'curse' and deliver consistent performances for the Blues?

Joao Pedro's Stunning Start to the Season

Forward Joao Pedro marked the beginning of his campaign with an electrifying start, scoring his first goal in just over half a minute. Such an instant impact has set an optimistic tone for Chelsea fans hoping the young striker can bring a new era of attacking prowess to the club. Known for his agility, sharp movement, and eye for goal, Pedro was prolific last season, tallying 20 goals while donning the number 20 shirt. His new squad number, nine, however, carries with it a complicated history at Stamford Bridge.

The Infamous Number Nine Shirt at Chelsea

Chelsea's number nine jersey has acquired a reputation for being 'cursed' over the years. Iconic players like Fernando Torres, Gonzalo Higuaín, and Álvaro Morata have all struggled to live up to the expectations that come with this storied shirt. While each had their moments, the weight of the number seemed to hinder more than help. Chelsea fans and analysts alike have often speculated whether this is simply coincidental or a psychological burden placed on those who wear it. Joao Pedro stepping into this role brings hope that he can turn the page on this long-standing narrative.

Analysis

Joao Pedro's explosive start could be an indication of things to come. But while his instant goal may excite fans, the true challenge lies in sustaining this form throughout an entire season in one of the world's most competitive leagues. His transition from wearing the number 20 shirt – under which he flourished last season with 20 goals – to adopting the pressure-laden number nine will be closely watched. Should he succeed in breaking the so-called 'curse,' it could redefine Chelsea's approach to using their storied jersey and put to rest years of speculation. Analysts might argue that Pedro's ability to start strong demonstrates his confidence, which could be an essential asset in overcoming past narratives tied to his shirt number. However, consistency and resilience in the face of tough defenses will ultimately determine his level of success this season.

Joao Pedro's immediate impact this season has sparked hope among Chelsea fans that the number nine 'curse' could finally be broken. As he aims to replicate or even surpass last season's impressive tally of 20 goals, all eyes will remain firmly on the young striker, who could redefine what it means to wear this iconic – yet enigmatic – shirt.

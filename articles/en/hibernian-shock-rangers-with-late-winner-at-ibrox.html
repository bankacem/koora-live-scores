Hibernian Shock Rangers with Late Winner at Ibrox

8/10/2026 · By Koora Live

In a thrilling Scottish Premiership encounter, Hibernian secured a dramatic victory over Rangers at Ibrox, courtesy of Callum Wright's late goal. The result not only stunned the home crowd but also leaves new manager Derek McInnes still searching for his first win at the helm of Rangers.

Late Drama Seals Victory for Hibernian

Hibernian delivered a remarkable performance at one of the most iconic venues in Scottish football, Ibrox Stadium, emerging victorious against Rangers. Callum Wright's decisive late strike earned his side all three points, silencing the home supporters and igniting celebrations within the visiting fans' camp. The match was tightly contested, with Rangers looking to capitalize on their home advantage under the leadership of Derek McInnes, who recently took charge as manager. However, Wright’s intervention proved decisive, marking a memorable outing for Hibs.

Derek McInnes Era Begins with Frustration

The appointment of Derek McInnes, a seasoned manager in Scottish football, was seen as a move to steady the ship at Rangers and guide the club back to top form. Known for his managerial stints at Aberdeen and Kilmarnock, McInnes arrived at Ibrox with high expectations. However, his start has been anything but smooth, as Hibernian's victory prolongs his search for a debut win. Rangers, a club with a rich history including 55 top-flight league titles and countless domestic trophies, are under pressure to regain their dominance in Scotland. This defeat, particularly on home soil, adds another layer to the challenges McInnes faces in turning results around.

Analysis

This stunning result underlines Hibernian's resilience and ability to perform on the big stage. For Rangers, it raises questions about their current form and whether Derek McInnes can swiftly implement his vision. Analysts might view Wright's late winner as emblematic of the competitive nature of the Scottish Premiership, where smaller clubs can seize crucial moments to upset their more renowned rivals. Furthermore, Rangers' inability to manage the closing stages of the match could point to issues related to mental toughness or fitness, areas that McInnes will likely prioritize in the coming weeks.

Hibernian's late winner against Rangers at Ibrox is a moment of triumph for the Edinburgh-based club and a stark reminder for Rangers of the challenges ahead under Derek McInnes. The Scottish Premiership continues to deliver high drama, keeping fans eagerly anticipating what’s next in this fiercely contested league.

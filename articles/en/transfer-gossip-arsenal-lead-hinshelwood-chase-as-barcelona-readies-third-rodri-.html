Transfer Gossip: Arsenal Lead Hinshelwood Chase as Barcelona Readies Third Rodri Bid

BBC Sport reports a busy Saturday of transfer gossip with Arsenal reportedly leading the race for Jack Hinshelwood, Barcelona preparing a third bid for Rodri, and Manchester United and Tottenham joining Arsenal in discussions over Victor Osimhen. These developments, compiled by BBC Sport, highlight renewed activity among some of Europe's biggest clubs as they look to shape squads.

Arsenal reportedly lead the race for Jack Hinshelwood

According to BBC Sport, Arsenal are at the front of the queue for Jack Hinshelwood. The brief report does not provide further transactional details but names Hinshelwood as a target with Arsenal leading interest.

Barcelona's pursuit of Rodri and Premier League interest in Victor Osimhen

BBC Sport says Barcelona are set to make a third bid for Rodri, indicating the Spanish club's continued interest in the Manchester City midfielder. Rodri is widely known for his role in midfield at Manchester City, where he has been central to their ball-retention and defensive shielding. Multiple bids suggest Barcelona view him as a strategic target to strengthen their midfield profile.

Separately, BBC Sport reports that Manchester United and Tottenham have joined Arsenal in discussing Victor Osimhen. Osimhen is recognised across Europe as a leading striker in Serie A and for his national team. Interest from several Premier League clubs signals how coveted top forwards remain in the market, with teams often exploring multiple avenues to boost goal-scoring capacity. These linked moves — a high-profile midfielder attracting renewed bids and several big clubs monitoring a top striker — are indicative of how competitive recruitment can be at the elite level.

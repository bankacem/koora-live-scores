Gustavo Hamer Rejoins Coventry City: Long-Term Deal Confirmed by BBC Sport

8/11/2026 · By Koora Live

Coventry City have re-signed midfielder Gustavo Hamer from Sheffield United on what the club described as a "long-term" contract, with the transfer carried out for an undisclosed fee, BBC Sport reports. The move sees Hamer return to the club where he previously played, strengthening Coventry's midfield options.

What the transfer entails

According to BBC Sport, Gustavo Hamer has moved from Sheffield United back to Coventry City on a long-term contract. The fee involved in the transaction has not been made public. Re-signing a player in this manner typically indicates the acquiring club views the player as part of its medium- to long-term plans, and Coventry will be hoping Hamer can contribute consistently in the seasons ahead.

The term "long-term" in transfer announcements generally refers to a multi-year agreement, though the exact duration and any contract clauses have not been disclosed in this case. An undisclosed fee means the clubs have chosen not to reveal financial details to the public; this is a common practice in football transfers.

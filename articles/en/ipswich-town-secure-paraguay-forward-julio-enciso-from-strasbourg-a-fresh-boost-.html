Ipswich Town secure Paraguay forward Julio Enciso from Strasbourg – a fresh boost to their attack

8/12/2026 · By Koora Live

Ipswich Town have announced that they have reached an agreement with Bundesliga side Strasbourg to sign Paraguayan forward Julio Enciso. The deal, confirmed by the club’s official channels and reported by BBC Sport, will see the 22‑year‑old arrive at Portman Road ahead of the new campaign. The transfer underlines the club’s ongoing build‑up behind manager Mick McCarthy’s plans to strengthen the frontline in preparation for next season’s League One challenge.

Transfer details and implications

While the exact fee remains undisclosed, Ipswich Town’s confirmation that they have secured Enciso’s services signals a sign of confidence in the club’s next‑generation squad. The deal follows a period of strategic recruitment aimed at adding pace, width and creativity to a front line that struggled to convert chances last season. With the signing announced in the run‑up to the new season, the players now have the opportunity to integrate during pre‑season training and on the official competitive front. The acquisition will also be a strong talking point for the club’s supporters, who have been eager for fresh attacking options following a dip in goal‑scoring output.

Enciso – A versatile attacking asset

Julio Enciso’s background includes experience in European football and a reputation for being a dynamic forward capable of operating across the front three. Prior to joining Ipswich, he had spent the 2023‑24 season with Strasbourg in Ligue 1, where his contributions included work‑rate, link‑up play and a keen eye for goal. The move to the English club offers him an environment of a higher tempo and physicality that could suit his growth as an attacking player. The 22‑year‑old is known for his determination on the pitch and his ability to adapt quickly, qualities that are likely to allow him to find a place in the squad’s rotation under the new management.

Analysis

From a strategic viewpoint, Ipswich’s decision to bring in Enciso appears guided by the need to add depth and versatility to an attack that dimmed in the previous campaign. By tapping into a forward who has performed in a top European league, the club may increase its competitive edge in a league that offers a high level of competition for cricket of promotion and relegation. Analysts will likely view the transfer as a positive step towards reinforcing a squad that aims to challenge for a higher position in the third tier of English football while maintaining a low‑cost, high‑potential investment.

The arrival of Julio Enciso underscores Ipswich Town’s resolve to shatter a defensive and offensive lull, positioning the club for a robust campaign. His experience across European competition should bring an additional layer of creativity to the Doonhamers’ attack, enhancing their prospects for a strong finish.

Hallgrimsson blames Israel for intimidation after spitting row in Nations League draw

10/5/2026 · By Koora Live

In a confrontation that has tipped the Nations League into a media storm, Republic of Ireland head coach Heimir Hallgrimsson accused Israeli officials of attempting to intimidate his squad during the group draw for last Sunday’s round. The claim came amid a spitting incident that sparked angry statements from Irish officials and put a spotlight on diplomatic tensions that could ripple into match day. BBC Sport has reported that Hallgrimsson’s reaction explains why Ireland now stands on the sidelines of a growing controversy that is unfolding as the tournament gears up across Europe.

A tense opening act at the Nations League draw

The Nations League, launched in 2018 by UEFA, is structured to give each national team a degree of competitive balance while providing a path to European Championships qualification. The draw for the latest edition was held in Dublin, with teams from Scotland, Wales, Israel, and several other nations slated to compete in their respective groups.. During the televised event, the Irish delegation was approached by Israeli staff members who, according to Hallgrimsson, launched a series of overt statements that the coach described as an attempt to "intimidate" his players. The tension escalated when an alleged spitting altercation erupted in the area surrounding the venue, prompting the Irish team to raise a formal complaint. The claim underlined the growing sense that the political climate in certain regions may be affecting the sporting arena. The incident has already caused ruptures within the Irish Football Association, prompting an inquiry into the conduct of the Israeli delegation. While no details about the identities of those responsible or the legal framework of the allegations have been made public, the incident could have implications for the mechanics of the Nations League fixtures.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who is Heimir Hallgrimsson?

Heimir Hallgrimsson is a former Icelandic international footballer and currently the head coach of the Republic of Ireland national team. His coaching experience includes spells at clubs such as Leeds United, as well as leading the Irish national side for senior fixtures.

What is the UEFA Nations League?

The Nations League is an annual competition created by UEFA in 2018 to provide competitive matches for national teams within Europe while eliminating the need for friendly games. It is divided into four tiers with promotion and relegation mechanisms to keep teams motivated throughout the year.

What was the alleged incident during the draw?

According to the BBC Sport report, during the draw for the latest Nations League edition, the Irish team claims that Israeli officials tried to intimidate them, a situation further inflamed by a spitting incident that led to formal complaints from the Irish side.

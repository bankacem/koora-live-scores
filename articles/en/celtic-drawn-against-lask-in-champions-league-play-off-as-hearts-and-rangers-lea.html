What the draw means for Celtic

Celtic being paired with LASK pits the Glasgow side against the champions of Austria in a high-stakes play-off tie. The Champions League play-off round is the final phase before the group stage, so the outcome will determine which club progresses to Europe's premier club competition. LASK earned the status of Austrian champions, making them a credible opponent with domestic momentum.

For Celtic, this draw will be viewed through the prism of European qualification ambitions. A successful play-off would secure a lucrative and prestigious place in the Champions League group stage — an outcome that brings competitive and financial benefits. Conversely, elimination at this stage typically leads clubs to continue their continental season in UEFA’s secondary competitions, depending on the competition’s pathway rules.

What is the Champions League play-off round format?

The play-off round is the final qualifying stage before the Champions League group phase and is typically contested over two legs (home and away). The winner on aggregate advances to the group stage; if teams are level after both legs, the tie is usually decided by extra time and, if required, a penalty shootout.

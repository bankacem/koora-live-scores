Hull City Stun Manchester United: Jakirovic Praises Tactical Discipline

8/22/2026 · By Koora Live

Hull City head coach Sergej Jakirovic lauded his team's tactical execution after they secured an impressive 2-0 victory over Manchester United on the opening weekend of the Premier League. The win marks a strong start to Hull's campaign, and Jakirovic believes it validates their ability to compete at the highest level.

A Statement Victory for Hull City

Hull City delivered a commanding performance against Manchester United to claim a 2-0 victory, sending shockwaves across the Premier League. On the opening weekend of the new season, many expected the Red Devils—one of English football's most storied and decorated clubs—to assert themselves against the Tigers, a team that has not always been a fixture in the top flight. However, Hull showcased a masterclass in tactical discipline and determination under Jakirovic's management, securing three vital points in significant style.

Jakirovic's Tactical Approach Pays Off

Sergej Jakirovic, who took the helm at Hull City earlier this year, was quick to commend his squad's adherence to the game plan. His post-match remarks reflected his satisfaction with the team’s ability to implement the strategy designed to neutralize Manchester United's attacking prowess. The result not only demonstrated Hull's preparedness but also highlighted Jakirovic's credentials as a tactically astute manager. Under his guidance, the team appears poised to defy expectations and cause trouble for more illustrious opponents throughout the season.

Analysis

This result could serve as a major turning point for Hull City as they establish themselves in a grueling Premier League campaign. Beating a club with the pedigree and resources of Manchester United no doubt sends a resounding statement to rivals that the Tigers cannot be underestimated this season. Jakirovic's remarks about following a tactical plan underscore the key to their success: discipline and preparation. For Manchester United, the defeat raises questions about their consistency and their ability to handle lower-ranked opponents, a problem that has haunted the Red Devils in recent years. Analysts may surmise that both teams have much to work on, albeit for very different reasons.

Hull City's opening-day triumph against one of English football's traditional powerhouses is bound to bolster their confidence as they embark on their Premier League journey. Under Jakirovic's tactical guidance, the Tigers have shown that they mean business this season, and fans will undoubtedly hope this result is a sign of things to come.

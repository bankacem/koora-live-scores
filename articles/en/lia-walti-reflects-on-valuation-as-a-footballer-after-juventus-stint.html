Lia Walti Reflects on Valuation as a Footballer After Juventus Stint

8/21/2026 · By Koora Live

Brighton midfielder Lia Walti has opened up about her time at Juventus, admitting that she 'missed the feeling of being valued as a footballer' during her stay in Italy. Speaking candidly, Walti revealed her motivation to return to the Women’s Super League (WSL), highlighting her craving for the competitive environment and recognition that the league offers.

Walti's Challenging Spell at Juventus

Lia Walti, now a key player for Brighton in the Women’s Super League, has shared revealing insights into her brief period at Juventus. While Juventus are a dominant force in the Italian women's football scene, Walti explained that she felt undervalued during her time at the club. This sentiment ultimately catalyzed her decision to return to the WSL, a league renowned for its competitiveness and its emphasis on fostering player development and recognition.

A Return to the WSL

The Women’s Super League is widely regarded as one of the premier stages for women’s football, boasting strong teams, world-class talents, and a growing global audience. Walti’s decision to leave Juventus and rejoin the WSL speaks volumes about the league's capacity to attract and retain top players. Brighton has seen a transformation in recent years, working steadily to establish themselves among England’s most consistent clubs. For Walti, this return is not just about rediscovering her passion but also seeking an environment where her contribution to the game is fully recognized.

Analysis

Walti’s admission touches on an intrinsic aspect of professional football—how the culture of a league or club can impact a player’s sense of worth and motivation. Her remarks indicate that, despite Juventus' reputation as a serial winner in Serie A Femminile, the environment may not have provided her with the validation or opportunities she sought. Analysts might view her return to the WSL as a testament to the league’s growing stature, due in part to its professional approach, investment in talent, and ability to cater to the needs of players both on and off the pitch. Moreover, her comments may spark further conversations about how clubs across Europe can strive for more inclusive and supportive environments to retain top talent.

Lia Walti’s reflections on her career underline the importance of environment and recognition in professional athletes’ lives. Her move back to the Women’s Super League not only benefits Brighton but also highlights why the WSL is increasingly viewed as a top destination for talent.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who is Lia Walti?

Lia Walti is a Swiss professional footballer currently playing as a midfielder for Brighton in the Women’s Super League. She has previously played for clubs like Juventus.

Why did Lia Walti return to the WSL?

Lia Walti stated that she missed feeling valued as a footballer during her time at Juventus and wished to return to the competitive and supportive atmosphere of the Women’s Super League.

What is the Women’s Super League (WSL)?

The Women’s Super League is England’s top-tier women’s football league, known for its high level of competition, world-class players, and growing global popularity.

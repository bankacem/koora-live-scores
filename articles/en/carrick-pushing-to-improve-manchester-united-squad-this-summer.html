Carrick pushing to improve Manchester United squad this summer

8/13/2026 · By Koora Live

BBC Sport reports that Michael Carrick is pressing ahead with plans to strengthen the Manchester United squad during the upcoming summer window. Ahead of what will be his first full Premier League campaign in charge at Old Trafford, Carrick has said he feels the squad is making good progress.

Where Carrick stands as he prepares for a full season in charge

The headline from BBC Sport makes clear that Carrick is entering a significant phase of his managerial tenure: his first full Premier League campaign at Old Trafford. That transition from interim or caretaker responsibilities to a full season in charge often brings fresh scrutiny and a need to stamp a long-term identity on the team. Managers at clubs of Manchester United’s size commonly use the summer window to align the squad with their tactical ideas and to address gaps revealed over a campaign.

Summer transfer imperatives and the broader context

Improving a squad in the summer involves balancing short-term fixes with longer-term planning. A manager like Carrick will generally consider positional needs, depth to cope with multiple competitions, and the blend of youth and experience. The Premier League’s competitive nature, combined with financial regulations and squad registration rules, means recruitment has to be strategic rather than reactive. Clubs also weigh the development of academy players and integration into the first team when planning summer moves.

Analysis

Carrick’s statement to BBC Sport that the squad is "making good progress" can be interpreted in several ways. It suggests internal development — perhaps improvements in training, cohesion or tactical understanding — which can reduce the urgency of wholesale change. At the same time, his explicit intent to push to improve the squad this summer signals that he still sees room for additions to help translate progress into consistent results. Observers might view a balanced approach as prudent: backing the current core while selectively strengthening areas that fit his long-term plan.

Carrick’s intention to push for summer reinforcements, alongside his view that the squad is progressing, frames a familiar off-season narrative: build on internal improvements while selectively investing to meet the challenges of a full Premier League campaign. BBC Sport’s report underlines that the upcoming window will be an important test of his long-term plan at Old Trafford.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who is leading the summer recruitment drive mentioned by BBC Sport?

BBC Sport identifies Michael Carrick as the manager pushing to improve the squad this summer. As the coach in charge at Old Trafford, it is typical for a manager to outline priorities and work with the club’s recruitment team during the transfer window.

What does it mean that this is Carrick’s first full Premier League campaign in charge?

The phrase indicates that Carrick will be managing a complete Premier League season from start to finish at Old Trafford for the first time. That full-season responsibility often involves setting tactical direction, squad building over a transfer window, and preparing for the sustained demands of the league.

Why do managers focus on the summer window to improve their squads?

The summer transfer window is the primary period when clubs can make longer-term changes to their squad. It allows managers to recruit players, adjust the depth and balance of the team, and prepare for the upcoming season’s tactical demands. Additionally, it provides more time for new signings to settle in ahead of competitive fixtures.

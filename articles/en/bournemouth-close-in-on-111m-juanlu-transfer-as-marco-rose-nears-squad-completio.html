Bournemouth close in on £11.1m Juanlu transfer as Marco Rose nears squad completion

8/2/2026 · By Koora Live

Bournemouth are reported to be finalising an £11.1m move for Sevilla right-back Juanlu, according to BBC Sport. The move comes as new manager Marco Rose approaches the end of his summer squad building, with this signing described as part of the club's recruitment drive.

BBC Sport's coverage states that the transfer is being finalised and identifies Juanlu as a right-back currently on Sevilla's books. The report frames the move within the larger picture of Marco Rose preparing his squad for the coming campaign.

What the Juanlu deal would add to Bournemouth

Bringing in a right-back from Sevilla would add depth to Bournemouth’s options at full-back, a position that often requires a blend of defensive solidity and attacking support. Sevilla are a well-regarded Spanish club, and a player arriving from La Liga would be expected to bring experience of a technically and tactically demanding environment. The reported fee of £11.1m suggests Bournemouth see the signing as a meaningful investment rather than a short-term stopgap.

For a Premier League side like Bournemouth, steady recruitment in key positions is commonly viewed as essential to sustaining competitiveness. If the Juanlu move is completed, it would reflect a targeted signing aimed at strengthening a specific area of the squad rather than a wholesale rebuild.

Marco Rose and the broader recruitment picture

Marco Rose is identified in the BBC Sport report as Bournemouth’s new manager, and the story frames the Juanlu move within the context of his squad nearing completion. Managers arriving at new clubs typically seek to imprint their tactical preferences and fill positions that fit their system; right-back is frequently a role that can be crucial to how a manager wants to build play from wide areas.

For Bournemouth, adding a right-back from a club like Sevilla could be part of Rose’s plan to balance defensive duties with forward thrusts from the flanks, although the precise tactical fit will depend on Rose’s chosen formation and how he deploys his full-backs.

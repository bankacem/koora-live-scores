Newcastle United Manager Jaissle Speaks on Bruno Guimaraes' Pre-Decided Exit

8/7/2026 · By Koora Live

Newcastle United manager Matthias Jaissle has revealed that the decision regarding Bruno Guimaraes’ future at the club was already finalized before his tenure began. This surprising disclosure sheds light on the departure of one of Newcastle’s key midfielders, who also served as the team's captain.

Bruno Guimaraes: A Pillar of Newcastle's Midfield

Bruno Guimaraes had emerged as a vital figure for Newcastle United, both on and off the pitch. Since joining the club, the Brazilian midfielder showcased immense technical ability, vision, and leadership qualities. His contributions were instrumental in Newcastle’s recent resurgence under the ambitious ownership by the Saudi Public Investment Fund. Frequently occupying a defensive midfield role, Guimaraes anchored the team’s efforts during their charge toward qualifying for European competitions and performed impressively in the highly competitive Premier League.

Jaissle Comments on Pre-Decided Departure

Matthias Jaissle, newly appointed as Newcastle’s manager, disclosed that the decision regarding Guimaraes’ departure was made prior to his arrival. Jaissle pointed out that his first interactions with the club coincided with a transitional period, with the captain's future already set in motion. Although full details about where Guimaraes will head next remain unclear, the announcement marks an important moment in Newcastle’s ongoing evolution. Transitions of key players often require careful management, especially as clubs work to rebalance their squad dynamics in response.

Matthias Jaissle Secures First Premier League Win with Newcastle Against Spurs

8/29/2026 · By Koora Live

Matthias Jaissle has marked a significant milestone in his managerial career by clinching his first Premier League victory. Newcastle United registered a comprehensive 2-0 win over Tottenham Hotspur, with second-half goals from Anthony Elanga and Yoanne Wissa sealing the result in London. It was a frustrating performance for Spurs, who struggled to find momentum in front of their home fans.

Newcastle Shine in Jaissle's Breakthrough Moment

In a highly anticipated clash at Tottenham Hotspur Stadium, Newcastle United delivered a clinical performance under their new manager, Matthias Jaissle. With the match locked at 0-0 at halftime, Newcastle found their breakthrough in the second half. Goals from Anthony Elanga, the former Manchester United winger, and Yoanne Wissa, known for his finishing prowess, dismantled a sluggish Tottenham side. Jaissle will undoubtedly cherish this moment as a landmark in his fledgling Premier League journey.

Tottenham Struggle Amid Lacklustre Performance

For Tottenham Hotspur, the evening was one to forget. Despite playing at home, Spurs failed to impose themselves on the game and struggled to create clear-cut chances. Their inability to break down Newcastle's resolute defense was evident, leaving their supporters frustrated. Tottenham, a club with a rich history dating back to their founding in 1882 and known for iconic players and managers, now faces pressure to respond quickly under the microscope of an unforgiving Premier League schedule.

Analysis

Matthias Jaissle's maiden Premier League victory is a significant achievement, particularly given the club's ambitions and the competitive nature of English football. With the season still unfolding, there is time for Spurs to regroup, but fans will demand a swift response to avoid a slip in form. These early-season results often set the tone, and for Newcastle, this could be the springboard they need to challenge for European spots once again.

Matthias Jaissle's first Premier League victory with Newcastle United is a moment to remember for the German manager and the club.

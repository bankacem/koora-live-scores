The Top 10 Greatest Managers in Premier League History

8/25/2026 · By Koora Live

The Premier League has been graced by countless iconic managers since its inception in 1992. Out of the 13 managers who have lifted the coveted title, only a select few are recognized as the very best. BBC Sport's recent ranking of the top 10 managers in Premier League history takes on the ambitious task of evaluating those who have left an indelible mark on English football.

The Legacy of Premier League Managers

Since its formation in 1992, the Premier League has been synonymous with incredible talent both on and off the pitch. While players such as Thierry Henry, Alan Shearer, and Frank Lampard have dazzled fans with their performances, the managers orchestrating these teams often shape the league in lasting ways. They bring varied tactical systems, establish club cultures, and leave lasting memories through their triumphs and rivalries. Among the thousands who have graced the dugout, only 13 managers have secured the Premier League crown, highlighting the difficulty of this achievement in perhaps the most competitive league in the world.

What Defines the Best Managers?

Defining greatness among Premier League managers is no easy task. Is it based purely on titles won, or does tactical innovation, longevity, and the ability to develop players also play a role? Certain figures, like Sir Alex Ferguson, dominated multiple eras, combining tactical flexibility with an unrelenting appetite for success. Others, such as Arsène Wenger, not only brought silverware to their clubs but also revolutionized the English game with their philosophies. Meanwhile, more recent names like Pep Guardiola have set new benchmarks for attacking football. A truly great manager balances domestic dominance with European ambition, all while commanding the respect of players and fans alike.

Analysis

The ranking by BBC Sport is bound to spark debate among fans, pundits, and players alike. While certain inclusions, such as serial title winners, may seem obvious, there’s always room for debate regarding the exact order or who narrowly missed out. Analysts might view this ranking as an effort to highlight not only winners but also those managers whose influence goes beyond lifting trophies. Consideration of long-term impacts on clubs, such as Wenger's transformation of Arsenal's playing style and facilities or Klopp's revival of Liverpool as a European powerhouse, showcases how 'greatness' is a multifaceted concept. Moreover, tactical innovation and the ability to succeed in varied eras often elevate managers into such lists.

BBC Sport’s ranking of the best Premier League managers serves as a reminder of the immense influence these figures have had on the league’s history. While discussions on who truly deserves a spot in the top 10 will continue, it is clear that the Premier League owes much of its global success to the tactical genius and leadership of its greatest managers.

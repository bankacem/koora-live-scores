Shakhtar Eye Stamford Bridge for Champions League Home Fixtures

8/14/2026 · By Koora Live

BBC Sport has reported that Ukrainian side Shakhtar Donetsk have asked to stage their Champions League home matches at Chelsea’s Stamford Bridge this season. The request, as described by the broadcaster, would see the Ukrainian club nominate a London venue normally used by the Premier League side to host their European fixtures.

Stamford Bridge is the long-standing home ground of Chelsea FC in west London, and fixtures being moved to neutral or alternative stadia is a recognised option for clubs facing extraordinary circumstances. The report makes clear that Shakhtar have expressed a desire to use that specific venue for their continental home games this campaign.

What Shakhtar's request actually says

According to BBC Sport, Shakhtar Donetsk want to play their Champions League home ties at Stamford Bridge this season. That is the extent of the confirmed information: the club has reportedly sought to nominate Chelsea’s stadium as the location for the games they would nominally host in the competition.

Beyond the core report, there are no further confirmed details in the BBC Sport piece about any agreement, how many matches would be affected, or whether Chelsea or governing bodies have been consulted. The description is a statement of intent by Shakhtar rather than a confirmation that fixtures will be switched.

Why clubs choose alternative venues and what that involves

It is not unusual for clubs to request a change of venue for international or continental matches when circumstances make their usual ground unsuitable. Reasons can include safety and security concerns, logistical challenges, stadium licensing requirements, or the need to ensure an adequate matchday infrastructure for high-profile UEFA fixtures.

Any such move, in general terms, touches on multiple practicalities: the chosen stadium must meet UEFA technical and safety standards for Champions League games; ticketing and allocation arrangements must be reconciled between the host club and visitors; and local authorities and police need to be involved to ensure public order. These are standard considerations when a club seeks to stage European matches away from its customary home ground.

Analysis

If approved, staging Champions League home fixtures at Stamford Bridge would offer Shakhtar practical advantages such as access to a large, UEFA-tested stadium and the logistical support typical of top-tier European venues. For displaced or affected clubs, selecting an established ground in a major city can help meet competition requirements and provide a consistent environment for players and officials.

At the same time, there are drawbacks and sensitivities. Playing 'home' matches away from a club’s supporter base can dilute the traditional home advantage and complicate fan access. Any suggested move would need to satisfy regulatory checks and demonstrate that the interests of supporters, competition integrity and safety are properly managed. As BBC Sport notes, the current story is a request rather than a completed arrangement, and multiple stakeholders would have to be engaged before fixtures could be relocated.

BBC Sport's report that Shakhtar Donetsk want to play Champions League home games at Stamford Bridge this season outlines a clear request but stops short of stating any confirmed arrangement. Any move of this kind would follow a process involving regulatory checks, host-club cooperation and logistical planning before becoming a reality.

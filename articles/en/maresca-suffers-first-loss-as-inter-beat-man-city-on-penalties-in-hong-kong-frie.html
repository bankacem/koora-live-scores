8/2/2026 · By Koora Live

BBC Sport reports that Enzo Maresca suffered his first match in charge of Manchester City after a 1-1 friendly draw with Inter Milan in Hong Kong, with the Italian side prevailing on penalties. Highlights of the encounter have been made available by BBC Sport.

Match summary: a 1-1 friendly that went to penalties

The scheduled friendly in Hong Kong finished level at 1-1 after regular time, and the outcome was ultimately decided by a penalty shootout in which Inter Milan emerged victorious. As reported by BBC Sport, the result marked Enzo Maresca's first match in charge to end in defeat. Friendly fixtures often use penalty shootouts to produce a definitive result for supporters and broadcasters, and this game followed that practice.

A penalty-shootout loss in a friendly does not carry the same competitive weight as a defeat in a domestic league or continental competition, but it can still offer useful takeaways for a new manager and coaching staff. Observers may view this game as an early opportunity for Enzo Maresca to assess his squad under match conditions and identify areas for adjustment ahead of competitive fixtures.

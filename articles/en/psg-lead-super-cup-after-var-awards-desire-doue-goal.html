PSG Lead Super Cup After VAR Awards Desire Doue Goal

8/13/2026 · By Koora Live

Paris Saint-Germain took the lead in a tightly contested Super Cup clash against Aston Villa thanks to Desire Doue's second-half goal. Initially flagged offside, the decision was overturned following a VAR review, proving decisive in this high-stakes encounter.

Doue's Goal Turns the Tide with VAR Intervention

Desire Doue's crucial strike in the second half set the stage for PSG to edge ahead in their Super Cup showdown against Aston Villa. Initially disallowed due to an offside call by the on-field officials, the goal underwent VAR scrutiny and was subsequently awarded. This rapid turn of events showcased how modern technology continues to influence decision-making in football. Making use of Video Assistant Referee (VAR) protocol has often sparked debates among fans, but in this case, it ensured the correct decision prevailed, boosting PSG's chances in this pivotal match.

Super Cup Showdown: A Clash of Two Elite Clubs

The Super Cup brings together champions or top-performing sides, creating thrilling encounters that often spark global interest. For PSG, decorated with multiple Ligue 1 titles and a lineup of international stars, this match is an opportunity to reinforce their dominance. Aston Villa, on the other hand, boasts storied traditions in English football, including their illustrious European Cup triumph in 1982. Meetings between clubs of this stature are often closely fought, with moments like Doue’s VAR-assisted goal proving decisive. Both teams entered this game aiming to add another prestige-filled trophy to their collection.

Analysis

The awarding of Doue's goal following VAR review highlights both the strengths and challenges of technology in football.

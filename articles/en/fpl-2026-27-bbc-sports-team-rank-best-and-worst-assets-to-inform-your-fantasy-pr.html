FPL 2026-27: BBC Sport's team rank best and worst assets to inform your Fantasy Premier League picks

8/14/2026 · By Koora Live

BBC Sport has published a new piece in which its team of Fantasy Premier League (FPL) experts assess the best and worst assets across all 20 Premier League clubs for the 2026-27 season. The feature offers a club-by-club ranking designed to help managers decide who to select, avoid or monitor as they build squads and plan transfers.

What BBC Sport’s ranking covers and why it matters

The BBC Sport article gathers expert judgement on the most and least promising FPL assets from each of the 20 Premier League teams for 2026-27. Rankings like this condense informed opinion about potential season-long value into an accessible format, highlighting players worth targeting and those who may carry unnecessary risk. For many managers, expert lists serve as a starting point for research rather than the sole basis for decisions. They can flag unexpected options, remind managers about rotation risks, and spotlight roles such as primary set-piece takers or first-choice strikers that generally translate into consistent FPL returns.

Because the piece comes from a respected outlet, managers may use it alongside statistics, fixture difficulty, and club news. The value of any ranking is in the context it provides: understanding why an expert values a player (for example, minutes, role, or historic returns) helps managers decide how the recommendation fits into their own squad construction and transfer plans.

How to interpret expert rankings when planning transfers and drafts

When integrating these club-by-club rankings into your FPL approach, treat them as qualitative guidance rather than absolute truth. Experts typically weigh minutes, attacking involvement, durability, set-piece responsibility, and underlying metrics such as expected goals and assists when valuing assets. Managers should cross-check those indicators with fixture schedules, injury updates, and price movements in the game.

Practical steps include using the ranking to build a shortlist of players to monitor, tracking preseason reports and early-season line-ups to confirm minutes, and avoiding knee-jerk moves solely because a player appears on either a best or worst list. For drafts and early-week transfers, combine the ranking with your personal risk tolerance: premium assets can anchor a squad but reduce flexibility, while budget enablers can free funds to invest elsewhere.

Analysis

Expert rankings such as the one published by BBC Sport are valuable because they translate a season’s worth of positional and tactical knowledge into actionable lists for FPL managers. That said, this is an opinion-driven resource: sensible managers will use it to generate shortlists and hypotheses rather than as a definitive shopping list. Combining these expert views with objective data—fixture difficulty, preseason form, and early-season minutes—will yield the most defensible transfer decisions. Analysts might also view such rankings as a tool to spot market inefficiencies: if a highly recommended player starts the season overlooked in FPL ownership, that can create early differential opportunities.

BBC Sport’s expert-led club rankings give FPL managers a structured way to consider who to pick and who to avoid for the 2026-27 season. Use the analysis as a starting point, cross-reference with data and team news, and integrate the advice into a flexible game plan that responds to what unfolds in the opening weeks.

Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly did BBC Sport publish about FPL for 2026-27?

BBC Sport's team of FPL experts released a club-by-club ranking that identifies the best and worst Fantasy Premier League assets from each of the 20 Premier League teams for the 2026-27 season. The piece is an expert opinion feature intended to guide managers.

Should I transfer in every player listed as a 'best pick'?

No. Rankings reflect expert opinion and should be used alongside other information such as minutes, fixtures, injury status and your squad balance. Treat the list as a source of candidates to monitor, then confirm playing time and role before committing transfers.

How can I use the rankings to find useful differentials?

Use the BBC Sport list to identify recommended players who currently have lower ownership in FPL. When an expert highlights a player whose broader ownership is low, that player could become a differential if they secure minutes and deliver early returns. Always verify with recent team news and matchday line-ups.

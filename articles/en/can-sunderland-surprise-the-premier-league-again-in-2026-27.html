Sunderland's Stunning 2025-26 Campaign

Sunderland’s performance last season captured the imagination of football fans across England. Sunderland’s preparations over the summer will be crucial in determining whether they sink or swim in this dual-front campaign.

As Sunderland prepare for the upcoming campaign, football fans will watch intently to see if they can continue to defy expectations.

Wales withdraws backing for Gianni Infantino — UEFA VP Laura McAllister proud

8/5/2026 · By Koora Live

BBC Sport has reported that UEFA vice-president Laura McAllister said she was proud after Wales became the first of the home nations to withdraw their backing for Gianni Infantino. The brief report highlights McAllister's reaction to Wales taking that step, a development noted by the broadcaster.

What the BBC Sport report states

According to BBC Sport, Laura McAllister — who serves as a vice-president at UEFA — expressed pride in Wales after the country withdrew its backing for Gianni Infantino. The outlet summarised McAllister's reaction and identified Wales as the initial home nation to take that action. The report focuses on her response and the fact of withdrawal rather than providing extended detail on the surrounding circumstances.

Why this matters in the wider governance picture

Home nations — commonly referred to as England, Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland — have historic and distinct roles within the football family of the United Kingdom and the Republic of Ireland. Actions taken by any one of these associations can carry symbolic weight within wider discussions about the governance of the game.

Gianni Infantino is widely known as the president of FIFA, the global governing body for football. A withdrawal of backing from a home nation is notable because endorsements and public positions from member associations can influence debates about leadership and reform within football's governing structures. Laura McAllister's role at UEFA gives her statements additional relevance in these conversations, as UEFA is the confederation that represents European national associations at the continental level.

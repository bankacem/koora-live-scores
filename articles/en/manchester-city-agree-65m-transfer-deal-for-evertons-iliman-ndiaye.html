Manchester City Agree £65m Transfer Deal for Everton's Iliman Ndiaye

8/31/2026 · By Koora Live

Manchester City have reportedly reached an agreement to sign Everton's Iliman Ndiaye for a deal valued at a minimum of £60 million, according to BBC Sport. This high-profile transfer marks yet another significant acquisition for the Premier League champions as they aim to bolster their squad for current and future campaigns.

Manchester City Secure Ndiaye in Big-Money Move

Manchester City, known for their financial firepower and meticulous recruitment strategy, are set to welcome Iliman Ndiaye from Everton in a deal worth at least £60 million. This move reinforces their trend of targeting some of the best talents in football to maintain their dominance in domestic and European competitions. While the exact structure of the transfer has not been disclosed, the significant price tag highlights the value that City places on Ndiaye as they look to further fortify their already star-studded team.

Ndiaye's Journey: From Everton to Manchester

Iliman Ndiaye's transfer is a testament to his burgeoning reputation as a top-tier footballer. The Senegalese international, who made his mark at Everton in the Premier League, has evidently caught the attention of Manchester City with his performances. While details surrounding his time at Everton remain under wraps in this announcement, his move is expected to add more dynamism to Pep Guardiola's tactical setup. Ndiaye's style of play, characterized by technical skill and versatility, aligns well with City's methodical and possession-based approach to the game.

Analysis

This deal underscores Manchester City's relentless pursuit of excellence on and off the pitch. By securing Ndiaye for such a substantial fee, the club signals its intent to stay ahead of the competition, both domestically and in European tournaments. Analysts may view this move as part of a broader strategy to replace aging stars or fill potential gaps in the squad, ensuring sustainability alongside success. Everton, on the other hand, could utilize the significant financial windfall to invest in areas requiring reinforcement, a vital step in solidifying their position in the top half of the table or even pushing for European contention.

Manchester City's acquisition of Iliman Ndiaye signals another major investment in their quest for sustained success under Pep Guardiola. Meanwhile, Everton will now have a significant financial boost to shape their future plans in an increasingly competitive environment.

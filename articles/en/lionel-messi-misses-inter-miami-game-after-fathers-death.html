Lionel Messi Misses Inter Miami Game After Father’s Death

8/9/2026 · By Koora Live

Lionel Messi, widely regarded as one of the greatest footballers of all time, was absent as Inter Miami faced Monterrey in a Leagues Cup fixture. His absence came following the tragic news of his father’s passing, as reported by BBC Sport. The match culminated in Miami’s defeat, adding somber weight to an already difficult occasion for the Argentine star and his team.

A Somber Moment for Lionel Messi

Lionel Messi's absence from Inter Miami’s Leagues Cup match against Monterrey marked an emotionally challenging moment for the football icon. BBC Sport reported Messi was unavailable for the game on Saturday night due to the death of his father. This heartbreaking personal loss understandably took precedence for the World Cup-winning forward, who joined Inter Miami earlier this year in a high-profile move from Paris Saint-Germain.

Inter Miami Struggles in Messi’s Absence

Playing without Messi significantly influenced Inter Miami’s fortunes during the match. The team fell short in their clash with Monterrey, who ultimately secured victory. Messi's presence has rejuvenated Miami ever since his arrival, with his playmaking, scoring ability, and leadership proving instrumental in the club's recent successes. His immediate impact included dazzling performances during the Leagues Cup, where he made headlines with trademark moments of brilliance. However, his unavailability on Saturday left Miami vulnerable against a Monterrey side boasting a rich history in North American football.

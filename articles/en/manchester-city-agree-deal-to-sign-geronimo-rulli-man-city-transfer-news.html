Manchester City agree deal to sign Geronimo Rulli — Man City transfer news

8/8/2026 · By Koora Live

BBC Sport reports that Manchester City have agreed a deal to sign Argentina goalkeeper Geronimo Rulli from Marseille. The move, confirmed in the BBC Sport headline and excerpt, links one of Europe's leading Premier League clubs with an experienced South American keeper currently on the books at Olympique de Marseille.

What the signing means for Manchester City

On the face of it, adding Geronimo Rulli gives Manchester City another specialist goalkeeper within a squad that has long been among the elite in both England and Europe. Manchester City are a Premier League club regularly competing for domestic and continental honours, and the recruitment of an experienced international goalkeeper could be viewed as bolstering depth in a key position.

Any new goalkeeper joining a top-level side must adapt to the tactical demands and ball-playing expectations that come with City’s style. The club’s established first-choice keeper is widely known to be Ederson, who has been Manchester City’s long-term starter; the arrival of Rulli therefore appears to be a move aimed at strengthening overall goalkeeping resources rather than necessarily displacing the incumbent number one.

Profile: Geronimo Rulli and his career context

The BBC Sport excerpt identifies Rulli as an Argentina goalkeeper who was playing for Marseille prior to this agreed deal. As an Argentine international, Rulli brings experience of different leagues and styles of play. Olympique de Marseille are a leading club in France’s top division, Ligue 1, and moving from Marseille to Manchester City represents a step into the Premier League environment, which is among the most watched and competitive domestic leagues worldwide.

For Manchester City, recruiting a goalkeeper with international experience fits a broader pattern of elite clubs adding proven professionals who can handle pressure, rotation, and cup competitions. Rulli’s background as an Argentina international and his time at Marseille are the core factual elements reported by BBC Sport about this transaction.

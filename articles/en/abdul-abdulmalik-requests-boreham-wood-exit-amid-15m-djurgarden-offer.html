Abdul Abdulmalik Requests Boreham Wood Exit Amid £1.5m Djurgarden Offer

8/25/2026 · By Koora Live

Rising talent Abdul Abdulmalik has asked to leave Boreham Wood following the club's decision to reject a £1.5m bid from Swedish team Djurgarden. The forward, playing in the National League, expresses ambition to reach the pinnacle of the sport.

Rejected Djurgarden Bid for Abdulmalik

Boreham Wood has turned down a significant £1.5m bid from Swedish outfit Djurgarden for their star forward, Abdul Abdulmalik. This development places the young talent's situation in the spotlight, with the player himself expressing a desire to move on. According to BBC Sport, Abdulmalik envisions himself as a player capable of achieving success at the highest levels of football.

National League Star with Big Dreams

Abdulmalik is currently playing for Boreham Wood, a club competing in the National League, the fifth tier of English football. While the National League has gained recognition in recent years for producing promising talents, Abdulmalik’s public statement suggests he sees his potential extending far beyond this level. His aspiration to progress mirrors the journeys of several players who have risen from the lower tiers to make a mark in prominent European leagues.

Lionel Messi Fined by MLS for Slapping Incident During Inter Miami Match

8/22/2026 · By Koora Live

Lionel Messi, one of football's all-time greats, has been fined by Major League Soccer following an incident in which he slapped Philadelphia Union's Quinn during Inter Miami's recent draw. The episode has sparked discussions about discipline in the league and Messi's adjustment to the North American football landscape.

Messi Penalized Following On-Field Controversy

Lionel Messi, who joined Inter Miami in 2023 after a glittering career in Europe, has been fined by Major League Soccer for an altercation during a game against Philadelphia Union. The incident, which occurred during a draw on Wednesday, saw Messi slap Philadelphia Union player Quinn. While the exact fine amount remains undisclosed, MLS's swift action underscores its commitment to maintaining discipline and sportsmanship across the league.

Inter Miami's Rollercoaster Season

Since Messi's high-profile arrival, Inter Miami has experienced a renaissance, both on and off the pitch. Drawing global attention, Messi's presence has not only boosted ticket sales and merchandise but also significantly improved the team's performances. However, the club has faced its fair share of challenges, including this latest incident involving their star player. Such controversies could potentially distract from the team’s progress, highlighting the delicate balance between the pressures of stardom and maintaining decorum in an intensely competitive environment.

Barcelona Criticize 'Disproportionate' Police Force Used on Fans Before Elche Match

8/24/2026 · By Koora Live

Barcelona Football Club has expressed serious concerns over the conduct of law enforcement officers, claiming that fans were met with 'disproportionate force' ahead of their dominant 5-0 victory against Elche on Sunday. The Catalan giants have called attention to the alleged treatment of their supporters outside the Estadio Manuel Martínez Valero, adding an off-field controversy to their stunning on-field triumph.

Barcelona's Allegations of Police Misconduct

Barcelona publicly condemned what they described as an excessive and unnecessary use of force by police officers targeting their fans before Sunday's match against Elche. While they did not elaborate extensively on the incidents, the club's statement referred to the actions as 'disproportionate,' adding a note of unease to an otherwise celebratory occasion. Such allegations are taken seriously in contemporary football, where the safety of traveling supporters is prioritized by clubs, governing bodies, and local authorities alike. It is unclear as of now if Barcelona plans to file a formal grievance with relevant regulatory organizations such as La Liga or the Spanish Football Federation (RFEF).

A Dominant Display on the Pitch

Despite the off-field discord, Barcelona put on a dazzling performance against Elche, securing a commanding 5-0 victory at the Estadio Manuel Martínez Valero. Matches against Elche often favor the Catalan side historically, and this fixture was no different. The three points keep Barcelona's campaign on track as they aim for domestic and European success this season. While full match details have not been disclosed, such scorelines typically showcase the attacking prowess of Xavi Hernández’s squad, renowned for their clinical finishing and intricate passing play. The result undoubtedly pleased fans, though it seems some of them faced difficulties at the venue before the kick-off.

Analysis

Barcelona’s accusations regarding the treatment of their fans highlight the persistent challenges around crowd management in football, particularly during high-profile matches.

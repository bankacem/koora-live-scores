Ferran Torres Set to Join PSG: Barcelona Forward Misses Training to Finalise Move

8/12/2026 · By Koora Live

Ferran Torres, a player well-acclaimed for his contributions to Spain’s World Cup squad, appears to be on the verge of a major transfer. According to BBC Sport, Torres has been granted permission by Barcelona to skip training as he works to wrap up a move to Paris Saint-Germain (PSG). The imminent deal could mark a substantial shift for both his career and the dynamics of European football.

Barcelona Forward Ferran Torres Prepares for PSG Switch

Ferran Torres, widely recognized for his significant impact on Spain’s national team, particularly during the World Cup, is reportedly finalising a transfer away from Barcelona to French giants PSG. The timing of his absence from training signals that negotiations between the two clubs and player are potentially in their final stages. Torres joined Barcelona from Manchester City in January 2022 and has showcased his versatility as an attacking player since. His departure would leave a void in the Catalan club’s frontline, prompting questions on how they plan to reconfigure their attacking strategy.

PSG Target Yet Another Star to Bolster Their Squad

Paris Saint-Germain has been a prominent player in the transfer market in recent years, strengthening their team with top-tier talents. If the Ferran Torres deal materialises, it will add another exciting component to their attacking lineup. Known for frequently aiming to assemble one of the most star-studded squads in European football, PSG has already attracted global icons such as Lionel Messi, Neymar, and Kylian Mbappé in the past. With Torres potentially joining their roster, their ambitions for domestic and European supremacy remain evident.

Analysis

The decision to allow Ferran Torres to miss training highlights the gravity of the situation and suggests that the move to PSG is likely close to completion. Analysts may see this transfer as another calculated step by PSG to secure top-performing young players with proven records on football’s biggest stages. For Barcelona, losing Torres might be seen as a pragmatic decision—either due to financial considerations or squad optimization—but it could also hint at new arrivals to bolster their squad. It remains uncertain how Torres will fit tactically into PSG's lineup, but his flexibility across the forward positions may make him a valuable asset.

Ferran Torres’s move to PSG represents a significant shift in his career and adds another layer of intrigue to the upcoming European football season. As both clubs await the finalisation of the deal, fans wish to see how this transfer impacts their respective campaigns.

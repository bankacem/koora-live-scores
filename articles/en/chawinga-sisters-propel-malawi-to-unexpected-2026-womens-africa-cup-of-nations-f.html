Frequently Asked Questions

Who are the Chawinga sisters?

Tabitha and Temwa Chawinga are two highly regarded Malawian footballers who have secured central roles within the national women’s team, noted for their partnership on the pitch and leadership qualities.

What is the Women’s Africa Cup of Nations?

The Women’s Africa Cup of Nations, or WAFCON, is a biennial tournament organized by the Confederation of African Football, featuring national teams from across the continent competing for continental glory.

Why was Malawi’s appearance in the final considered a surprise?

Historically, Malawian women’s football has had limited success on the continental stage, so a run to the WAFCON final signalled an unexpected breakthrough and reflected significant progress in the nation’s football development.

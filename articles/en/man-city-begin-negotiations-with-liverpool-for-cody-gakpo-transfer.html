Man City Begin Negotiations with Liverpool for Cody Gakpo Transfer

8/30/2026 · By Koora Live

Manchester City have commenced talks with Liverpool regarding a potential move for Netherlands international Cody Gakpo. While Tottenham Hotspur are also reportedly interested, sources suggest that Gakpo favors a transfer to Pep Guardiola's side should he depart Anfield.

Cody Gakpo in Demand: A Rising Star of Dutch Football

Cody Gakpo has steadily established himself as one of the most promising talents in European football. Known for his versatility, pace, and clinical finishing, the Dutch forward has made a significant impact both at the club and international levels. Having successfully transitioned from the Eredivisie, where he was a standout performer for PSV Eindhoven, to the Premier League with Liverpool, Gakpo has demonstrated an ability to adapt to high-pressure situations and compete at the highest level. This latest development, involving an approach from Manchester City, signals another chapter in the 24-year-old’s burgeoning career.

Manchester City Emerge as Gakpo's Preferred Destination

According to BBC Sport, Manchester City has emerged as Gakpo's preferred choice should he leave Liverpool. This preference is hardly surprising given City’s status as a dominant force in English and European football under Pep Guardiola’s stewardship. The reigning Premier League champions have cultivated a reputation for recruiting versatile, dynamic attackers who thrive in their possession-oriented system. Gakpo’s ability to operate across the front line would make him a seamless fit for City’s fluid approach. Despite signing the likes of Erling Haaland and Jack Grealish in recent seasons, the addition of Gakpo could provide Guardiola with yet another potent weapon in his already star-studded squad.

Analysis

The potential transfer of Cody Gakpo to Manchester City highlights the club’s unrelenting drive to remain at the pinnacle of English football. Adding a player of Gakpo’s caliber and versatility could be seen as a statement of intent, especially given the links to domestic rivals like Tottenham Hotspur. Furthermore, this move would not only bolster Guardiola's attacking options but could also signal a tactical shift or serve as preparation for future squad transitions. From Liverpool's perspective, the prospect of losing a top player like Gakpo to a direct Premier League rival will likely be unsettling. However, the decision to hold talks could indicate that the Merseyside club is prepared to cash in on the forward, potentially to fund other transfer market pursuits.

The potential transfer of Cody Gakpo from Liverpool to Manchester City could make headlines in the footballing world. While City have initiated talks and appear to be Gakpo’s preferred choice, it remains to be seen how the discussions unfold. Fans and analysts alike will undoubtedly keep a close eye on this developing story.

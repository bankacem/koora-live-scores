McInnes Bares Frustration as Rangers Continue Winless Start to Season

8/9/2026 · By Koora Live

Rangers manager Derek McInnes has admitted that the club’s opening three fixtures have failed to produce a positive result, leaving him both frustrated and anxious about the team’s trajectory. After a 2‑1 home defeat against Hibernian, the hosts were further dampened by the injury lay‑off of striker Youssef Chermiti, whose transfer value has generated significant interest.

Rangers’ Early Struggles: A Summary of Results

The Scottish giants kicked off the campaign with a narrow loss at home to Hibernian, a match that showcased their defensive vulnerability despite a resilient defensive line. Earlier in the round of three, Rangers failed to secure victory in two other fixtures, leaving the team without a win on board. The cumulative effect of these results has seen the club slip in the league standings, a position that honours their rich history and high expectations.

Despite persistent efforts from the coaching staff, the third match denied them their first trophy of the season. The pressure has intensified as the club’s supporters and media analysts – including coverage by BBC Sport – called for a decisive result to restate Rangers’ reputation as a top-tier side in Scottish football.

Manager Stagnancy and the Chermiti Dilemma

Derek McInnes remains under a heavy weight of scrutiny, struggling to inspire a title‑aspiring squad that has delivered inconsistent performances. The injury to Youssef Chermiti, a forward deemed a high‑value acquisition in a big‑money transfer, has deprived the attack of a key creative outlet, putting additional strain on the team’s forward build‑up.

McInnes’s frustration is evident; he is still in pursuit of a win after three league games, as reported by BBC Sport. The emotional toll of a winless streak in a club steeped in tradition is palpable, especially when combined with the loss of a primary striker. The manager faces the challenge of reshuffling the line‑up to regain momentum and restore confidence amongst his squad.

Analysis

From a tactical perspective, Rangers’ early lack of titles suggests a deeper issue with squad cohesion and a possible mismatch between the manager’s philosophy and the players’ current form. The injury to Chermiti not only reduces offensive potency but also could affect morale and goal‑scoring variety. Analysts might view this as a moment for the club’s front office to rigorously evaluate the team's depth and consider bolster options.

Given Rangers’ historical pedigree, securing a positive result in forthcoming fixtures is critical to staving off pressure from the transfer‑linked media narrative. Moreover, the club’s home advantage might prove decisive if the management can quickly reorganise the structure to better distribute workrate, ultimately turning the season around.

Rangers’ 20‑year task will depend on rallying behind a reinvigorated squad, overcoming the setback of an injury‑plagued start, and restoring confidence that will deliver the results crucial to join the top tier of Scottish football.

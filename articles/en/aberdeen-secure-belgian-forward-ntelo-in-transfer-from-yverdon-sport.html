Aberdeen Secure Belgian Forward Ntelo in Transfer from Yverdon-Sport

8/8/2026 · By Koora Live

Aberdeen FC has made an intriguing addition to their squad by signing Belgian forward Mitchy Ntelo from Swiss outfit Yverdon-Sport. Announced by BBC Sport, the transfer involves an undisclosed fee with manager Stephen Robinson highlighting the player's versatility as a key factor behind the move. This recruitment comes amidst the club's ambition to bolster their attacking options for the ongoing season.

Who is Mitchy Ntelo?

Mitchy Ntelo is a promising forward from Belgium, known for his versatility on the pitch. While Ntelo's name may not yet be widely recognized among international football audiences, his potential has evidently caught the attention of Aberdeen's management. Having previously played for Yverdon-Sport, a team featuring in the Swiss Challenge League, Ntelo has honed his skills in European football. Belgian players have historically gained a reputation for blending technical ability with physical prowess, traits that Ntelo potentially brings to the Scottish Premiership.

Stephen Robinson’s Vision for Aberdeen

Aberdeen manager Stephen Robinson has pointed to Mitchy Ntelo's versatility as a major factor in the decision to bring him to Pittodrie Stadium. This flexibility could prove crucial for the Dons, given the demands of the Scottish Premiership, where injuries and fixture congestion can necessitate players capable of operating effectively in multiple positions. As Aberdeen aims to secure a strong top-half finish, Robinson's philosophy of recruiting adaptable players may be vital in maintaining consistency throughout the season.

No Away Fans Allowed for SPFL Old Firm Matches This Season

9/4/2026 · By Koora Live

A significant decision has been made regarding one of the fiercest football rivalries in the world. According to BBC Sport, no away fans will be permitted at Celtic vs. Rangers matches in the Scottish Premiership or Premier Sports Cup for the current season. This ruling will undoubtedly impact the atmosphere of these iconic fixtures.

The Old Firm Rivalry: A Brief Look

The rivalry between Celtic and Rangers, popularly known as the Old Firm, is one of the most intense and historic in world football. Based in Glasgow, these two clubs have dominated Scottish football for more than a century, amassing a combined total of over 100 league titles. Matches between the two sides are known not only for their quality on the pitch but also for the electric atmosphere generated by passionate fans. Traditionally, away supporters have added to the spectacle with vocal backing, making this recent decision to exclude them a notable change to the derby dynamic.

The Decision: No Away Fans in 2023/24

BBC Sport reports that neither Celtic nor Rangers will allow away supporters to attend their encounters in the Scottish Premiership or the Premier Sports Cup this season. This decision follows a history of tensions and controversies surrounding Old Firm matches, where the rivalry sometimes spills over into off-pitch conflict. While previously smaller allocations of away tickets were an effort to manage crowd control, this season will mark a complete absence of away fans at these fixtures.

Analysis

The absence of away fans at Old Firm matches this season could have a profound impact on the dynamics of these encounters. On one hand, it may enhance safety and reduce the likelihood of crowd trouble, a concern that has long surrounded these derbies. On the other hand, it could diminish the atmosphere, as the presence of rival fans often serves to make these games even more heated and intense. From a sporting perspective, the decision may also offer a slight advantage to the home side, removing a key source of motivation for the visiting team. This development underscores the unique challenges faced by such high-profile derbies in balancing passion with safety and security.

The move to exclude away fans from Old Firm matches marks a substantial change in how these historic games will be experienced this season. While safety concerns are undoubtedly front and center, the absence of rival supporters will alter the dynamic in both the stadium and on the pitch. Whether this contributes to a calmer environment or detracts from the spectacle, only time will tell.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Old Firm rivalry?

The Old Firm refers to the intense football rivalry between Scottish clubs Celtic and Rangers, both based in Glasgow. It is one of the oldest and most famous rivalries in the sport.

Why are there no away fans at Old Firm games this season?

According to BBC Sport, Celtic and Rangers have decided not to allow away fans at their league and cup matches this season, likely as a measure to enhance crowd safety and reduce tensions.

The decision applies to Old Firm matches in both the Scottish Premiership and the Premier Sports Cup during the current season.

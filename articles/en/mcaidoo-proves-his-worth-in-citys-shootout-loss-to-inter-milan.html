McAidoo proves his worth in City’s shootout loss to Inter Milan

8/2/2026

According to BBC Sport, Manchester City’s season has taken an unexpected turn after a penalty‑shootout defeat to Inter Milan ended their bid for another trophy. The loss triggered an urgent moment for several squad members to step up.

Among those eager for a chance, teenage winger Ryan McAidoo stood out. Although the match – which saw the English club ultimately fall short – offered a dramatic finish, it also illuminated McAidoo’s growing influence on the pitch.

Throughout the game, the 17‑year‑old displayed his trademark speed and agility on the right flank, picking up possession amid pressure and delivering precise crosses into the box. He also contributed defensively, tracking back to support the full‑back and forcing up on edge‑of‑the‑area drills in the final minutes.

Manager Enzo Maresca’s comments were notable: he praised McAidoo for seizing the opportunity to showcase his abilities to the coaching staff. Maresca has long admired the youngster’s work ethic, hinting that he thinks “the kind of winger I love” is rapidly emerging from City’s ranks.

Looking ahead, the defeat will likely prompt a reassessment of squad options for upcoming fixtures. Yet for McAidoo, the penalty shootout loss has only reinforced his desire to prove himself. With his combination of pace, technical skill, and determination, he is poised to become a key contributor when the club moves forward in domestic and European competitions. The experience gained from the shootout will serve him well as he continues to develop into a top‑class winger under Maresca’s guidance.

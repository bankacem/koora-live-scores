Transfer gossip: Man City explore Enzo Fernández as Arsenal and Man United eye Pio Esposito; Liverpool, Barcola fee unresolved

8/7/2026 · By Koora Live

BBC Sport has highlighted fresh transfer-market chatter involving several Premier League heavyweights. Manchester City are reportedly exploring a move for Enzo Fernández, Arsenal could enter a contest with Manchester United for Pio Esposito, and Liverpool have yet to finalise a fee for Bradley Barcola, according to a roundup published by BBC Sport.

Man City linked with Enzo Fernández

BBC Sport's transfer bulletin states that Manchester City are exploring a move for Enzo Fernández. Fernández is widely known as an Argentina international midfielder who rose to prominence following his performances for club and country, including at major international tournaments. He has been a high-profile name in transfer speculation since establishing himself at the top level.

Manchester City are one of Europe's wealthiest and most successful clubs in recent years, with a track record of recruiting midfield talent to suit Pep Guardiola's tactical model. Any reported interest in a player of Fernández's reputation would be in keeping with the club's profile and ongoing activity in the transfer market.

Arsenal, Man United and Liverpool stories: Pio Esposito and Bradley Barcola

The same BBC Sport piece mentions that Arsenal could battle Manchester United for Pio Esposito. Arsenal and Manchester United are two of England's most decorated clubs and frequently compete for emerging talents and established players alike. When those clubs are linked to the same target, it typically signals a player attracting significant attention.

BBC Sport also reports that Liverpool have not yet agreed a fee for Bradley Barcola. Barcola is known as a forward who has been linked with moves to Premier League clubs; he is associated with Olympique Lyonnais in the French league. Transfer negotiations for attacking players can hinge on valuation, timing and squad needs, and a lack of an agreed fee simply means talks are ongoing rather than concluded.

Analysis

These kinds of rumours are a staple of the transfer window and can reflect genuine interest, preliminary enquiries, or media-driven speculation. Manchester City's reported exploration of Enzo Fernández aligns with their historical appetite for elite midfield reinforcements, while Arsenal and Manchester United squaring up over the same player is consistent with both clubs' strategies of targeting promising talent. Liverpool's situation with Bradley Barcola — where a fee is yet to be agreed — illustrates a common commercial hurdle: clubs can be attracted to a player but differ on valuation. Analysts might see these reports as early indicators of potential moves, but until clubs confirm deals, such stories remain provisional.

The BBC Sport roundup highlights several intriguing transfer threads involving top Premier League clubs. While interest in Enzo Fernández, competition for Pio Esposito, and an unresolved Barcola fee are notable, these items should be treated as developing stories until clubs make official announcements.

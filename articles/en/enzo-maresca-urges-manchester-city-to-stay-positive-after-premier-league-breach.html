Enzo Maresca Urges Manchester City to Stay Positive After Premier League Breach

10/8/2026 · By Koora Live

Manchester City’s head coach Enzo Maresca has issued a clear message of calm and determination to his squad following the club’s admission of paying for a play‑off return involving the Premier League’s financial fair play regulations.

The club’s admission, confirmed by the BBC, found them guilty of breaching the league’s financial rules that aim to keep clubs’ spending in line with their revenues. Maresca, who stepped into the role after the departure of Pep Guardiola, remains focused on keeping the players centred on performance rather than the scrutiny surrounding the club’s financial compliance.

Financial Fair Play Breach and Its Footballing Context

The Premier League has long enforced a financial framework designed to prevent clubs from running out‑of‑balance budgets, thereby maintaining competitive integrity across the league. A breach suggests that Manchester City may have exceeded permitted spending limits or breached the structure around transfers and player contracts.

While the club’s history is peppered with domestic triumphs – four Premier League titles in five years, a historic 2023 treble, and the 2022–23 Super Cup – the financial outlay has often done City’s reputation a favour in attracting elite talent. However, the league’s rules are strictly enforced to discourage unsustainable spending.

Suspicion of a breach, therefore, places City under significant public scrutiny. It may carry a range of repercussions, from fines to potential point deductions, and could also affect squad morale and public perception if not handled tactfully by the club’s management.

Maresca’s Call for Focus Amid the Financial Storm

In a calm but firm address, Maresca told City’s players to keep the focus on football and remain positive. The coach highlighted that the team’s primary objective is still on the pitch – to secure victories, maintain momentum in the Premier League, and continue a push for silverware.

Maresca also underscored the importance of the squad’s unity during a period of potential unrest. He described the challenges of a compliance situation as a game plan that will not distract from immediate performance. By emphasising positivity, he is steering the team clear of the often calmer whisper of probability that fines might affect the club’s future budget.

"We have to keep looking at the positives," Maresca remarked. "The next match is still a match. The players must keep playing, stay positive, and keep focusing." Such an approach echoes the tactical discipline City is known for under the previous management, creating a stable environment in unpredictable times.

Analysis

The situation for Manchester City is, on the surface, a risk to the club’s financial standing and a blow to its brand image. Maresca’s insistence on keeping a focus on football and maintaining a positive mindset could be interpreted as a strategic attempt to protect the team’s morale and performance.

Analysts might see the coach’s remarks as an attempt to contain distractions and reduce the potential negative impact on player concentration so that the “on‑field work” stays at the centre of the day. It also represents an opportunity for the squad to embody resilience—reinforcing their commitment to the club’s sporting rather than administrative disputes.

Despite the financial scrutiny that now shrouds the club, Enzo Maresca’s call for positivity and focus suggests a strategy for Manchester City to engage with the Premier League’s rules while keeping the team’s ambitions on track. The long‑term impact will depend on how the club navigates compliance measures, yet the squad’s resilient stance sets a tone that may well be integral to the club’s continued success.

Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly did Manchester City breach?

Manchester City was found guilty of paying for a play‑off return involving the Premier League’s financial fair play regulations, leading to the club’s admission of a financial rules breach. The BBC reported this finding as the primary fact of the event.

What are the Premier League’s financial rules about?

The Premier League implements a financial fair play framework to maintain competitive integrity by aligning clubs’ spending with revenue outcomes. Violating these rules can lead to fines, points deductions, or other sanctions aimed at preventing unsustainable spending.

How is the squad expected to react?

According to Maresca’s public statement, players are advised to remain focused and positive while preparing for matches, regardless of the administrative issue. The emphasis is on mental discipline and not allowing external controversies to detract from on‑field performance.

Mohamed Salah's Decision: Why Trabzonspor Over Saudi Arabia and MLS?

8/6/2026 · By Koora Live

Mohamed Salah has made headlines with his surprising decision to join Trabzonspor, opting against moves to the Saudi Pro League or Major League Soccer. This transfer not only marks a significant chapter in Salah's illustrious career but also raises intriguing questions about its impact on Turkish football.

Mohamed Salah's Career Journey Until Now

Mohamed Salah is widely regarded as one of football's elite talents, with a career highlighted by goal-scoring records and accolades during his time at Liverpool. Having previously represented clubs such as Chelsea, Roma, and Fiorentina, his move to Trabzonspor is particularly noteworthy given his stature in the global game. Known for his blistering speed, incredible finishing ability, and work ethic, Salah has become an iconic figure, especially in his native Egypt. His role at Liverpool saw him spearhead the team’s domestic and European successes, notably winning the UEFA Champions League in 2019 and the Premier League in 2020.

Why Trabzonspor Over Saudi Arabia and MLS?

As reported by BBC Sport, Salah surprised many by choosing Trabzonspor instead of moving to Saudi Arabia or MLS, arguably more lucrative destinations. The decision could reflect personal priorities beyond financial gain, such as a desire to play competitive football in Europe or to experience a less commercialized football environment. Trabzonspor, one of Turkey’s most historic clubs, may offer Salah the opportunity to solidify his legacy in a league with passionate fan culture and fierce competition. Turkish football, while not as globally prominent as the leagues he decided against, boasts a rich history and often serves as a proving ground for skilled players looking to thrive away from the limelight of Europe’s top five leagues.

Analysis

Salah's choice of Trabzonspor signals several possible motivations. For one, it could boost the profile of Turkish football, potentially reigniting interest in the Super Lig as a destination for top-tier players. It may also represent a preference for a fresh challenge after years of competing at the highest levels in England. Analysts might view this move as Salah valuing legacy building through adaptability, demonstrating his commitment to tackling unique challenges rather than opting for primarily financial motivations or lifestyle-driven leagues like Saudi Arabia or MLS. This could inspire a trend among players of similar stature, showcasing the competitive appeal of leagues outside the usual mainstream destinations.

Mohamed Salah’s decision to join Trabzonspor is a fascinating twist in his celebrated career, hinting at a focus on legacy and competition over pure financial reward. The move could have wide-reaching implications for Turkish football and inspire future career paths for other superstar players.

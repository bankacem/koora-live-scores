Medical and sporting context: what these terms usually mean

Pharmacological sedation is a medical practice used to reduce consciousness or stress responses in patients who require intensive care, invasive procedures, or rest to aid recovery. It can be applied for various reasons, including to tolerate mechanical ventilation, control pain or agitation, or to stabilize a patient while treatment continues. Ventilator support, referenced in the BBC Sport headline, indicates assisted breathing via a machine when a patient cannot breathe adequately on their own; its usage ranges from short-term support to longer critical care interventions, depending on the underlying condition.

Manchester City Reportedly Eye Cody Gakpo Transfer

8/24/2026 · By Koora Live

Manchester City has emerged as a potential suitor for Dutch forward Cody Gakpo, according to BBC Sport. The burgeoning transfer saga adds another exciting layer to the club's pursuit of elite talent, while Newcastle United and Manchester United are also making major moves in the transfer market.

Manchester City's Interest in Cody Gakpo

Per BBC Sport's latest update, Manchester City has been named as a surprise contender in the race to sign Cody Gakpo. The Dutch international, known for his versatility and sharp attacking instincts, has made waves since excelling in his home country and impressing in international competitions. Gakpo, capable of playing across the frontline, is widely regarded as one of the most promising talents in European football. If the Premier League champions pursue this move further, it would add yet another dimension to an already star-studded attacking lineup at the Etihad under Pep Guardiola. Manchester City, reigning English champions and ranked among Europe's elite clubs, are no strangers to making high-profile transfers to bolster their squad. Their interest in Gakpo signals ambition, especially given their fluid attacking system that thrives on multi-functional forwards.

Newcastle United and Manchester United's Transfer Activities

In addition to the buzz around Manchester City, two other Premier League clubs are making significant strides in the transfer window. Newcastle United, whose ambitions have soared under new ownership, are reportedly closing in on a deal to bring Nico Gonzalez to St. James’ Park. The young prospect could provide depth and quality to Eddie Howe’s evolving squad, which has already shown promise in breaking into the Premier League's upper echelons. Meanwhile, Manchester United remains confident of securing the services of Myles Lewis-Skelly. Known for their aggressive participation in the transfer market, United seems set on bolstering their roster, potentially adding youthful energy to a squad aiming for long-term success under manager Erik ten Hag. Both Newcastle and Manchester United’s moves highlight the competitiveness of the English top flight as clubs jostle not just for silverware, but also for the young talent that can shape their future.

Analysis

The reported interest from Manchester City in Cody Gakpo underlines their ongoing commitment to staying ahead of their rivals by targeting top emerging talents. Pep Guardiola's system thrives on flexible forwards who can interchange positions and adapt to different tactical demands — qualities Gakpo has demonstrated repeatedly. Newcastle aims to consolidate its breakthrough onto the European stage with fresh talent, while Manchester United appears to be focused on reinforcing its foundation with a longer-term strategy. These moves set the stage for a fascinating transfer window as English clubs continue to reshape their squads.

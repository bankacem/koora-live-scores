Tottenham agree £75m deal to sign Savinho from Manchester City

8/21/2026 · By Koora Live

Tottenham Hotspur have agreed a deal to sign Manchester City winger Savinho for a reported £75m, with a further £10m available in potential add-ons, BBC Sport reports. The move, as described by BBC Sport, would see a high-profile Premier League transfer between two of England's top clubs involving a young attacking wide player.

The deal as reported

According to BBC Sport, Tottenham have reached an agreement with Manchester City to sign Savinho for an initial fee of £75m, with up to £10m in additional payments contingent on add-ons. The source identifies Savinho as a winger and lists the two clubs involved. Those are the only specific financial details provided in the report excerpt; further elements such as personal terms, contract length, medical clearance or an exact completion date are not part of the information given here.

Transfers of this scale between Premier League clubs frequently include performance-related bonuses, sell-on clauses or appearance incentives — the mention of potential add-ons fits that common structure. BBC Sport is cited as the reporting outlet for the agreement.

From a general footballing perspective, a transfer that brings a winger into Tottenham’s squad is consistent with a club seeking to bolster its attacking options. Wide players can provide direct goal threat, assist creation and tactical flexibility, particularly in systems that rely on pace, dribbling and wide combinations.

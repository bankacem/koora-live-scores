Egypt's Haissem Hassan Signs Four-Year Deal with Celtic

8/12/2026 · By Koora Live

Celtic have officially secured the services of highly-rated Egyptian winger Haissem Hassan, signing him to a four-year contract from Spanish club Real Oviedo. The deal also includes an option for a fifth year, signaling the Scottish powerhouse’s confidence in the young talent.

Haissem Hassan Joins Celtic from Real Oviedo

Celtic have announced the signing of Haissem Hassan from Real Oviedo on a four-year deal. The agreement also includes an option for an additional year, a move that underscores the Glaswegian club’s intent to bolster their attacking options ahead of upcoming fixtures. Hassan, who has showcased his potential in Spain, is expected to bring creativity and flair to Celtic’s lineup. The transition to Scottish football could offer him a platform to further develop his skills and make a mark in European football.

Celtic’s History of Investing in Rising Talents

Under the stewardship of recent managers, Celtic has had a long-standing tradition of identifying and nurturing emerging players. By signing promising talents like Hassan, the club continues its commitment to building a squad capable of dominating domestically and competing in European tournaments. With notable facilities like Celtic Park, which seats over 60,000 fans, and a history of success in the Scottish Premiership, Celtic has consistently provided a springboard for young players to shine on the big stage.

Analysis

The acquisition of Haissem Hassan suggests Celtic are focusing on reinforcing their attacking depth for both domestic competition and European campaigns. While Hassan arrives with experience from Spain, adjusting to the Scottish Premiership could be an interesting challenge, given the league’s physicality and tempo. Analysts are likely to view this signing favorably, as it aligns with Celtic’s strategic focus on targeting young talents with high potential. If Hassan can adapt quickly, he could be an essential piece in Celtic's attacking puzzle moving forward.

Haissem Hassan’s signing marks yet another strategic addition to Celtic’s squad, reflecting their pursuit of talent and ambition. If the Egyptian winger can settle into the Scottish Premiership, he could become a vital asset to the club's aspirations on multiple fronts in the seasons ahead.

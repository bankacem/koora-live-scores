Manchester City's £80m Transfer for Cody Gakpo Reportedly Nearing Collapse

9/1/2026 · By Koora Live

A high-profile £80 million move for Cody Gakpo from Liverpool to Manchester City is reportedly on the verge of falling apart, according to BBC Sport. The Dutch forward, known for his versatility and creativity, had been linked with a blockbuster transfer, but the deal now appears uncertain.

Cody Gakpo's Rise and Impact at Liverpool

Cody Gakpo joined Liverpool in January 2023 from Dutch side PSV Eindhoven, commanding attention for his impressive performances in the Eredivisie and on the international stage with the Netherlands. Known for his versatility, Gakpo excels in multiple attacking roles, including as a winger or central forward, making him a valuable asset for any side. Since joining Jurgen Klopp's team, Gakpo has showcased his skillset in the demanding environment of the Premier League, contributing goals and assists at critical moments. His ability to adapt to Liverpool's high-pressing system highlighted his tactical intelligence and work ethic, making him a key figure in the team's attack.

Manchester City's Interest in Cody Gakpo

Reports of Manchester City's interest in Gakpo signaled their intent to further bolster an already formidable squad. Having clinched a historic treble last season by winning the Premier League, FA Cup, and Champions League, Pep Guardiola's side has shown no signs of slowing down. Bringing in a player of Gakpo’s profile appeared to align with Guardiola’s philosophy of utilizing technically proficient and adaptable players. At £80 million, such a transfer would reflect the dominant spending power of Manchester City, whose recruitment strategy has been a cornerstone of their recent domestic and European success.

Analysis

The reported collapse of this deal could stem from various factors, such as disagreements over valuation, the timing, or Liverpool's reluctance to part with a key player. For Liverpool, retaining Gakpo could be viewed as crucial for maintaining their attacking depth and aspirations for a return to title contention. Meanwhile, for Manchester City, the potential failure to secure Gakpo might prompt them to shift focus to other targets, as they are unlikely to compromise on strengthening their squad. Analysts might interpret this situation as a sign of Liverpool's determination to hold onto a squad capable of challenging on multiple fronts, or perhaps a calculated negotiation tactic that could still resurrect the deal.

The potential breakdown of Manchester City's pursuit of Cody Gakpo adds an intriguing layer to this summer’s transfer window. Whether this deal is truly beyond salvage or simply a hurdle in prolonged negotiations remains to be seen. What is certain, however, is that both clubs have significant stakes in the outcome.

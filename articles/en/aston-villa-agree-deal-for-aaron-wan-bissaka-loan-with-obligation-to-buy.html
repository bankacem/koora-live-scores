Aston Villa agree deal for Aaron Wan-Bissaka — loan with obligation to buy

8/18/2026 · By Koora Live

Aston Villa have agreed to sign West Ham defender Aaron Wan-Bissaka on an initial loan arrangement that includes an obligation to complete a permanent transfer at the end of the season, according to BBC Sport. The move, reported by BBC Sport, marks a notable squad addition for Villa and a new chapter for the right-sided defender.

What the deal actually is

The clubs have agreed the structure of the transaction as an initial loan with a contractual obligation to buy once the temporary period ends. This means Wan-Bissaka will join Aston Villa immediately on a loan basis, with a permanent transfer expected to follow at the conclusion of the season under the terms agreed between the clubs.

Loan deals with obligations to buy are a common mechanism in modern transfer activity. They allow the acquiring club to register a player quickly while deferring the formal completion of a permanent transfer until a later contractual point, which can be driven by sporting or financial timing considerations. BBC Sport is the source reporting this agreement.

Player background and what he brings

Aaron Wan-Bissaka is a recognized right-back who has come through English football over the last decade. He rose to prominence at Crystal Palace before earning a high-profile move to Manchester United. More recently he has been part of West Ham United's squad prior to the agreement reported by BBC Sport.

On the pitch Wan-Bissaka is widely known for his defensive abilities at full-back, particularly one-on-one defending and tackling. Those attributes can offer a team greater solidity on the right flank, while his experience in the Premier League brings familiarity with the pace and demands of England's top division. For Aston Villa, adding a player with established Premier League minutes could be viewed as strengthening depth and defensive options at full-back.

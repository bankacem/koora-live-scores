Nottingham Forest Linked with Tijjani Reijnders Amid Transfer Gossip

8/4/2026 · By Koora Live

Transfer speculations are heating up as Nottingham Forest reportedly set their sights on Tijjani Reijnders. Meanwhile, Tottenham Hotspur are eyeing Savinho and Nicolas Jackson, while Real Madrid have resumed negotiations over a contract extension for Vinicius Jr. These developments come as clubs across Europe strategize their moves ahead of upcoming windows.

Nottingham Forest Target Tijjani Reijnders

According to reports from BBC Sport, Nottingham Forest have expressed interest in Tijjani Reijnders. The Dutch midfielder currently plays for AC Milan and has shown considerable promise in Serie A. Forest, who are competing in the English Premier League, appear keen to bolster their midfield options. The club, known for its rich history including two European Cup triumphs in the late 1970s, has recently been active in the transfer market as it seeks to consolidate its position in the top flight following promotion in 2022. The potential addition of Reijnders could inject creativity and technical prowess into their squad. It remains to be seen whether the club will step up its pursuit of the player as the season progresses.

Tottenham Hotspur Eye Savinho and Nicolas Jackson

Tottenham Hotspur also feature prominently in Wednesday’s transfer gossip shared by BBC Sport. The North London side reportedly has its eye on Savinho, a Brazilian talent often praised for his flair, as well as Villarreal's Nicolas Jackson. Spurs, under the management of Ange Postecoglou, are looking to strengthen their squad as they aim to remain competitive in both domestic and continental competitions. Historically a side known for nurturing young talent, Tottenham could see potential signings like Savinho and Jackson as ideal candidates to fit their technical and attacking philosophy.

Analysis

These transfer rumors highlight the relentless strategic efforts of top clubs in Europe to compete at the highest level. Nottingham Forest's interest in Tijjani Reijnders suggests a focus on shoring up their midfield with high-caliber talent, which aligns with their goals of establishing long-term Premier League stability. Meanwhile, Tottenham Hotspur's pursuit of Savinho and Nicolas Jackson demonstrates the club's commitment to maintaining its attacking edge while continuing its well-known policy of investing in promising youth players. Lastly, Real Madrid resuming contract talks with Vinicius Jr underscores the club's desire to secure its future around one of its brightest stars, in a move that could prove crucial amidst their broader ambitions in Europe.

As transfer rumors swirl, clubs across Europe are eyeing moves that could shape their competitive futures. Nottingham Forest, Tottenham Hotspur, and Real Madrid each offer intriguing storylines in this latest round of speculation. Fans will be eager to see how these developments unfold in the coming weeks.

8/19/2026 · By Koora Live

Celtic delivered a polished performance in their first-leg Champions League play-off against Austrian side LASK, securing a commanding lead. The spotlight was firmly on Camilo Duran, whose two spectacular goals set the tone for a night of dominance by the Scottish champions.

Celtic Take Commanding First-Leg Advantage

Scottish giants Celtic are within touching distance of returning to Europe’s elite competition after a convincing display against LASK. The Glasgow club, known for their rich history in European football, showcased their pedigree with a performance that sparkled both offensively and defensively. Anchoring their success on the night was the brilliance of Camilo Duran, who delivered two striking goals to help his side secure a crucial lead heading into the return leg. These strikes encapsulated not only his technical ability but also his knack for influencing high-stakes matches.

Camilo Duran Steals the Show

The match was defined by Duran's clinical finishing and sharp instincts in front of goal. The Colombian forward's brace included two stunning goals that left the LASK defense scrambling. His contributions are a testament to the depth of Celtic's attacking options, a hallmark of their sustained domestic dominance and ability to compete on the European stage. With this commanding performance, Duran continues to solidify his role as a key figure in the team's aspirations for a successful campaign, both at home and abroad.

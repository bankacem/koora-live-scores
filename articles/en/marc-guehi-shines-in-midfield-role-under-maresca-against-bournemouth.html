Marc Guehi Shines in Midfield Role Under Maresca Against Bournemouth

8/24/2026 · By Koora Live

Enzo Maresca's tactical experiment proved a masterstroke as Marc Guehi delivered an impressive performance in an unfamiliar midfield role during Manchester City's match against Bournemouth. According to analysis on Match of the Day, the centre-back adapted seamlessly, drawing high praise for his contribution.

Guehi's Transition from Defence to Midfield

Marc Guehi, known primarily as a commanding centre-back, found himself at the center of attention for his remarkable adaptation to a midfield role during Manchester City’s recent encounter with Bournemouth. Under Enzo Maresca’s management, such tactical gambles seem to be part of the strategy to unlock new dimensions in the team's play. While it is unusual for a central defender to step out of their comfort zone into midfield, Guehi’s composure, passing ability, and understanding of positional play ensured his success in the role.

Maresca's Tactical Experiment Pays Off

Enzo Maresca, Manchester City's assistant coach who is reportedly known for his tactical flexibility, appears to have introduced this innovative move to give his team a fresh edge. By placing Guehi in midfield, Maresca likely banked on the player’s defensive knowledge to support the team’s transitions and structure. This calculated risk paid off handsomely, as discussed on Match of the Day by former England international Joe Hart. The decision reflects how modern football increasingly values positional adaptability, especially among players with Guehi's technical and physical caliber.

Analysis

The decision to deploy Marc Guehi in a midfield position against Bournemouth highlights an ongoing evolution in football tactics, where versatility is increasingly prioritized. Analysts might see this as a pivotal move that broadens Manchester City's tactical options. Such experiments, while risky, can yield significant dividends, especially when players like Guehi possess the technical quality and football IQ needed to execute them successfully. This also underscores Maresca’s innovative approach, which complements Pep Guardiola’s philosophy of using players in multipurpose roles to outmaneuver opponents.

Marc Guehi's stellar performance in a midfield role against Bournemouth serves as a testament to his versatility and Maresca's bold tactical ingenuity. As football continues to evolve, experiments like these could become the cornerstone of top-level strategies.

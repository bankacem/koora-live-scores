Transfer gossip: Liverpool not pursuing Djed Spence as Juventus eye Joshua Zirkzee loan; Arsenal & Man United scout Kaua Elias

8/7/2026 · By Koora Live

Friday's transfer gossip, as reported by BBC Sport, delivers a clear update on several high-profile strands of summer speculation. The headline items include Liverpool deciding not to pursue Djed Spence this window, Juventus looking to arrange a loan move for striker Joshua Zirkzee, and scouts from Arsenal and Manchester United monitoring Kaua Elias, among other items in the round-up.

Liverpool and the Djed Spence situation

According to BBC Sport's Friday gossip column, Liverpool have no intention of pursuing Djed Spence this summer. That development closes one line of speculation connecting the English club with the right-sided defender/wing-back, at least for the current transfer window.

Liverpool have a well-established recruitment model that balances immediate first-team needs with longer-term squad planning. When a major club publicly rules out interest in a player — as reported here — it typically signals either satisfaction with existing options in that position or a shift of priorities elsewhere in the market.

Juventus and Joshua Zirkzee; scouts at Kaua Elias

BBC Sport also reports that Juventus are looking to secure Joshua Zirkzee on loan. Zirkzee, known broadly as a forward option in European football, has been linked with various moves in past windows and a loan approach would be a typical mechanism for a club like Juventus seeking to add attacking depth without committing to an immediate permanent transfer.

The same report notes that Arsenal and Manchester United have sent scouts to watch Kaua Elias. Scouting by top Premier League clubs routinely targets both established professionals and younger talents; the presence of observers from Arsenal and Manchester United suggests interest in assessing the player's attributes and potential fit, though the BBC piece does not claim any imminent offers.

Analysis

These snippets from BBC Sport reflect how transfer windows generate a mixture of definitive clarifications and ongoing interest. Liverpool's explicit withdrawal of interest in Djed Spence narrows the speculation and will likely redirect attention to other full-back options or internal solutions. Meanwhile, Juventus pursuing Joshua Zirkzee on loan fits a pattern where big clubs employ temporary moves to reinforce attacking ranks without long-term commitment.

The scouting of Kaua Elias by Arsenal and Manchester United underlines the premium top clubs place on first-hand observation. Scouts are sent not only when a club intends to buy immediately, but to build dossiers, compare prospects with existing targets and gauge development trajectory. These processes often precede formal approaches, but they equally can end with clubs deciding not to proceed — so observation alone is an early stage, not a guarantee of transfer activity.

BBC Sport's Friday gossip offers a concise snapshot of transfer-market movement: Liverpool stepping away from the Spence story, Juventus exploring a loan for Zirkzee, and Premier League heavyweights keeping an eye on emerging talent. As with all transfer windows, these developments may evolve, but the BBC update sets the current shape of these particular stories.

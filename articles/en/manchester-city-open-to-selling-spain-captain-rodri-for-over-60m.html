Manchester City Open to Selling Spain Captain Rodri for Over £60m

8/7/2026 · By Koora Live

In a potentially surprising move, Manchester City are reportedly open to selling their star midfielder Rodri, with a price tag set at over £60 million, according to BBC Sport. As one of the Premier League’s most reliable performers and Spain’s national team captain, Rodri’s availability on the market will undoubtedly catch the interest of top clubs across Europe.

Rodri’s Role at Manchester City

Since joining Manchester City from Atlético Madrid in 2019, Rodri has established himself as a key component of Pep Guardiola's tactical system. A deep-lying midfielder with an exceptional ability to control the game’s tempo, Rodri played a pivotal role in City's domestic success, including multiple Premier League titles, and their maiden Champions League win in 2023. His calmness under pressure and precise passing have often made him the fulcrum of City’s midfield. As the captain of the Spanish national team, he represents leadership on and off the pitch.

What Rodri's Departure Could Mean for Manchester City

If Manchester City proceed to sell Rodri, it could signify a major reshuffle within Guardiola's squad. The Premier League champions are known for their meticulous squad planning and have never shied away from letting high-profile players leave if deemed necessary. While City would demand over £60 million for Rodri, replacing a player of his caliber would require not just financial outlay but also careful consideration of tactical balance. City's midfield depth includes players like Kalvin Phillips and Mateo Kovacic; however, neither fully replicates Rodri’s unique skill set.

Analysis

Manchester City’s reported willingness to sell Rodri raises intriguing questions about their transfer strategy. It appears that despite his pivotal contributions, the club may prioritize financial prudence or long-term squad evolution over maintaining their current talismanic midfielder. Analysts might argue that €60 million is a hefty price for any club targeting Rodri, but his pedigree and proven impact on both domestic and European football could justify such an investment. Moreover, this decision might reflect Guardiola's confidence in reconfiguring his midfield without compromising overall team performance.

Rodri’s potential availability on the transfer market promises to spark significant interest and debate throughout the footballing world. While Manchester City may benefit financially from his sale, losing a player of Rodri's caliber would undoubtedly pose challenges for Guardiola as he seeks to maintain his team’s dominance. Fans and analysts alike will closely watch how this story develops in the coming weeks.

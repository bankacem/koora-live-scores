Xabi Alonso Praises Cole Palmer After Chelsea's 3-2 Win Over Fulham

8/24/2026 · By Koora Live

Chelsea manager Xabi Alonso has heaped praise on rising star Cole Palmer following a standout performance against Fulham at Craven Cottage. The young playmaker played a decisive role in Chelsea's hard-fought 3-2 victory, scoring a goal and providing a crucial assist in the process.

Palmer Shines in a Thrilling London Derby

In a closely contested London derby, Chelsea edged Fulham 3-2 at Craven Cottage, with Cole Palmer proving instrumental to the Blues' success. The 21-year-old English midfielder not only found the back of the net but also supplied a key assist to help secure the win. His contributions underline his growing importance in Xabi Alonso's team and highlight his ability to make an impact on high-stakes occasions.

Xabi Alonso's Praise for the Rising Playmaker

After the match, Alonso didn't shy away from expressing his admiration for Palmer, lauding the young player for showcasing 'responsibility, maturity, and freedom' on the pitch. Such qualities are often hard to come by in younger players, and Alonso’s remarks indicate his confidence in Palmer's potential to become a pivotal player for Chelsea, a club known for nurturing some of England's finest footballing talents over the years.

Analysis

Chelsea's victory over Fulham and Cole Palmer's outstanding performance reflect a promising new chapter for the team under Xabi Alonso's leadership. Having recently taken over managerial duties, Alonso seems intent on building a side that balances experienced stars with emerging talent like Palmer. This approach could recall the success of previous Chelsea eras, where a blend of seasoned professionals and vibrant young players led the club to domestic and European glory. Palmer's decisive contribution in this match might mark the beginning of a breakthrough season for him, especially if he continues to repay the faith Alonso has placed in him with consistent performances on the big stage.

This victory against Fulham, highlighted by Cole Palmer's stellar performance, comes as a significant boost for Chelsea and their manager Xabi Alonso. As Palmer continues to grow under Alonso's guidance, his development could prove to be a key factor in Chelsea's pursuit of success this season.

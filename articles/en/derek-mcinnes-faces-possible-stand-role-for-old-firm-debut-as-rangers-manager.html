Derek McInnes Faces Possible Stand Role for Old Firm Debut as Rangers Manager

8/24/2026 · By Koora Live

Derek McInnes may face the prospect of watching his first Old Firm match as Rangers manager from the stands. This follows the scheduling of his touchline ban appeal hearing, which is set to take place on 31 August, according to BBC Sport.

Touchline Ban Appeal Could Impact McInnes' Old Firm Involvement

Derek McInnes, the recently appointed Rangers manager, is awaiting the outcome of an appeal that could influence his participation in one of Scotland's most iconic football fixtures, the Old Firm derby. The appeal, set for 31 August, pertains to a touchline ban that could prevent McInnes from managing his team at pitch-side in this high-stakes clash. The timing of this hearing means that McInnes’ role on match day remains uncertain, adding further intrigue to what is already one of the most closely watched fixtures in Scottish football.

The Stakes of the Old Firm Derby

The Old Firm derby, contested between Glasgow rivals Rangers and Celtic, is regarded as one of the most intense and significant rivalries in world football. The match is not only a sporting spectacle but also a deeply cultural event, reflecting historical, social, and sometimes even political divides in Scotland. For McInnes, this match represents a critical milestone in his managerial tenure at Rangers, given the importance of the fixture to the club's supporters and its potential implications in the battle for domestic supremacy. Should McInnes be confined to the stands, it could alter his managerial approach and potentially influence the outcome of this fiercely competitive encounter.

Analysis

The timing of the appeal raises questions about how prepared McInnes will be for his debut Old Firm derby experience, especially if he is relegated to the stands during the match. While some managers thrive on orchestrating their team from the touchline, others have proven it possible to influence proceedings effectively from a more removed vantage point. However, considering the intensity of an Old Firm derby atmosphere, direct touchline involvement could be crucial in motivating players and making tactical adjustments. How Rangers adapt to this potential scenario may offer early clues to McInnes' ability to manage under such high-pressure conditions.

As the appeal date approaches, the uncertainty surrounding Derek McInnes' touchline ban brings an added layer of intrigue to his first Old Firm derby as Rangers manager. Whether from the dugout or the stands, McInnes faces a monumental challenge in what is sure to be a defining moment in his managerial career at Rangers.

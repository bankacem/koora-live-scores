Liverpool Eye Ismaila Sarr After Brighton Decline Yankuba Minteh Offers

8/26/2026 · By Koora Live

Liverpool have reportedly set their sights on Crystal Palace winger Ismaila Sarr after their pursuit of Brighton & Hove Albion's Yankuba Minteh hit a roadblock. According to BBC Sport, the Reds’ bids for Minteh were turned down, prompting the club to explore alternative options in the market.

Liverpool Redirect Focus to Ismaila Sarr

Following a rejection of two bids for Brighton's Yankuba Minteh, Liverpool appear to have shifted their focus to Crystal Palace's Ismaila Sarr. The Senegalese international has previously drawn attention for his performances in both the Premier League and international competitions, making him a desirable option for several clubs. Known for his blistering pace, dribbling ability, and sharp attacking instincts, Sarr could bring a new dynamic to Liverpool’s already potent attack.

Liverpool's Interest in Minteh Stalls

BBC Sport revealed that Liverpool had submitted two offers for Yankuba Minteh, currently contracted with Brighton. However, both bids were firmly rejected by the Seagulls, who are known for their strong stance when it comes to retaining or selling their top talents. Minteh, a promising young winger, has been noted for his skill and potential, attributes that likely piqued Liverpool’s interest. Nevertheless, it seems Brighton's valuation of the player remains a significant barrier to any potential deal.

Analysis

This latest development in Liverpool’s transfer dealings highlights the club’s strong desire to bolster its attacking options, either to add depth or prepare for potential departures in the future. Ismaila Sarr’s experience in the Premier League, coupled with his international pedigree, makes him a strong candidate for a team looking to compete on both domestic and European fronts. On the other hand, Brighton's rejection of the bids for Yankuba Minteh aligns with their reputation for standing firm on their valuation of emerging talents, reflecting their long-term vision and strategic planning.

Liverpool's transfer strategy is continuing to unfold as they adjust their focus following Brighton’s rejection of their Minteh bids. A move for Ismaila Sarr could signify an exciting new chapter for the Reds, pending further developments in the transfer market.

Crystal Palace has a long tradition of identifying and nurturing young players. With the transfer window still open, Palace’s front office is reported to be negotiating on friendly terms with both the player and Union SG to secure a move before key rivals step in.

Anan Khalaili, born in 2003, has grown up in the Belgian football structure and has already showcased his talents with Union Saint‑Gilloise, a club competing in the Belgian First Division A. While specific statistics such as appearances or goals are not publicly disclosed in this report, Khalaili’s trajectory suggests he has earned regular first‑team minutes in Belgium and has drawn attention from clubs beyond national borders.

He is 21, which places him at a critical phase of development where regular top‑flight minutes can significantly accelerate his growth. Analysis

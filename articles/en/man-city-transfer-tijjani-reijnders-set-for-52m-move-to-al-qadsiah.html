Man City transfer: Tijjani Reijnders set for £52m move to Al-Qadsiah

8/13/2026 · By Koora Live

Manchester City midfielder Tijjani Reijnders is set to leave the Premier League champions after just a year at the club, with BBC Sport reporting that a £52m move has been agreed with Saudi Pro-League side Al-Qadsiah. The development adds to a busy transfer narrative involving high-profile moves to Saudi Arabia and will be watched closely by supporters and pundits alike.

What the BBC Sport report says

According to BBC Sport, Manchester City midfielder Tijjani Reijnders is set to depart after a year with the club following the agreement of a £52m transfer to Al-Qadsiah in the Saudi Pro League. The outlet describes the transaction as agreed, indicating that both clubs have reached terms over the fee. Beyond that core information — the player, the selling club, the buying club and the transfer amount — BBC Sport is the named source for the report.

Context: transfers to the Saudi Pro League and what it means for squads

The Saudi Pro League is the top tier of football in Saudi Arabia and in recent seasons has been the destination for several significant international moves. Transfers from major European clubs to the Saudi top flight have drawn attention due to the financial terms involved and the league's ambition to raise its global profile. For selling clubs in Europe, such moves can represent substantial transaction values that allow them to reinvest in their squads or balance budgets.

From a squad-management perspective, the departure of a midfielder who has been at a club for around a year naturally leads to questions about replacement plans and long-term strategy. Midfielders typically provide balance between defence and attack, contribute to ball circulation, pressing and transitional play — roles that are valued in elite squads. How Manchester City chooses to deploy resources after this reported agreement will be a point of interest for supporters and analysts.

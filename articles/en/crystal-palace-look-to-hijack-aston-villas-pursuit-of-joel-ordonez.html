Crystal Palace Look to Hijack Aston Villa's Pursuit of Joel Ordonez

8/26/2026 · By Koora Live

Crystal Palace have intensified their pursuit of Club Brugge defender Joel Ordonez, a player they have reportedly been monitoring for an extended period. This development comes as Premier League rivals Aston Villa also show interest in the promising centre-back, particularly after encountering difficulties in securing a deal for Manchester City loanee Taylor Harwood-Bellis.

Joel Ordonez: A Rising Talent in Demand

Joel Ordonez is a player who has been catching attention in European football, currently plying his trade at Belgian club Club Brugge. The young defender has emerged as a player with significant potential, drawing interest from major clubs like Aston Villa and Crystal Palace. His performances in Belgium's Jupiler Pro League have marked him as a solid defensive prospect who could adapt to the physical and fast-paced environment of the Premier League. Palace have reportedly had their eyes on him over an extended period, underlining his appeal as a long-term investment for clubs aiming to strengthen their backlines.

Villa’s Defensive Challenges Could Give Palace an Opening

Aston Villa’s move for Ordonez appears to stem from complications in their efforts to secure Taylor Harwood-Bellis. The English defender enjoyed a breakout campaign on loan at Burnley last season, helping them secure promotion to the Premier League. Despite that success, Villa have reportedly struggled to agree to terms with his parent club, Manchester City. This uncertainty over Harwood-Bellis has seemingly opened the door for them to focus on alternatives, with Ordonez being a top candidate. However, Villa now face direct competition from Palace, who have used their scouting network to keep Ordonez firmly on their radar.

Analysis

While both clubs are looking to bolster their defensive options, the reported interest from Crystal Palace in Joel Ordonez showcases their commitment to adding youthful promise to their squad. Under the experienced management of Roy Hodgson, Palace are focusing on securing their long-term defensive line. Aston Villa, on the other hand, appear to be chasing more immediate solutions to bridge the gap to European qualification under Unai Emery’s ambitious project. The outcome of this transfer pursuit could shape the defensive dynamics of both sides for the foreseeable future, especially with Ordonez's potential to become a key player in the Premier League.

The battle between Crystal Palace and Aston Villa for Joel Ordonez highlights the competitive nature of the Premier League transfer market. Both sides are keen to lure the Club Brugge defender, but only time will tell which club secures the services of this promising talent. His next destination could play a significant part in shaping the fortunes of either side this season.

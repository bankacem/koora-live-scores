Manchester United Falter in Premier League Opener: Carrick Admits 'We Didn't Click'

8/22/2026 · By Koora Live

Michael Carrick's first match of the 2026-27 Premier League season as Manchester United head coach ended in disappointment as his side suffered a 2-0 defeat at the hands of newly promoted Hull City. Speaking after the game, Carrick acknowledged that his team struggled to find cohesion on the pitch, describing their performance as one where they 'didn't click.'

Manchester United's Tough Start to the Season

Manchester United entered the 2026-27 Premier League campaign with high expectations under Michael Carrick's stewardship. A former United midfielder and a key member of the club's coaching staff in recent years, Carrick has been tasked with bringing the Red Devils back to the summit of English football. However, their opening fixture against Hull City proved to be a stern reminder of the challenges ahead.

Hull City, making their return to the Premier League after clinching promotion last season, delivered a confident and disciplined performance on home turf. Their 2-0 victory was a strong statement from the Tigers, underscoring their intent to make an impact at the top level. For United, the loss exposed flaws that will need addressing sooner rather than later if they are to compete with the league's leading contenders.

Carrick's Reflection: 'We Didn't Click'

Speaking to BBC Sport in the aftermath of the defeat, Carrick admitted that his side lacked the cohesion and fluidity required to impose themselves on the match. The comment 'we didn’t click' encapsulated a performance where Manchester United struggled to break down a resilient and well-organized Hull City defense.

While Manchester United's squad boasts a mix of seasoned international stars and promising young talents, finding the right balance and rhythm is a work in progress. For Carrick, the challenge will be to quickly iron out these issues as the early weeks of the Premier League season leave little room for error.

Analysis

This early setback highlights the unpredictable nature of the Premier League, where newly promoted teams such as Hull City often punch above their weight in the opening weeks of the season. For Manchester United, it raises familiar concerns about their consistency and cutting edge in key moments.

Carrick's candid acknowledgment of his team's struggles can be seen as a positive indicator of his analytical approach, but he now faces the tough task of turning words into action. The result reinforces the need for tactical clarity and better execution, especially as the Red Devils look to mount a serious challenge for silverware this season.

As Manchester United and Michael Carrick regroup following their opening-day defeat, the result serves as a wake-up call for the team. With the Premier League season underway, the pressure is on to bounce back quickly and establish themselves as serious contenders. For Hull City, however, this victory is a dream start to their campaign, offering a glimpse of their potential to compete in the top tier.

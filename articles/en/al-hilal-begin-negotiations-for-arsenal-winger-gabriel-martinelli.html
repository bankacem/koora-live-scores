Al-Hilal Begin Negotiations for Arsenal Winger Gabriel Martinelli

8/23/2026 · By Koora Live

Saudi Pro League giants Al-Hilal have reportedly initiated talks with Arsenal over the potential transfer of Brazilian winger Gabriel Martinelli. This development, reported by BBC Sport, signals yet another significant move by a Saudi club in their bid to attract elite football talent.

Al-Hilal's Growing Influence in the Transfer Market

Al-Hilal, one of Saudi Arabia's most decorated football clubs, has been making headlines in recent months with bold moves in the transfer market. Known for their rich history, the club boasts an impressive record in domestic and continental competitions, including multiple AFC Champions League titles. By pursuing Gabriel Martinelli, Al-Hilal signals their ongoing ambition to bring high-profile players from Europe's top leagues to bolster their squad and elevate the league's global reputation. Martinelli, with his immense talent and versatility as a forward, would certainly be a marquee signing for the Riyadh-based side.

Martinelli's Rise at Arsenal and Role in the Current Squad

Gabriel Martinelli has been a standout performer for Arsenal since joining the club from Brazilian side Ituano in 2019. Known for his electrifying pace, creativity, and eye for goal, the young forward has quickly become an integral part of Mikel Arteta's team. Martinelli played a key role in helping Arsenal challenge for major honors in recent seasons, often operating from the left wing but showcasing the versatility to play centrally as well. His contributions have endeared him to the Gunners' faithful, with many considering him as one of the Premier League's brightest young talents.

Analysis

The move for Gabriel Martinelli reflects the Saudi Pro League's aggressive strategy to become a prominent player on the global football stage. Over the past year, clubs like Al-Hilal have concentrated efforts on securing world-class talent by offering lucrative deals. If successful, luring a player of Martinelli's caliber would further enhance the league's appeal and competitiveness. However, convincing Arsenal to part ways with one of their core players will likely be a challenge. Arsenal are currently aiming to maintain a squad capable of pushing for Premier League and European glory. Allowing an emerging star like Martinelli to leave would imply a significant reshuffle of Arteta's tactical plans and an urgent need to find an appropriate replacement.

The reported negotiations between Al-Hilal and Arsenal over Gabriel Martinelli hint at the Saudi club's growing ambitions in global football. Whether they succeed in their pursuit remains to be seen, but the transfer talks underscore the increasing allure of the Saudi Pro League for top-tier talent.

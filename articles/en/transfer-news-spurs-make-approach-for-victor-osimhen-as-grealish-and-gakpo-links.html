Transfer News: Spurs Make Approach for Victor Osimhen as Grealish and Gakpo Links Surface

8/2/2026 · By Koora Live

Tottenham Hotspur have reportedly made an approach for Napoli striker Victor Osimhen, while Atletico Madrid are said to be considering a move for Manchester City winger Jack Grealish. BBC Sport carried the initial report, which also notes that Liverpool forward Cody Gakpo has held positive talks with Spurs. These are approaches and discussions reported in the media rather than completed deals.

Spurs' reported approach for Victor Osimhen

According to BBC Sport, Tottenham Hotspur have made an approach for Victor Osimhen. Osimhen is widely recognised as a leading centre-forward in European football and currently plays for Napoli in Italy's Serie A. An approach in transfer terms generally means a club has opened formal contact — often to express interest or begin negotiations — but it does not necessarily indicate that an agreement has been reached. For Spurs, who play their home games at the Tottenham Hotspur Stadium and routinely target attacking reinforcements, interest in a high-profile striker fits a familiar recruitment pattern.

Atletico Madrid eye Jack Grealish and Gakpo talks with Spurs

The BBC report also states Atletico Madrid are considering a move for Manchester City winger Jack Grealish. Grealish is a high-profile England international known for his play on the wing and attacking midfield roles, and Manchester City are one of Europe’s top clubs. Separately, the same report notes that Liverpool forward Cody Gakpo has held positive talks with Tottenham. Gakpo, a forward for Liverpool who has featured for the Netherlands national team, appears to be part of the broader chatter connecting Spurs with attacking options. It is important to emphasise that consideration and talks are early stages in transfer activity and do not equate to completed transactions.

Analysis

From a strategic perspective, the reports point to Spurs prioritising forward options — a typical stance for a club aiming to compete at the top end of the Premier League and in European competitions. Victor Osimhen's profile as a powerful, mobile central striker would, on paper, offer a different profile to other attacking players linked with Tottenham in recent windows. Atletico Madrid's interest in Jack Grealish suggests they are exploring ways to refresh or diversify their wing and attacking midfield options, while Liverpool's Cody Gakpo being linked to Spurs through positive talks indicates Tottenham are pursuing multiple avenues to strengthen their forward lines. All of these reports stem from early-market manoeuvres; transfer approaches and consideration rarely produce immediate outcomes and can evolve in many directions before any final decision is made.

These reports, first compiled by BBC Sport, underline that several high-profile clubs are exploring attacking options as the transfer market heats up. Approaches, considerations and positive talks are part of the early rhythm of transfer activity — significant developments may follow, but at this stage the stories reflect interest and dialogue rather than completed transactions.

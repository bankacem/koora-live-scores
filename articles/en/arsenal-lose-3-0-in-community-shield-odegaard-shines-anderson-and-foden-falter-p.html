Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Community Shield?

The Community Shield is an annual football match held at Wembley Stadium in London, featuring the reigning Premier League champions against the FA Cup holders. It serves as the opening fixture of the English football season and has been played since 1908, evolving into a high‑profile curtain‑raiser that showcases new players and provides a platform for clubs to assess early form.

Nottingham Forest Secure £22m Deal for Crystal Palace's Daniel Munoz

8/29/2026 · By Koora Live

In a significant move during the transfer window, Nottingham Forest have come to an agreement with Crystal Palace to sign Daniel Munoz. The transfer, valued at a minimum of £22 million, underscores Forest's ambitions as they look to strengthen their squad for the challenges ahead.

Nottingham Forest's £22m Deal for Munoz

Nottingham Forest have bolstered their squad with the acquisition of Daniel Munoz from fellow Premier League outfit Crystal Palace. The two clubs have reached an agreement worth a minimum of £22 million for the deal, as reported by BBC Sport. This move highlights Forest's intention to stay competitive in England's top-flight football league, where depth and quality in the squad are crucial for sustained success.

Daniel Munoz has garnered a reputation as a reliable and dynamic player during his time with Crystal Palace. Known for his prowess in both defense and attack, he has proven himself capable of adapting to the physical and tactical demands of Premier League football. With this transfer to Forest, Munoz brings a wealth of top-level experience that could be instrumental to his new team's campaign. Forest fans will undoubtedly hope he plays a pivotal role in solidifying their standing in the league.

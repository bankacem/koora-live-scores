Chris Sutton Predicts Premier League Opening Weekend Matches

8/21/2026 · By Koora Live

As the new Premier League season kicks off, BBC Sport's football expert Chris Sutton has made his predictions for the opening weekend fixtures. Joining him in this exercise is Liam Fray, the frontman of indie band The Courteeners, alongside BBC readers and AI, to forecast how the games will unfold.

Chris Sutton: A Familiar Figure in Football Analysis

Chris Sutton, a former Premier League-winning striker with Blackburn Rovers, has become a respected figure in football punditry. Known for his candid opinions and sharp analysis, Sutton applies his on-field experience to offer insights into matches, players, and team strategies in his role with BBC Sport. His weekly predictions have become a staple for football fans eager to measure their own opinions against a seasoned professional.

Liam Fray Brings Musical Flair to Football Predictions

Joining Sutton in this week's predictions is Liam Fray, lead singer and guitarist of The Courteeners, a celebrated indie band hailing from Manchester. While widely known in music circles, Fray is also a passionate football enthusiast, particularly about his Manchester roots. His collaboration with Sutton and the BBC adds a fresh element, blending the worlds of football and entertainment to create a unique take on the Premier League's opening weekend.

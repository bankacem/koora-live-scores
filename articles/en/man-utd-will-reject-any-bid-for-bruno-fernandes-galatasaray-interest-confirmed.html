Man Utd Will Reject Any Bid for Bruno Fernandes — Galatasaray Interest Confirmed

8/17/2026 · By Koora Live

Manchester United have no intention of selling captain Bruno Fernandes, the club told reporters via BBC Sport, even as Turkish giants Galatasaray are reportedly preparing to submit an offer. The situation underlines a clear stance from United’s hierarchy at a moment when the Portuguese midfielder’s future has attracted outside attention.

BBC Sport report: United to turn down offers

According to a report from BBC Sport, Manchester United will not entertain proposals for their captain, Bruno Fernandes. The outlet says the club has no intention of selling the 31-year-old and would reject any bid that arrives. The same report states Galatasaray — the Istanbul-based Turkish club — are prepared to lodge a bid for Fernandes later this week. Those are the core facts as presented by BBC Sport.

How this fits into the wider football landscape

Bruno Fernandes is a high-profile figure at Manchester United, serving as captain and a key presence in midfield. Manchester United are one of England’s most prominent clubs, based at Old Trafford, while Galatasaray are among the biggest and most successful teams in Turkey, with a large fan base and regular participation in both domestic and continental competitions. Transfer interest in established players often arises from clubs seeking to boost leadership and creative quality in their squads, and high-profile approaches can test the resolve of a selling club.

Clubs commonly take a firm stance on key players they regard as central to their plans; publicly rejecting bids can be a signal of long-term intent to keep an influential squad member. Equally, interest from another major club — domestic or international — is not uncommon and can reflect differing ambitions and resources between clubs.

Analysis

United’s reported stance suggests they view Fernandes as integral to their short- and medium-term plans. Keeping a captain and experienced midfielder can be important for squad stability, leadership on the pitch and continuity in the dressing room. From Galatasaray’s perspective, lodging a bid would indicate ambition to strengthen their squad with a player who brings leadership and creative influence. Analysts might read this as a straightforward clash of objectives: one club signalling continuity, the other testing options to upgrade their squad. Either way, the BBC Sport report frames the immediate outlook as one in which Manchester United do not want to do business over Fernandes.

The BBC Sport update makes Manchester United’s position clear: they do not intend to sell Bruno Fernandes, even with reported interest from Galatasaray. Any further developments will depend on whether Galatasaray proceed with a formal offer and whether United maintain their publicly stated stance.

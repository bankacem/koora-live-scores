Luka Modric Aims to Extend Croatia Career Past 41st Birthday

9/5/2026 · By Koora Live

Croatian midfield maestro Luka Modric, now 41 years old, is showing no signs of slowing down as he looks to prolong his storied international career. According to BBC Sport, the veteran playmaker has expressed his determination to remain a key figure for the Croatia national team, including in their upcoming UEFA Nations League fixtures against England and beyond.

Modric's Remarkable Longevity in International Football

Luka Modric's enduring career is nothing short of extraordinary. With an international debut dating back to 2006, Modric has become the heartbeat of the Croatian midfield for well over a decade. Known for his exceptional technical ability, vision, and leadership, he has been a consistent presence on the international stage, leading Croatia to numerous memorable campaigns. Most notably, he inspired his nation to a runners-up finish at the 2018 FIFA World Cup in Russia and a third-place showing at the 2022 tournament in Qatar—a testament to his elite skillset and remarkable fitness levels even as he entered his late 30s.

Focus on the Upcoming Nations League Matches

Modric's ambition to continue representing Croatia comes ahead of their UEFA Nations League fixtures, which will see them face high-profile opponents such as England. The Nations League, a relatively recent addition to international football, provides European nations with an opportunity to test themselves in competitive matches outside of traditional qualification campaigns and major tournaments. Croatia, often punching above their weight on the world stage, will undoubtedly look to Modric's experience and creativity to steer them through these challenges. His ability to control games from midfield, even at his advanced age, remains invaluable to the team’s tactical setup.

Analysis

Modric’s decision to continue for Croatia into his 41st year highlights not only his personal drive but also his deep connection with his national team. Playing for Croatia has always been a point of pride for the midfielder, and even after achieving so much in his career, he seems as motivated as ever to contribute. Analysts may view his longevity as a rare feat in modern football, where the physical demands of the game often force players to retire earlier. His continued presence could also serve as a source of inspiration for younger members of the team, offering a living example of professionalism and consistency. However, there will naturally be concerns about whether he can maintain his performance levels amidst the rigors of international competition, especially against top-tier teams like England.

Luka Modric’s desire to extend his international career beyond his 41st birthday showcases the remarkable attributes that have defined his journey: resilience, passion, and a relentless pursuit of excellence. As he prepares for Croatia’s upcoming Nations League matches, fans and pundits alike will undoubtedly keep a keen eye on one of football’s enduring icons.

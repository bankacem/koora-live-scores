Andoni Iraola Highlights Lack of Wingers in Liverpool Squad Ahead of New Season

8/21/2026 · By Koora Live

Liverpool manager Andoni Iraola has raised concerns about squad depth on the flanks as the new football season approaches. Speaking to BBC Sport, Iraola expressed that his team currently lacks sufficient options in wide positions but remains optimistic about resolving the issue in due course.

Iraola Speaks on Liverpool’s Wing Options

In an interview with BBC Sport, Andoni Iraola, newly appointed manager of Liverpool, admitted that the club is facing a shortage of wingers as they gear up for the upcoming season. Despite this challenge, Iraola appeared undeterred, expressing confidence in the team's ability to find solutions to this apparent gap in the squad. This revelation comes as football fans eagerly anticipate the opening fixtures of what promises to be another gripping campaign in English football.

Transfer Concerns and Squad Balancing

A lack of depth in specific areas, such as the wings, can be a crucial factor in a team's performance over a long and grueling campaign. While full details of Liverpool's transfer strategy under Iraola are yet to emerge, the manager's acknowledgment of this issue hints at ongoing discussions that could lead to potential reinforcements. Liverpool, known globally for their high-intensity, attack-driven football, have historically relied on versatile players like Mohamed Salah and Sadio Mané in wide positions. However, managing injuries, form, and fixture congestion will require a larger pool of players who can maintain the team's tactical demands.

Analysis

It is not uncommon for managers, especially new appointments like Andoni Iraola, to assess squad depth and highlight gaps during the preseason period. The revelation about Liverpool's shortage of wingers underscores the challenges teams face when they aim to balance quality, depth, and tactical flexibility. Liverpool, long admired for their potent wing play under previous managers, will likely aim to address this concern either through player development or in the transfer market. Iraola's confidence in finding a solution suggests that discussions—whether tactical, strategic, or recruitment-related—are already underway internally. His ability to manage this issue effectively could be a key subplot in his debut season at Anfield.

Andoni Iraola’s acknowledgment of Liverpool’s wing shortage adds an intriguing layer to the narrative surrounding the club’s preparations for the new season. Fans will now look forward to seeing how the manager addresses this challenge and whether it will impact Liverpool’s performance in the months ahead.

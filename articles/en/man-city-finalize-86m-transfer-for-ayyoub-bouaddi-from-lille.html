Man City Finalize £86m Transfer for Ayyoub Bouaddi from Lille

8/26/2026 · By Koora Live

Manchester City have completed the signing of Moroccan midfielder Ayyoub Bouaddi from Lille for a reported fee of £85.6 million, according to BBC Sport. The star player has penned a five-year contract with the reigning Premier League champions, marking a blockbuster move that could shake up the transfer window.

Ayyoub Bouaddi: A Big-Money Arrival at the Etihad

Manchester City’s acquisition of Ayyoub Bouaddi underscores their ambition to dominate both domestic and European competitions. The Moroccan midfielder joins a squad replete with world-class talent under the guidance of renowned manager Pep Guardiola. With a significant investment of £85.6 million, City have signaled their belief in Bouaddi’s potential to bring an added dimension to their team. Having earned praise for his performances at Lille, where he showcased not only technical proficiency but also tactical adaptability, Bouaddi could fit seamlessly into Guardiola’s possession-based, high-pressing system. The addition of the 23-year-old is expected to fortify Manchester City’s midfield engine as they aim to contend for multiple trophies across four competitions.

Manchester City's Spending and Transfer Strategy

This signing continues Manchester City’s trend of investing heavily in young, dynamic players who can thrive under Guardiola’s demanding style. The club, since its takeover by the City Football Group in 2008, has consistently pursued top-tier talent to compete at the highest level. Bouaddi’s move aligns with City’s preference for players capable of influencing games in multiple phases, whether through incisive passing, technical dribbling, or defensive work rate.

Bouaddi joins a midfield that already boasts the likes of Kevin De Bruyne, Bernardo Silva, and Rodri—each pivotal during City’s treble-winning campaign. His arrival suggests Guardiola is planning for both squad depth and succession planning. As City battle on multiple fronts, with the Premier League, FA Cup, League Cup, and Champions League all in their sights this season, Bouaddi’s presence could be vital to maintaining their competitive edge during fixture-packed months.

Analysis

This transfer reflects Manchester City’s forward-thinking approach to team building. By investing heavily in a young player like Ayyoub Bouaddi, City are demonstrating their faith in his ability to deliver both immediately and in the long term. His skill set and high-level experience in French football should help him integrate into Guardiola’s system, but he will face significant pressure to justify his hefty price tag.

Analysts might view this as part of City’s wider strategy to refresh their squad and maintain their dominance. With other European giants also active in the transfer market, securing Bouaddi early could give City a crucial edge in the evolving football landscape. However, it remains to be seen if he can adapt quickly to the Premier League—a league renowned for its pace, physicality, and competitiveness.

With the capture of Ayyoub Bouaddi, Manchester City have further bolstered their star-studded squad. As the Moroccan midfielder prepares to make his mark at the Etihad, fans and analysts will be eager to see how he adjusts to Premier League football and contributes to City’s quest for continued dominance.

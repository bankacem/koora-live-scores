Is Erling Haaland's £15.5m FPL Price Justified?

8/19/2026 · By Koora Live

As the Fantasy Premier League (FPL) season gets underway, Manchester City's star striker Erling Haaland has become a hot topic among FPL managers. With a hefty £15.5m price tag, many are pondering whether the prolific Norwegian forward is a must-have asset or a luxury best avoided. BBC Sport has highlighted FPL expert Gianni Buttice's analysis on this pressing question for fantasy football enthusiasts.

Erling Haaland: A Goalscoring Phenomenon

Erling Haaland has cemented himself as one of the most lethal forwards in world football. Since his move to Manchester City, he has not only adapted to the competitive nature of the Premier League but also thrived, consistently finding the back of the net across all competitions. Known for his clinical finishing, blistering speed, and physical presence, Haaland has become a focal point of City's attack under the tactical brilliance of Pep Guardiola. His performances have already etched his name among the league's elite. This reputation undoubtedly explains his sky-high valuation in FPL, where goals and assists translate directly into points for managers.

Understanding Haaland's £15.5m FPL Price Tag

At £15.5m, Haaland is not only one of the most expensive players in this season's FPL but also represents a significant chunk of the £100m budget managers have to construct their squads. This figure reflects his incredible ability to deliver match-defining moments and accumulate points. Players at this price level are expected to perform consistently and offer a reliable captaincy option week in, week out. However, splurging on Haaland necessitates sacrifices elsewhere in the team, forcing managers to rely on cheaper, less proven options for balance across the squad—a dilemma that leads to the burning question: is his inclusion worth compromising depth elsewhere?

Analysis

Haaland's £15.5m price in FPL signifies the value placed on premium strikers capable of delivering consistently in the highly competitive Premier League. Many managers may view him as a cornerstone of their team, particularly due to Manchester City's style of play, which is geared toward creating high-quality scoring chances. On the flip side, such a high price could deter those who value squad depth and flexibility, especially when alternative forwards or midfielders offer decent potential at a lower cost. This balancing act of risk and reward is central to the FPL experience, making Haaland's inclusion both a gamble and an opportunity for fantasy managers to consider carefully.

Erling Haaland's £15.5m FPL price tag places managers at a crossroads—his undeniable ability to deliver points makes him a tempting purchase, yet the cost forces tough decisions on squad structure. Whether he proves to be a worthwhile investment or not will ultimately depend on each manager's strategy and risk appetite over the course of the season.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is Erling Haaland priced so high in FPL?

Haaland's £15.5m price reflects his consistent goalscoring ability, his importance in Manchester City's attack, and his potential to be a reliable captaincy option for double points.

What should FPL managers consider before selecting Haaland?

Managers need to weigh the impact of Haaland's high cost on their overall squad depth, fixture difficulty, and team structure while also evaluating his potential for delivering high points.

Is it worth building an FPL team around Haaland?

This depends on individual strategy. While Haaland's goalscoring record makes him an appealing option, his high price means investing in cheaper players elsewhere, which could affect the team's overall balance.

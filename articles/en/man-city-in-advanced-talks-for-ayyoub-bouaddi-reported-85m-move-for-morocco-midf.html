Manchester City are a Premier League club known for competing at the top of English football and for making significant investments in squad development. Lille are a club in France's top division, Ligue 1, that has a history of developing young talent and selling players to larger European clubs.

Reported fees in those deals can vary widely depending on perceived potential, contractual situations and add-ons; any single reported figure should be understood as part of negotiation reporting until clubs issue formal statements.

Celtic's Struggles Highlighted in Painful Champions League Loss to LASK

8/26/2026 · By Koora Live

Celtic's Champions League journey hit another stumbling block, as their performance against LASK left fans and players reeling. Club captain Callum McGregor didn't hold back in his criticism of his team after their disheartening defeat, providing a rare moment of clarity on what was otherwise a disappointing night for the Scottish champions.

A Night to Forget for Celtic in Europe

Celtic's encounter with Austrian side LASK in the Champions League once again exposed major weaknesses in a team that has struggled to find its footing on the European stage in recent years. Despite the optimism that often accompanies nights under the floodlights of Europe’s premier club competition, Celtic ended up enduring a tough experience. The Scottish champions, a club with a proud history that includes a famous European Cup triumph in 1967, have found the modern-day Champions League to be an unforgiving arena.

The performance against LASK was described by BBC Sport as 'spineless,' emphasizing the lack of fight and cohesion within the team. In such high-stakes fixtures, the ability to rise to the occasion is critical, but this was an area where Celtic appeared woefully unprepared. Mistakes and general disarray plagued their effort, allowing their opponents to capitalize on opportunities and exploit vulnerabilities.

McGregor’s Stern Rebuke Points to Deeper Issues

Following the final whistle, Celtic captain Callum McGregor delivered a stinging critique of his team-mates, showcasing his frustration with what he saw as a lack of responsiveness and resilience on a key European night. McGregor's comments highlight the growing concern about Celtic’s inability to perform consistently against quality opposition on a grand stage.

The midfielder's actions not only underscored his personal commitment to the team but also hinted at potential underlying issues within the squad. His pointed remarks may signal internal problems with mentality or preparedness that need urgent addressing if Celtic aim to hold their own in Europe. Leadership from figures like McGregor will be crucial for the club to navigate difficult moments, but it is clear that more than words will be needed to turn the tide.

Analysis

This troubling loss to LASK paints a concerning picture for Celtic's ongoing struggles in European competitions. Despite their domestic dominance in recent years, translating Scottish Premiership success onto the Champions League stage has been a recurring challenge. The gap between their domestic performances and their European results suggests an urgent need for a reassessment of their approach to recruitment, tactics, and preparation.

McGregor’s remarks could prove pivotal in sparking a change in mentality within the squad, yet it will take more than just strong words to address systemic deficiencies. The club’s faithful fan base, who have long supported their team through thick and thin, will be hoping for a swift and meaningful response from both the players and the management. This defeat could serve as a wake-up call to identify and rectify the issues standing in the way of progress.

Celtic’s faltering Champions League campaign lays bare the challenges the team currently faces both on and off the pitch. While McGregor’s leadership remains a positive constant, the club must urgently address its vulnerabilities to reclaim its place among Europe’s elite. Time will tell if this defeat catalyzes the necessary changes or simply adds to the frustration of fans expecting more from their beloved team.

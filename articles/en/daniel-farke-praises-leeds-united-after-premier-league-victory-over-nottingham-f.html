Daniel Farke Praises Leeds United After Premier League Victory Over Nottingham Forest

8/22/2026 · By Koora Live

Leeds United commenced their 2026-27 Premier League campaign in impressive fashion with a narrow yet vital 1-0 win over Nottingham Forest. Manager Daniel Farke expressed his satisfaction with the result and lauded his players, emphasizing the importance of the victory, which was secured through Anton Stach’s well-taken free-kick.

Leeds United Edge Forest in Season Opener

Leeds United bolted out of the blocks in the new Premier League season with a disciplined 1-0 win over Nottingham Forest. The pivotal moment came from Anton Stach, whose precise free-kick found the back of the net and proved to be decisive. This hard-fought win underlines Leeds' intentions to begin the season on a strong note under the stewardship of Daniel Farke. The German manager, known for his tactical nous and dynamic coaching style, displayed his ability to galvanize his squad during high-pressure games.

Farke Full of Praise for His Squad

After the match, Daniel Farke was effusive in his praise for the team, stating that he was 'very happy' with their performance. His comments indicate a sense of optimism within the Leeds camp. This victory not only gives Leeds a valuable three points to start the campaign but also sets a confident tone for the challenges ahead. Over his managerial career, Farke has been recognized for nurturing team cohesion and allowing players to excel in structured systems, qualities that were evident in Leeds’ resolute display against Forest.

Analysis

Leeds United’s victory in their first game of the 2026-27 Premier League season showcases their readiness to compete at the highest level. After earning promotion to the Premier League, which is one of football’s most competitive leagues, maintaining confidence and securing points early in the campaign is critical. This win, achieved through a disciplined performance and a moment of individual brilliance from Anton Stach, reflects both the team’s potential and the positive influence of Daniel Farke’s management. Farke’s praise for his players suggests that Leeds are building a foundation of resilience and determination, qualities that could help them navigate the demands of a grueling season.

Leeds United’s opening-day triumph against Nottingham Forest could serve as a springboard for a strong Premier League campaign under Daniel Farke. With an early three points on the board and evident cohesion within the squad, Leeds will undoubtedly aim to build on this promising start as they seek to make an impact in the top flight.

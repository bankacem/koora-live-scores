Man Utd Pay Tribute to Late Fan and Officer PC Matthew Blades at Old Trafford

8/30/2026 · By Koora Live

Manchester United are set to honor the memory of PC Matthew Blades, a devoted supporter of the club, who tragically passed away in a crash on the A66. His family, including his widow and two children, will be present at Old Trafford during a Premier League fixture against Ipswich, as the club commemorates his legacy.

A Heartfelt Tribute to a Proud Manchester United Fan

PC Matthew Blades, described as a 'proud Red,' tragically lost his life in a road accident on the A66. Known for his dedication to both his profession and his beloved football club, his loss has deeply affected those who knew him. In a gesture of solidarity and respect, Manchester United plans to honor his memory at their iconic home of Old Trafford. His family — his widow and two children — will be present during the Premier League game against Ipswich, reflecting both their personal connection to the club and its effort to support the family during this difficult time.

Manchester United’s Tradition of Honoring Fans and Heroes

Manchester United, one of the world's most followed football clubs, has a long history of standing by its community during times of need. As a club deeply rooted in its local and global fanbase, tributes such as this showcase their recognition of those who form the backbone of their support. Old Trafford, known as the 'Theatre of Dreams,' has hosted many emotional moments over the years, and this gesture adds to its legacy as a place where football transcends the sport to touch lives in meaningful ways. By welcoming PC Blades’ family to the stadium, the club aims to express its condolences and appreciation for one of its own.

Manchester City bolsters goalkeeping options with Geronimo Rulli signing from Marseille

8/12/2026 · By Koora Live

Manchester City have confirmed the arrival of Argentine goalkeeper Geronimo Rulli, signing him from top French club Marseille. The move was reported by BBC Sport and adds depth to the Bees’ back‑line as they look to consolidate their position among the very best clubs across Europe.

Rulli, known for his shot‑stopping ability and composure under pressure, has spent the last several seasons developing a reputation in Ligue 1. The City transfer brings a player who has experience in both the World Cup and the European Champions League into an environment where competition for the first‑choice goalkeeper spot is fierce. The acquisition underscores manager Pep Guardiola’s willingness to secure backup options that can step in during a championship‑pursuing campaign.

A strategic addition to Manchester City's goalkeeping roster

The Spanish club’s goalkeeping hierarchy features world‑class nominees: Ederson Santana de Moraes has been the first‑choice over recent seasons, while Andrei Lunga and other backup options have been shuffled between loan spells and squad rotation. By bringing in Rulli, the club strengthens the squad depth behind Ederson’s fingers.

Manchester City’s philosophy, under Pep Guardiola, is to assemble a roster that is capable of being competitive on multiple fronts. The decision to sign Rulli from the French capital club Marseille aligns with this objective, offering a fit between the high technical demands of City’s style and a keeper who thrives on organised, high‑press systems. Rulli has represented Argentina at various youth and senior levels, displaying a track record of performing against elite opposition.

Marseille’s departure: A well‑known competitive Ligue 1 environment

Olympique de Marseille, established in 1899, has competed in France’s top flight for decades. Their home ground, Parc des Princes, is one of the most iconic stadiums in Europe. Rulli spent several seasons with the club, enjoying the attention of a passionate fan base and diverse managerial styles.

Since the pluralistic nature of Ligue 1, players are often exposed to high‑level tactical variations, making them comfortable under intense directives. Rulli’s time in the French league helped him develop a disciplined demeanor that is suited to the demands of Manchester City’s high‑pressing, possession‑based play. Furthermore, Marseille’s participation in the Champions League and other European competitions often places the team against top-tier opposition, providing a practical testbed for Rulli’s shot‑stopping capabilities.

Although the exact transfer fee and contract details remain undisclosed, the extension does signal that both clubs view the partnership favourably. Rulli’s integration into City will be monitored closely as the club attempts to maintain a stable defensive unit throughout the 2024‑25 campaign.

Liverpool Sporting Director Richard Hughes Departs for Al-Hilal Role

9/5/2026 · By Koora Live

Liverpool's sporting director, Richard Hughes, has stepped down from his position at the Premier League club. As reported by BBC Sport, Hughes will now take on a new challenge at Saudi Arabian side Al-Hilal. This move marks a notable development in Liverpool's backroom structure while further highlighting the growing pull of Saudi clubs in football's global landscape.

Richard Hughes Departs Liverpool for Saudi Venture

Liverpool Football Club will bid farewell to their sporting director Richard Hughes, who is set to take up a new executive role with Saudi Arabian club Al-Hilal. Hughes, whose tenure has been key to shaping Liverpool’s recruitment and sustainability strategies, leaves at a time when Saudi Arabian clubs continue to make waves in global football by attracting top talent and management. While Liverpool has long prided itself on its methodical approach to player acquisitions and squad management, Hughes’ departure may prompt changes in how the club navigates transfers moving forward.

Al-Hilal's Ambitious Recruitment Drive Continues

The Saudi Pro League, under the auspices of heavy state funding, has made an aggressive push to raise its profile in recent years. Al-Hilal, one of Saudi Arabia's most decorated football clubs, has been at the forefront of these efforts. They have attracted major talents like Neymar and Kalidou Koulibaly, transforming their domestic league into a hotspot for world-class footballing figures. Adding someone of Hughes’ pedigree to their ranks reinforces the ambition of not just Al-Hilal but the Saudi league as a whole, as they aim to compete with Europe's traditional footballing strongholds.

Analysis

Richard Hughes' decision to leave Liverpool for Al-Hilal carries significant implications. His departure underscores the increasing allure of Saudi clubs not just for players but also for key figures in football management. Analysts may view this as a pivotal moment in the sport, as the Gulf region positions itself as a serious competitor to Europe. On Liverpool's side, the search for a new sporting director will be crucial in ensuring continuity. Hughes played a major role in the club's intelligent transfer dealings, a factor credited with contributing to their domestic and international successes during this period of Jurgen Klopp’s management. Finding the right successor will likely be a priority as Liverpool aims to remain title contenders domestically and in Europe.

Richard Hughes’ move from Liverpool to Al-Hilal signifies a continued shift in football’s power dynamics, with Saudi clubs increasing their influence. For Liverpool, the departure of such a crucial figure presents both challenges and opportunities as the club enters a new chapter.

What does 'open talks' mean in transfer reporting?

In transfer-speak, 'open talks' generally means that formal or informal negotiations have begun between interested parties — typically clubs, and sometimes involving agents or the player. It indicates an early stage of dialogue and does not guarantee a transfer will be completed.

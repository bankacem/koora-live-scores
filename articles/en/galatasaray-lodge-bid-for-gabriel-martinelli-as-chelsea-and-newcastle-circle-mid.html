Galatasaray Lodge Bid for Gabriel Martinelli as Chelsea and Newcastle Circle Midfield and Defensive Targets

8/12/2026 · By Koora Live

Arsenal have reportedly received a formal offer from Turkish champions Galatasaray for forward Gabriel Martinelli, according to BBC Sport. The same report also links Spain midfielder Martin Zubimendi with a possible exit from the Gunners to Chelsea, while Newcastle United are said to be pressing their interest in Benfica defender Amar Dedic.

Galatasaray's approach for Gabriel Martinelli

BBC Sport states that Galatasaray have put forward a bid to sign Gabriel Martinelli from Arsenal. Martinelli is widely known as an attacking player for Arsenal, capable of operating across the front line, and has been a visible figure in the Premier League setup. Galatasaray, one of Turkey's most successful clubs with a large domestic and continental following, have in recent years looked to supplement their squad with high-profile signings to compete both domestically and in European competitions.

A bid being submitted signals a concrete interest rather than mere speculation. How Arsenal respond will depend on a number of factors, including squad planning, the manager's preferences, and the player's own stance — all typical considerations in transfer negotiations. Any developments would be followed closely by supporters and pundits given Martinelli's profile and the competitive dynamics of the clubs involved.

Links for Martin Zubimendi and Amar Dedic: Chelsea and Newcastle enter the frame

The BBC Sport bulletin also links Spain midfielder Martin Zubimendi with a move to Chelsea, saying he could leave the Gunners for Stamford Bridge. The item frames this as a potential exit rather than a completed transfer, indicating that talks or interest are at a preliminary stage. Chelsea, a London club with a long track record of recruiting midfield talent, often look to refresh their squad depending on tactical needs and squad balance.

Separately, the report names Amar Dedic of Benfica as a defensive target for Newcastle United, asserting the Magpies are pushing to sign the defender. Benfica are a Portuguese club known for developing and exporting young talent, and Newcastle have been active in the market in recent windows as they seek to strengthen various areas of their squad. These links suggest Premier League clubs continue to explore options across Europe to bolster depth and quality.

Analysis

Taken together, the items reported by BBC Sport paint a picture of active summer activity across several positions. Attacking players like Martinelli attract interest from abroad as well as domestically, while clubs still hunt for reinforcements in midfield and defence. If Arsenal were to entertain an offer for a high-profile attacker, it would raise questions around how they would reinvest or adjust their forward options. Similarly, links between Martin Zubimendi and Chelsea, and Amar Dedic and Newcastle, reflect common market patterns where Premier League sides target continental talent to plug perceived gaps.

This should be read as analysis rather than confirmation of transfers. Clubs, agents and players typically explore multiple possibilities during a window and many reported approaches do not culminate in deals. Fans and observers will want to monitor official club communications and reputable outlets like BBC Sport for any formal confirmations or denials.

The BBC Sport bulletin highlights a flurry of transfer activity involving Arsenal-linked players and targets for Chelsea and Newcastle. While bids and links signal serious interest, they are part of the fluid negotiation process in the transfer market; supporters should expect developments but await official confirmation from the clubs involved.

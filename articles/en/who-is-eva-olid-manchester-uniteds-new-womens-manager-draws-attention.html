Who Is Eva Olid? Manchester United's New Women's Manager Draws Attention

8/5/2026 · By Koora Live

Manchester United's Women's team has announced the appointment of Eva Olid as their new manager. Despite lacking prior experience in the Women's Super League (WSL), the former Hearts boss arrives on a two-year contract, with an option to extend for another 12 months, setting the stage for her leadership tenure.

Eva Olid Takes the Helm at Manchester United Women

Eva Olid has been officially named Manchester United Women's team manager, succeeding in one of the most ambitious managerial transitions in recent WSL history. While the appointment initially raises questions due to her lack of prior WSL experience, Olid’s reputation as a tactician and her managerial stint at Hearts highlight her potential to thrive at the elite level. She has signed a two-year deal with the option for a third year, signaling the club's confidence in her ability to oversee their developmental goals in both domestic competition and beyond. This is a significant move for Manchester United Women as they aim to cement their standing among the heavyweights of the WSL.

The Challenges of Transitioning to the WSL

The Women's Super League is widely regarded as one of the most competitive leagues in women's football, featuring powerhouse teams such as Chelsea, Arsenal, and Manchester City. Historically, it has served as a proving ground for managers and players alike, with demanding standards and fierce rivalries. For Eva Olid, transitioning from Hearts, where she managed in Scotland, to the high-intensity environment of the WSL will likely require not only adaptability but also a keen understanding of the league’s dynamics. With Manchester United Women recently gaining prominence as serious title contenders, Olid faces the dual challenge of maintaining their upward trajectory while building chemistry with a squad aiming for domestic and European success.

Analysis

The appointment of Eva Olid underscores Manchester United's belief in her long-term potential rather than immediate familiarity with the WSL. From an analytical perspective, this move might indicate a strategic shift—prioritizing managerial philosophy and team-building abilities over prior league experience. Her visionary style could prove refreshing for the club as they aim to not only compete but dominate in the WSL. However, the lack of WSL exposure also presents risks, as it may take time for her to fully adapt to the league's nuances. Analysts will likely monitor how quickly Olid can implement her methods within the pressures of managing a high-profile squad that has both expectations and aspirations for silverware.

Eva Olid’s appointment as Manchester United Women’s manager represents both an exciting new chapter and a calculated risk for the club. As she attempts to integrate her philosophies into one of the WSL’s rising teams, the football world will surely monitor whether this gamble pays off in terms of delivering consistent success at the highest level.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who is Eva Olid?

Eva Olid is the newly appointed manager of Manchester United’s Women’s team. She previously managed Hearts and is now set to manage in the highly competitive Women’s Super League.

What is the duration of Eva Olid’s contract with Manchester United Women?

Eva Olid has signed a two-year deal with Manchester United Women, with an additional option for one more year.

Does Eva Olid have prior WSL experience?

No, Eva Olid does not have prior experience in the Women's Super League. She joins Manchester United Women after managing Hearts in Scotland.

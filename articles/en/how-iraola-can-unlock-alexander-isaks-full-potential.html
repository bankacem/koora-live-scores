How Iraola Can Unlock Alexander Isak's Full Potential

8/29/2026 · By Koora Live

Alexander Isak recently opened his goal tally for the season during Bournemouth's 2-2 draw against Nottingham Forest. However, questions remain over how manager Andoni Iraola can help the forward rediscover his full potential and establish himself as a consistent threat in the Premier League.

Isak's impact in the Premier League

Alexander Isak, who joined Newcastle United in 2022 for a significant transfer fee, has shown glimpses of his exceptional talent during his time in the English top flight. Last season, he produced some stellar performances and demonstrated a knack for scoring important goals. However, consistency has been a challenge, and following a summer break, his performance this season has sparked discussions about how to get the best out of the Swedish international.

Isak is known for his silky skills, blistering pace, and ability to take on defenders one-on-one. His versatility allows him to operate both as a central striker and a wide forward, making him a valuable asset for any team. Despite this, as evident in the 2-2 draw with Nottingham Forest, there seems to be untapped potential in his game. With the dynamic attacking systems introduced by manager Andoni Iraola, supporters are eager to see consistency and end-product that matches Isak’s immense talent.

Iraola's style and its impact on forwards like Isak

Andoni Iraola's managerial philosophy places significant emphasis on high-intensity pressing and quick transitions. Transitioning from his time at Rayo Vallecano, Iraola has brought a distinct playing style that encourages attacking creativity while maintaining structural discipline. This system, while promising, poses a key question: how can Isak’s individual skills best be utilized within a collective framework?

At Rayo, Iraola showed a knack for maximizing the potential of his forwards through a vertical and incisive style of football. If he can replicate that approach at Bournemouth by supplying more goal-scoring opportunities to Isak — whether through quick counters or sharper interplay in the final third — it could help the Swedish striker thrive. However, striking a balance between freedom for individual brilliance and adherence to a structured tactical setup often takes time and adjustment for both manager and players.

Analysis

The fact that Alexander Isak has only just opened his account this season will likely prompt debates about tactical coherence under new manager Andoni Iraola. It’s possible that the Swede is still adapting to his managerial style, or that the balance of Bournemouth’s offensive strategy hasn’t yet fully unlocked his strengths. Some analysts might argue that Isak thrives best in systems that allow him greater individual freedom to exploit spaces and finish chances, rather than being confined to rigid tactical setups.

The 2-2 draw with Nottingham Forest, where Isak scored, could mark a turning point. If Iraola continues crafting systems that provide him with quality service and clear attacking roles, we might see the forward rediscover his most effective form in the weeks ahead. Strong partnerships with surrounding players, such as midfield creators or wingers with flair, could also play a crucial role in ensuring Isak maximizes his goal-scoring potential.

Alexander Isak's potential remains undeniable, but fully unlocking it under Andoni Iraola's system may require time, tactical adaptation, and improved chemistry with teammates. The 2-2 draw against Nottingham Forest could be a step in the right direction, providing a platform for Isak to grow into the prolific forward Bournemouth fans hope he can be.

Iliman Ndiaye Joins Manchester City in £65m Deal from Everton

9/1/2026 · By Koora Live

In a seismic move that highlights Manchester City's commitment to bolstering their attacking options, the Premier League champions have signed winger Iliman Ndiaye from Everton in a deal reportedly worth up to £65 million. This remarkable acquisition stands as a testament to Ndiaye's meteoric rise from non-league football to the pinnacle of European football. Here's a deeper look into this sensational transfer.

Manchester City's Statement of Intent

Signing Iliman Ndiaye for a reported fee of up to £65 million is a clear signal of Manchester City's ambition to maintain their dominance in both domestic and European competitions. Known for their relentless pursuit of excellence, the reigning Premier League champions are no strangers to making significant investments in players who fit their high-tempo, attacking philosophy.

Under manager Pep Guardiola, the club has cultivated a style of play that requires intelligence, versatility, and technical brilliance, traits Ndiaye is believed to possess in abundance. Analysis

The transfer of Iliman Ndiaye to Manchester City offers several talking points for analysts and fans alike. Firstly, this deal underscores the growing premium placed on versatile attacking players. Modern football places a high value on wingers capable of both scoring and creating chances, roles Ndiaye will likely be tasked with fulfilling for Pep Guardiola's side. Furthermore, the deal reinforces Manchester City's financial prowess in the transfer market as they continue to invest heavily to retain their dominance in a highly competitive Premier League.

For Everton, this transfer may represent a bittersweet moment. While they will benefit from the financial windfall of such a lucrative deal, losing a player of Ndiaye's potential and skill will likely pose challenges for a squad already looking to compete more robustly in the league. The future will reveal whether this deal sets a benchmark for other non-league players seeking to replicate Ndiaye’s astonishing ascent through the football pyramid.

Iliman Ndiaye's £65 million move to Manchester City marks a significant chapter in his inspiring journey from non-league football to the heights of the Premier League elite.

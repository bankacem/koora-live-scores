Feyenoord Fans Cause Injuries with Fireworks During Eredivisie Match

8/24/2026 · By Koora Live

Unfortunate scenes marred Sunday’s Eredivisie clash as fireworks thrown by Feyenoord fans caused injuries among attendees at SC Cambuur's home ground. Supporters suffered burns, hearing damage, and eye injuries, according to a statement released by the Dutch club.

Incidents During SC Cambuur vs Feyenoord Clash

The Eredivisie encounter between SC Cambuur and Feyenoord took a disturbing turn as fireworks launched by sections of the visiting Feyenoord supporters led to injuries among the crowd. SC Cambuur confirmed that some fans suffered burns and eye damage, while others experienced hearing-related injuries as a result of the actions. While the match itself is part of one of the Netherlands' top-flight competitions, the incidents off the pitch have taken the spotlight, creating an alarming narrative around fan behavior in Dutch football.

Eredivisie and Fan Misconduct Issues

The Eredivisie is well-known for its passionate fanbase, with club supporters often delivering elaborate displays and fervent chants that create an electric atmosphere. However, this incident highlights the darker side of fan culture when enthusiasm crosses the line into unsafe and reckless behavior. Fireworks and pyrotechnics have long been a problematic element in football stadiums worldwide, and the latest events at SC Cambuur's home ground are a stark reminder of the risks involved. For clubs and league organizers, ensuring the safety of supporters remains a top priority, and incidents like these will likely prompt further scrutiny of security measures and fan protocols during matches.

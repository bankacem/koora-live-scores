Aston Villa Pursue Club Brugge's Joel Ordonez to Bolster Defense

8/24/2026 · By Koora Live

Aston Villa have turned their attention to Club Brugge's Joel Ordonez as they seek to strengthen their defensive line. According to BBC Sport, the Midlands club is in negotiations for the Ecuadorian central defender after failing to finalize a deal with Southampton for Taylor Harwood-Bellis.

Villa’s Defensive Reinforcement Plans

Aston Villa’s attempts to improve their defensive options have taken an intriguing turn, with reports emerging that they are targeting Joel Ordonez of Club Brugge. This comes after discussions for Southampton's Taylor Harwood-Bellis reached an impasse. The 19-year-old Ordonez, a promising young talent, currently plies his trade in the Belgian Pro League—a competition often regarded as a fertile ground for nurturing future stars. Signing a young defender with his potential could align well with Villa's long-term strategy as they aim to remain competitive in both domestic and European competitions, including the UEFA Europa Conference League this season.

Ordonez: A Rising Star in Belgian Football

Joel Ordonez has been making a name for himself in Belgian football since joining Club Brugge, a club well-known for developing top-level players. The Ecuadorian center-back, despite his relatively young age, has shown composure and consistency in his performances, attributes that may have caught the attention of Aston Villa. Adding him to their squad could provide depth and youthful energy to a defensive unit that has been solid under the management of Unai Emery. Villa’s ambition under the Spanish head coach has been clear, with the team pushing for both domestic and international success this season.

Analysis

Villa's pursuit of Joel Ordonez suggests a strategic approach to integrate young, high-potential players into their roster. The club appears keen to strike a balance between proven Premier League experience and developing talent, ensuring sustainable performance over the long term. While Harwood-Bellis, a defender with high promise and English football experience, seemed like a logical target, negotiations appear to have hit a roadblock, prompting Villa to explore alternative options. Ordonez’s profile, as a technically-gifted and promising center-back, makes him an intriguing candidate for Villa’s ambitions, particularly as they juggle the demands of domestic and European campaigns.

As Aston Villa continue their efforts to strengthen their backline, their pursuit of Joel Ordonez underlines a commitment to investing in young talent with high potential. If successful, this move could prove to be a significant addition to their ambitions under Unai Emery.

Leicester City Readies for League One After a Decade as Premier League Champions

8/7/2026 · By Koora Live

Leicester City, currently preparing to compete in League One, look back at a time when they were defending the Premier League title just ten years ago. The memories of that stunning triumph still linger in the hearts of the Foxes fans and the club's management. As the team gears up for a new chapter in England’s third tier, the echoes of those "pinch‑me" moments remind everyone of how far the club has come and how quickly fortunes can change in football.

From Premier League Champions to League One: Leicester's Extraordinary Rise and Fall

Leicester City’s dramatic ascent from the lower leagues to the pinnacle of English football earned them the Premier League title in the 2015‑16 season—a moment still regarded as one of the most improbable in the sport’s history. Ten years on, the club is entering a season of League One, the third tier of English football. This swift transition underscores how volatile the football pyramid can be and how a single season of success can be followed by the need for regeneration. Leicester’s experience demonstrates that while glory is sweet, sustainability requires planning, depth, and adaptability.

The club’s journey has also highlighted how football infrastructure and governance operate at all levels. The King Power Stadium, the Foxes’ home ground, continues to host their matches while the club’s administrative staff works to rebuild and strengthen squad depth to compete effectively in the League One schedule.

Despite their current league placement, the 2015‑16 championship remains a benchmark for the club’s ambition, setting a high standard for performance across any division.

Rebuilding for Redemption: The Foxes’ Strategy for League One

Leicester’s preseason fixtures and training regimen now focus on developing a cohesive unit capable of challenging for promotion. The squad has been bolstered with a mix of experienced signs and promising academy talent, aiming to balance immediate competitiveness with long‑term growth.

Coaching staff emphasizes resilience, fitness, and tactical versatility—qualities that proved decisive in their title‑winning campaign. By maintaining core long‑term strategies while refining player roles for the demands of League One, the club hopes to avoid the pitfalls that led to their recent relegation.

Fans and management alike understand that a return higher up the league ladder will be earned through consistent performances, community engagement, and a respect for the club’s heritage. These foundational elements are at the core of Leicester’s plan as they look to resurrect the proud history of recent champions.

Analysis

The shift from Premier League champions to League One participants serves as a reminder that football clubs often follow a non‑linear trajectory. While the Foxes’ 2015‑16 triumph is a high point, the subsequent relegation illustrates that titles don’t guarantee permanence. Managers and directors now face the dual challenge of honouring that legacy while pragmatically restructuring a squad that can navigate the rigours of lower‑tier football. The club’s focus on blending proven experience with youthful potential reflects a broader industry trend of leveraging data‑driven insights to rebuild clubs after relegation. If Leicester can harness its historic winning mentality and combine it with disciplined squad building, the prospects for a swift climb back into the Championship or Premier League become increasingly realistic.

Leicester City’s path from Champions League‑bearing champions to League One hopefuls paints a vivid picture of football’s unpredictable cycles. While the memories of ten‑year‑old triumph remain etched in the club’s narrative, the current focus is on rebuilding a team that can reclaim its former glory. The journey ahead will test the Foxes’ resolve, but their storied past may well fuel the drive towards resurgence.

Frequently Asked Questions

When did Leicester City win the Premier League?

Leicester City secured the Premier League title during the 2015‑16 season, achieving one of football’s most remarkable storylines.

Why are they currently playing in League One?

The club was relegated after finishing in the bottom three of the Premier League in the 2023‑24 season, which places them in England’s third tier, League One.

What is the club’s approach to regaining higher league status?

Leicester’s strategy involves strengthening squad depth with experienced and young players, prioritising fitness and tactical flexibility, and building on the club’s established culture of resilience to push for promotion.

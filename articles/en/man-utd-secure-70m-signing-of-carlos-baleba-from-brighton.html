Man Utd Secure £70m Signing of Carlos Baleba from Brighton

8/25/2026 · By Koora Live

Manchester United have finalized the high-profile signing of Cameroon international Carlos Baleba from Brighton & Hove Albion in a deal worth £70 million. The 19-year-old midfielder’s move marks an ambitious stride in bolstering United's squad and strengthening their midfield options.

A Major Move for Manchester United

The addition of Carlos Baleba represents a major coup for Manchester United, who have been looking to reinforce their midfield as they strive for success both domestically and in European competitions. Baleba's move from Brighton underscores his growing reputation as one of the brightest young talents in football. The £70 million price tag reflects both his current abilities and the immense potential he brings to Old Trafford.

Carlos Baleba's Rapid Rise

At just 19 years old, Baleba's rise to prominence has been meteoric. The Cameroonian midfielder joined Brighton & Hove Albion as a promising talent and quickly caught the eye with his all-action performances, technical proficiency, and dynamic presence in the center of the park. His success at Brighton, noted for their ability to nurture young talents like Moisés Caicedo and Alexis Mac Allister, has made him a highly coveted player, leading to Manchester United's substantial investment.

Analysis

Carlos Baleba’s transfer can be seen as part of Manchester United's ongoing strategy to inject youthful energy into their squad while also addressing key weaknesses. Recent seasons have exposed United’s need for greater depth and versatility in midfield, particularly against high-intensity opponents. Fans will hope that Baleba’s development continues at Old Trafford, enabling him to become a pivotal figure for the club. On Brighton’s side, this transfer follows their established model of nurturing young talents and selling them for significant profits, highlighting their impressive scouting and player development network.

The arrival of Carlos Baleba is an exciting development for Manchester United as they continue to strengthen their squad for future challenges. While the midfielder has immense potential, the coming seasons will show whether he can fully realize it at one of football's most demanding stages.

Arsenal transfer news: Gunners invite offers for Martinelli and Nwaneri amid talks with Galatasaray

8/13/2026 · By Koora Live

BBC Sport reports that Arsenal have invited offers for Gabriel Martinelli and Ethan Nwaneri while holding talks with Turkish side Galatasaray over the prospect of selling various squad members. The coverage suggests the Gunners are exploring options to reshape parts of their squad as the transfer market evolves.

What BBC Sport has reported

According to a BBC Sport report, Arsenal are actively inviting offers for two named first‑team players, Gabriel Martinelli and Ethan Nwaneri. The same report also states the club have held talks with Galatasaray concerning the potential sale of a number of squad members. Those are the core facts published by BBC Sport; they describe exploratory activity rather than completed transactions.

Inviting offers is a common step for clubs that want to assess market interest or create leverage in potential negotiations. The report does not detail any accepted bids, fees, or final decisions — it frames the situation as preliminary discussions and a formal invitation to make offers.

Player profiles and wider context

Gabriel Martinelli is widely recognised as a forward who has been part of Arsenal's first‑team setup. He is known for his direct play, work rate, and attacking contributions. Ethan Nwaneri is a young academy product who has attracted attention as a promising talent coming through Arsenal's youth ranks. Both players are associated with the club’s recent squads and development pathway.

Arsenal is a major English club based in north London that regularly operates in the upper tiers of domestic and European competition. Galatasaray are one of the leading clubs in Turkey, based in Istanbul. Transfers and player exchanges between clubs in different countries are a standard part of the football calendar, and media outlets such as BBC Sport commonly report on clubs inviting offers or holding talks as part of routine transfer activity.

Analysis

This development, as reported by BBC Sport, could be interpreted in several ways. On one level, inviting offers allows Arsenal to test the market value of certain players and gauge interest without committing to a sale. For younger players or those with fluctuating playing time, it can also be a mechanism to open pathways for regular minutes elsewhere. Given the mention of talks with Galatasaray, Arsenal could be exploring both domestic and international options.

Any practical outcome will depend on multiple factors beyond the initial media report: the club's strategic plans, player preferences, squad balance and the offers received. Observers might see this as a pragmatic approach to squad management rather than an immediate sign that departures are imminent. Ultimately, until clubs confirm agreements, reports of invitations and talks remain part of the transfer market’s early noise rather than established transfers.

BBC Sport’s coverage indicates that Arsenal are actively testing the market for certain players and have engaged in talks with Galatasaray as part of broader transfer activity. Until clubs issue official confirmations, these reports should be seen as early steps in what can be a lengthy decision‑making process.

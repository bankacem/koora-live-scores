Liverpool's £50m bid for Brighton winger Yankuba Minteh rejected

8/20/2026 · By Koora Live

Liverpool have turned up the heat on Brighton & Hove Albion’s rising star Yankuba Minteh with a £50 million swoop, but the English Premier League club has turned the ball over, keeping the West London winger in their ranks. BBC Sport reports that Liverpool’s offer was officially declined, adding another twist to the summer’s transfer frenzy. While the Reds are geared up to refresh their attacking options, Brighton’s management appears eager to slot Minteh into their evolving squad.

Liverpool eyes bright potential with a hefty £50m offer

Liverpool, long accustomed to a high‑profile transfer policy, has consistently targeted young, dynamic players capable of filling gaps in their squad. Their interest in Minteh, who has impressed across the league, fits that pattern. The coronavirus‑shaped football economy has left clubs exploring different avenues to shore up their attacking lines, and the Ajax‑product is seen to be an exciting prospect. The £50 million figure is considerable and reflects Liverpool’s ambition to sign advanced players who can adapt to the fast, high‑pressing style the club has long used. Such a bid would have matched the club’s willingness to invest heavily in its future, following recent high‑value signings.

Brighton retains their key winger amid transfer speculation

Brighton & Hove Albion’s decision underscores their emerging defensive solidity and focus on youth development. With the club's fascia navigating a tight budget, joining a Premier League club that 'says yes' at that price has major financial implications. What this suggests is that Brighton’s management values Minteh’s current contribution. As the Seagulls work toward defensive stability, retaining a player who can switch play from the left flank to support forwards fits seamlessly with their tactical strategy.

What this means for the Reds and the Seagulls

For Liverpool, the rejection will likely set them looking for other options within or outside the Premier League. The club’s reputation for high‑investment transfers remains clear, but this incident may push them to view alternative candidate pool or compensate by raising their offers. The repayment of a big bid also highlights an authorial sense of a certain safe and cautious budget allocation.

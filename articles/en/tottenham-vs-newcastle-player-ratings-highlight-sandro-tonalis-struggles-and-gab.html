Tottenham vs Newcastle: Player Ratings Highlight Sandro Tonali's Struggles and Gabriel Gonzalez's Impact in Premier League Clash

8/29/2026 · By Koora Live

In a highly anticipated Premier League clash on Saturday, Newcastle United claimed a 2-0 victory over Tottenham Hotspur. While the visitors walked away with the crucial three points, the match also offered contrasting performances from key players, with Sandro Tonali struggling to make an impact while Gabriel Gonzalez delivered an impressive display. Here's how the players fared, according to BBC Sport's player ratings.

Newcastle Shine with Gonzalez's Standout Performance

Newcastle United's strong away performance was anchored by a series of solid individual displays, none more so than that of Gabriel Gonzalez. The young player's contributions caught the eye, with energy, creativity, and defensive commitment. The Magpies' ability to shine in such high-pressure games has been a hallmark of their recent resurgence under Eddie Howe, and Gonzalez's performance was symbolic of the team's growing strength and depth.

Sandro Tonali Struggles in Spurs Midfield

On the other hand, Tottenham midfielder Sandro Tonali endured a tough outing. The Italian failed to influence the game in the manner expected of him, as Newcastle's midfield superiority largely stifled Tottenham's creative efforts. Tonali joined Spurs amidst high expectations, given his pedigree as a highly talented playmaker at both Brescia and AC Milan before his move to England. However, he faces mounting pressure to adapt quickly to the pace and demands of Premier League football, particularly in high-stakes matches like this one.

Analysis

This result further underscores Newcastle's ability to challenge the Premier League's established elite, particularly in away fixtures. The Magpies, who have been on an upward trajectory since their recent ownership change and the arrival of Eddie Howe in 2021, once again showcased their effective mix of youthful talent and experienced campaigners. Gabriel Gonzalez's standout performance is a positive sign for Newcastle's depth, hinting at a bright future for the club.

Meanwhile, Sandro Tonali's struggles are unlikely to go unnoticed by Tottenham fans and coaching staff. Historically one of Serie A's emerging stars and a title-winner with AC Milan, Tonali might need more time to adapt to a new league and team system under the management of Ange Postecoglou. The pressure on players moving to the Premier League is substantial, and whether Tonali eventually finds his feet in North London will be a key storyline to watch.

Newcastle's 2-0 win over Tottenham serves as a statement of intent as they continue to challenge the Premier League's top teams. While Gonzalez's performance offers promise for Newcastle's ambitions, Tottenham will need to work on getting the best out of players like Tonali to keep pace with their rivals.

Context: pre-season friendlies and why kit issues matter

Pre‑season friendlies are warm‑up matches used by clubs to build fitness, test formations and give minutes to squad members ahead of the competitive campaign. These fixtures often feature experimentation — with tactics, personnel and sometimes even kit combinations — because the results do not carry the same formal stakes as league or cup games.

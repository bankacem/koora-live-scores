Context: Ferguson's exit and Moyes's appointment

Sir Alex Ferguson's decision to step down marked the end of a prolonged and trophy‑filled era for Manchester United. His retirement in 2013 prompted a high‑stakes search for his successor and generated intense public and media interest in who would inherit the role at Old Trafford.

David Moyes was appointed to succeed Ferguson in 2013. Moyes, who had built a reputation managing at a stable Premier League club prior to the United appointment, became the man entrusted with navigating the club through a period of transition after Ferguson's long reign. The contrast between Ferguson's era and the subsequent years has been a frequent subject of analysis in football circles.

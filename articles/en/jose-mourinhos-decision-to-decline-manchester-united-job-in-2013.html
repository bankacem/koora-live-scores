Jose Mourinho's Decision to Decline Manchester United Job in 2013

8/10/2026 · By Koora Live

Jose Mourinho's decision to turn down Manchester United's managerial role following Sir Alex Ferguson's retirement remains a defining moment in football history. A recent report by BBC Sport delves into the behind-the-scenes drama of United’s attempt to appoint the 'Special One,' revealing why he ultimately declined and the ripple effects on the club's trajectory.

The End of an Era at Manchester United

When Sir Alex Ferguson retired in 2013, Manchester United faced the daunting challenge of finding a suitable successor for one of history's most successful managers. Ferguson, appointed in 1986, brought an unprecedented level of consistency and success to the Premier League club, winning 13 league titles, five FA Cups, and two UEFA Champions League titles during his tenure. His departure marked the end of an extraordinary era at Old Trafford, leaving fans and football pundits speculating on who could possibly fill his shoes.

Jose Mourinho: A Prime Candidate

At the time of Ferguson's retirement, Jose Mourinho was regarded as one of the best managers in the world, boasting an illustrious record that included multiple league titles in Portugal, England, Italy, and Spain as well as two UEFA Champions League trophies. Known for his charisma, tactical acumen, and ability to win silverware quickly, Mourinho seemed like the ideal candidate to lead Manchester United into its post-Ferguson phase. According to BBC Sport's report, United made serious attempts to secure Mourinho as their next manager, but the Portuguese coach ultimately declined the offer after initially showing interest.

Analysis

Mourinho's decision to decline the Manchester United role raises fascinating questions about the club and the manager himself. Analysts might interpret this as a moment of hesitation from Mourinho, who may have had reservations about following Ferguson's unparalleled legacy. Taking over a club undergoing a seismic transition is often fraught with risks, and even for a manager of Mourinho's caliber, inheriting Ferguson's empire presented monumental expectations. This decision not only left United in search of an alternative, eventually appointing David Moyes, but also positioned Mourinho to return to Chelsea, where he achieved immediate success, further solidifying his reputation as a top-tier coach.

The decision by Jose Mourinho to decline Manchester United's managerial position in 2013 had a deep impact on the club's immediate future and the broader football landscape. It shifted the paths of both Mourinho and United and remains a key moment in the history of the Premier League.

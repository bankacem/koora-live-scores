Arsenal's Gabriel Martinelli Joins Al-Hilal in £60m Transfer

9/4/2026 · By Koora Live

In a significant development during this transfer window, Gabriel Martinelli has departed Premier League side Arsenal to join Saudi Pro League team Al-Hilal. The deal is reported to be worth £60 million, marking another high-profile addition to the increasingly ambitious league.

Gabriel Martinelli's Journey from Arsenal to Al-Hilal

Gabriel Martinelli's move to Al-Hilal represents a major milestone in his young career. The Brazilian winger joined Arsenal in 2019 and quickly became a fan favorite at the Emirates due to his energy, technical ability, and knack for scoring crucial goals. Over his tenure with the Gunners, Martinelli earned a reputation as one of the Premier League's most exciting young talents. Now, cashing in on Al-Hilal's growing financial influence and global pull, the 22-year-old winger is set to continue his career in the Middle East.

Al-Hilal's Ambition in the Saudi Pro League

Al-Hilal’s acquisition of Martinelli signals their ambitious intent to dominate the Saudi Pro League and raise their profile on the global stage. Founded in 1957 and boasting an impressive record of domestic and international success, Al-Hilal is one of Saudi Arabia's most successful and well-supported teams. In recent years, the club has aggressively invested in high-profile players to enhance the quality of their squad as part of a larger project aimed at elevating the league’s competitiveness. Martinelli's arrival, though costly at £60m, demonstrates confidence in his ability to make an immediate impact both on and off the pitch.

Analysis

This transfer underscores the growing influence of the Saudi Pro League on international football. The league has been making waves by signing top-level players from established European teams, and Martinelli’s move follows a trend of prominent footballing talent choosing Saudi Arabia as their next destination. For Arsenal, the £60m fee could be used to reinvest in their squad as they aim to remain competitive domestically and in European competitions. However, losing a promising talent like Martinelli also highlights the challenges clubs face in retaining young stars amidst lucrative offers from other leagues.

Gabriel Martinelli's transfer from Arsenal to Al-Hilal is a landmark move for both the player and the Saudi Pro League. As Martinelli ventures into a new chapter of his career, all eyes will be on how he adapts to the challenges of playing in Saudi Arabia and how Arsenal reinvests the substantial transfer fee.

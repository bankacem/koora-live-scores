Israel fans to attend Republic of Ireland Nations League fixture despite no official away allocation

8/4/2026 · By Koora Live

BBC Sport has reported that supporters of Israel will be present when their national side meets the Republic of Ireland in a UEFA Nations League fixture next month. The coverage makes clear that, while Israel supporters are expected to attend, there will be no facilitated away-ticket allocation for travelling fans.

What BBC Sport reported about the fixture

According to BBC Sport, Israel supporters will be in attendance for the Nations League match involving the Republic of Ireland next month. The report also states that organisers will not be facilitating away fans for this fixture. Those are the only specifics supplied about this particular match in the report, and no further operational details, dates or numbers were published in that piece.

Context: the Nations League and typical match arrangements

The UEFA Nations League is a competition for European national teams established to provide competitive fixtures and replace many international friendlies. It operates with a group phase and involves promotion and relegation between leagues in most editions. Home-and-away fixtures are commonly used in the group stage, and host associations normally handle ticketing and allocations for home and away supporters.

When clubs or national associations announce that there will be no away fans facilitated, this usually means there will not be an official travelling-supporters allocation managed through the home association. That can result from a range of practical considerations — including logistical constraints, safety assessments, or decisions by local authorities — but the BBC Sport piece does not offer the reasons behind the arrangement for this specific match.

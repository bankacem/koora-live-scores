Hull City Assistant Dean Holden Reflects on Momentous Win Against Manchester United

8/23/2026 · By Koora Live

Hull City's assistant head coach, Dean Holden, recently joined the Match of the Day (MOTD) panel on BBC Sport to discuss his side's extraordinary victory over Manchester United. This triumph, described as a 'dream' result for the Tigers, marked a significant moment for the club as they impressed in their opening home game of the season.

A Dream Start for Hull City

After securing a sensational home win over one of English football's most illustrious clubs, Manchester United, Hull City began their campaign in remarkable fashion. Assistant head coach Dean Holden appeared on BBC Sport's Match of the Day to reflect on what he referred to as an outcome 'beyond anyone's dreams.' The victory has brought immense joy to fans at the MKM Stadium and reinforced the team's ambitions for the season.

Dean Holden's Post-Match Reflections

Joining Gabby Logan, former England midfielder Danny Murphy, and ex-Manchester City goalkeeper Joe Hart in the MOTD studio, Holden offered insights into Hull City's performance. He praised the team's effort and resilience against the Red Devils, a side known for its extensive squad depth and global prominence. This analysis provided viewers with a glimpse of the tactical approach and mindset that guided the Tigers to such an impressive result.

Analysis

This unexpected result demonstrates the unpredictable nature of football, particularly in competitions where so-called underdogs can spring surprises against storied clubs. For Hull City, overcoming Manchester United not only boosts team morale but also sets a positive tone for the rest of the campaign, particularly at home. While it's too early to draw long-term conclusions, this performance highlights Hull's ability to compete at a high level and could serve as a warning to other teams who might underestimate them. For Manchester United, such a loss could underscore lingering inconsistencies that require attention as they progress through the season.

Hull City's emphatic win over Manchester United has not only delivered an unforgettable moment for their fans but has also showcased the competitive spirit within the team. As Dean Holden's remarks illustrate, the Tigers are determined to make their mark this season, and this result could be just the beginning of more memorable moments ahead.

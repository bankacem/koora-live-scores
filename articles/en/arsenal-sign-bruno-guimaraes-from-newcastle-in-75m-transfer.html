Arsenal sign Bruno Guimaraes from Newcastle in £75m transfer

8/8/2026 · By Koora Live

BBC Sport reports that Premier League champions Arsenal have completed the signing of Brazil midfielder Bruno Guimaraes from Newcastle United for a fee of £75m. The move will be one of the headline transfers involving top-flight English clubs, with Arsenal adding an established international midfielder to their squad.

The transfer represents a major piece of business for both clubs: Arsenal strengthening a squad that recently won the league, and Newcastle parting company with a high-profile player. BBC Sport is the source of this report, and the basic details are the club-to-club movement, the player involved and the fee.

Bringing in Bruno Guimaraes gives Arsenal another midfield option after their successful title campaign. As a Brazilian international, Guimaraes is widely regarded in general football circles as a midfielder capable of contributing both defensively and in possession. For a champion side, adding depth and variety in midfield can help manage the demands of domestic and potential continental competition over a long season.

Arsenal have a history as one of England's most prominent clubs, based in London and playing home matches at the Emirates Stadium.

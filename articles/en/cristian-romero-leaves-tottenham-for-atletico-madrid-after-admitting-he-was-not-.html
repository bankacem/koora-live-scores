Cristian Romero leaves Tottenham for Atletico Madrid after admitting he 'was not flawless' – transfer update

8/14/2026 · By Koora Live

BBC Sport reports that Cristian Romero has acknowledged he was not without faults during his five-year spell at Tottenham Hotspur as he prepares to finalise a move to Atlético Madrid. The defender’s candid reflection comes as his time in north London draws to a close and a new chapter in Spain awaits.

Romero's honest assessment of his Tottenham years

According to BBC Sport, Romero accepted that he was not perfect over the course of his five-year stay with Tottenham Hotspur. That self-appraisal is notable coming from a player who has been a prominent figure in the club’s defensive unit. While the precise context of his comments is provided in the BBC report, the broader theme is one of personal reflection as he prepares to leave the club.

Open admissions like this are not uncommon when players move on after multi-year spells. They can indicate a desire to take responsibility, to acknowledge learning curves, or simply to mark the end of a chapter with honesty. For supporters and observers, such statements often prompt discussions about a player’s legacy and the highs and lows of their tenure.

What the move to Atlético Madrid could represent

The same BBC Sport update notes that Romero is preparing to complete a transfer to Atlético Madrid. Atlético are one of Spain’s leading clubs, known for a disciplined, defence-first approach under long-term leadership that has prioritised organisation and physicality. For a defender, that environment is commonly seen as a place where defensive attributes are valued and tested.

A change of league and environment typically brings fresh challenges — from adapting to LaLiga’s tactical patterns to integrating with a new coaching staff and teammates. For Romero, this move represents a fresh start away from the Premier League and an opportunity to re-establish himself within a different footballing culture.

Analysis

Romero's admission of imperfection can be read as a mixture of personal candour and an acknowledgement of the natural ups and downs that accompany a lengthy spell at a high-profile club. Whether the move revitalises Romero’s career will depend on factors such as tactical fit, injuries, and adaptation to a new league — all typical considerations when a defender switches countries.

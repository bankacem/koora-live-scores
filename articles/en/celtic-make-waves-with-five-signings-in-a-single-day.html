Celtic Make Waves with Five Signings in a Single Day

9/3/2026 · By Koora Live

Scottish champions Celtic have made a significant statement in the transfer market by securing five signings in just one day. Among the new arrivals are former England goalkeeper Sam Johnstone and Chelsea's teenage prospects Shumaira Mheuka and Landon Emenalo, alongside prior acquisitions Van den Berg and Lotomba, according to BBC Sport.

An Ambitious Day of Transfer Activity at Celtic

Celtic have wasted no time in bolstering their squad, with BBC Sport confirming five new faces joining the club in a flurry of transfer activity. These include experienced shot-stopper Sam Johnstone, who brings Premier League pedigree to the side, and young talents Shumaira Mheuka and Landon Emenalo from Chelsea’s ranks. Earlier announcements revealed the arrivals of defender Van den Berg and Lotomba, emphasizing the club’s intent to strengthen across multiple positions.

Sam Johnstone Adds Experience, Chelsea Duo Signal Future Focus

The recruitment of Sam Johnstone marks a calculated move by Celtic to add depth and reliability in goal. A former England international, Johnstone has spent much of his career in England's top two league tiers and is expected to provide both experience and competition for first-choice goalkeeping duties. Meanwhile, the loan signings of Chelsea youngsters Shumaira Mheuka and Landon Emenalo underline Celtic's long-term planning. Both teenagers are highly rated at Stamford Bridge and will look to gain valuable first-team experience in Glasgow.

Analysis

Celtic’s proactive approach during this transfer window signals both short-term ambitions and long-term planning. The addition of Sam Johnstone brings stability and maturity to the squad, while the focus on securing young players from Chelsea highlights a commitment to developing talent. This approach could provide a solid platform for Celtic as they compete domestically and in European competitions. The timing of these deals, just ahead of crucial fixtures, suggests a purposeful bid to strengthen the team across key positions.

Through a busy day in the transfer market, Celtic have fortified their squad with a mix of experience and youthful promise. This blend of short-term reinforcement and investment in the future bodes well for the club as they pursue success in multiple competitions. All eyes will now be on how quickly these new additions integrate and contribute to Celtic’s ambitions moving forward.
